Front Seat Cushion Cover Replacement
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • LHD model is shown; RHD model is similar.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove the screw, and release the hook (A), the tabs (B), and the pin (C), then remove the front cover (D).
  1. Remove the recline cover (A).
    -1.
    Remove the screw, and detach the clip.
    -2.
    Detach the tabs (B).
    -3.
    Pull up the cover, then release the hook (C) and the tab (D).
    -4.
    Remove the upper inner cover (E).
    -5.
    Disconnect the power seat adjustment switch connector (F) and the lumber support switch connector (G).
    -6.
    Remove the wire harness (H) from the hooks (I).
  1. Remove the center cover (A).
    -1.
    Remove the screw.
    -2.
    Detach the tabs (B).
    -3.
    Pull up the cover, then release the tab (C).
  1. From under the seat cushion, release the hook strap (A) from the seat cushion frame springs (B), then pull the undercover (C) of the back cover back.
  1. Disconnect the seat cushion heater connector (A) and detach the harness clip (B).
  1. Release the Velcro fasteners (A), then pull back the harness holder covers (B) fastening the side airbag harness (C) and the seat wire harness (D).
  1. Detach the cloth (A) of the seat cushion cover from the connecting rod (B), then release the clip and hook strips (C) from both sides of the seat cushion frame.
  1. Release the hook strips (A) from the under the seat cushion.
  1. Remove the seat cushion cover/pad (A) from the seat frame (B).
  1. Release the hooks (A) from under the seat cushion (B).
  1. Pull back the edge of the seat cushion cover all the way around, and release the clips (A), then remove the seat cushion cover (B).
  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • To prevent wrinkles when installing a seat-back cover, make sure the material is stretched evenly over the pad before securing the clips, the hooks, and the hook strips.
    • Replace any clips (A) you removed with new ones. Install them with commercially available upholstery ring pliers (B).
