DTC Troubleshooting: B1305
DTC B1305:
 Rain Sensor Lost Communication with Relay Module (R/M Message)
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Wait for 6 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1305 indicated with DTC B1005 and B1158?
YES -
Faulty relay control module. Substitute a known-good under-hood fuse relay box.■
NO -
Go to Step 5 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between rain sensor 5P connector No. 3 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Reolace the rain sensor .■
NO -
An open in the wire or poor ground (G501).■
