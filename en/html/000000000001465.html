Air Cleaner Element Inspection/Replacement
  1. Remove the lid (A).
  1. Open the air cleaner housing cover (B) with air cleaner element (C).
  1. Remove the air cleaner element from the air cleaner housing cover.
  1. Check the air cleaner element for damage or clogging. If there is damage or clogging, replace the air cleaner element.
    NOTE: Do not use compressed air to clean the air cleaner element (except KY, KK models).
  1. Clean and remove any debris from inside the air cleaner housing.
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
