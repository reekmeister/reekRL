Hands Free Telephone System Symptom Troubleshooting - The HFT messages cannot be heard or are weak
The HFT message cannot be heard or are weak
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Check that the audio system is operating normally and the speaker sound levels from different audio sources (AM, FM, DISC/AUX, etc.).
Does the audio system work normally and is the audio output from the speaker normal when playing various audio sources?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Refer to the audio system symptom troubleshooting.■
  1. Check for DTCs.
Are there any DTCs?
YES -
Repair the indicated DTCs.
NO -
Go tot Step 4 .
  1. Check the audio system when HFT messages are played.
Dose the audio system mute when HFT messages are being played?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
