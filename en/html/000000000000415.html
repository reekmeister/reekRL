Rear Door Glass Outer Weatherstrip Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • The door glass outer weatherstrip will need replacement because it will bend during removal.
  • Take care not to scratch the door.
  1. Lower the glass fully.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Door panel
    • Door glass inner weatherstrip
    • Door sash outer trim
  1. Starting at the front, detach the front clip (A), and pull the door glass outer weatherstrip (B) up at each portion where the inner molding (C) catches the inside edge of the window slot, and then detach the rear clip (D), then remove the weatherstrip.
  1. Install a new weatherstrip in the reverse order of removal, and push the weatherstrip into place securely.
