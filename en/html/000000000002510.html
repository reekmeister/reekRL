VTEC Rocker Arm Test
Special Tools Required

Air supply, 3/8
 07LAJ-PR30102

VTEC Air Stop Tool A
 07AAJ-R70A100

VTEC Air Stop Tool B
 07AAJ-R70A200

Air Adapter, M10 x 1.0
 070AJ-0010101
  1. Start the engine and let it run for 5 minutes, then turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the six spark plugs.
  1. Remove the cylinder head covers.
  1. Rotate the crankshaft pulley clockwise, and visually check all intake primary rocker arms (A) and intake secondary rocker arms (B) move independently.
    • If the intake primary rocker arm and the intake secondary rocker arm move together, remove the intake primary rocker arm and the intake secondary rocker arm as an assembly, and check that the pistons in the rocker arms move smoothly. If any intake rocker arm needs replacing, replace the primary and secondary rocker arms as an assembly, then retest.
    • If the intake primary rocker arm and the intake secondary rocker arm move independently, go to Step 5 .
  1. Rotate the crankshaft pulley clockwise, and visually check all exhaust primary rocker arms (A) and exhaust secondary rocker arms (B) move independently.
    • If the exhaust primary rocker arm and the exhaust primary rocker arm move together, remove the exhaust primary rocker arm and the exhaust secondary rocker arm as an assembly, and check that the pistons in the rocker arms move smoothly. If any exhaust rocker arm needs replacing, replace the primary and secondary rocker arms as an assembly, then retest.
    • If the exhaust primary rocker arm and the exhaust secondary rocker arm move independently, go to Step 6 .
  1. Inspect the valve clearance.
  1. Check that the air pressure on the shop air compressor gauge indicates over 981 kPa (10.0 kgf/cm 2 , 142 psi).
  1. Remove the sealing bolts from the front cylinder head, then install the air adapter (A) to the inspection hole and install the VTEC air stop tool B, then connect the air supply (C).
  1. Remove the sealing bolts from the rear cylinder head, then install the VTEC air stop tool A to the inspection hole and install the air adapter (B), then connect the air supply (C).
  1. Loosen the valve on the regulator, and apply the specified air pressure.
    Specified Air Pressure:
    550−690 kPa (5.6−7.0 kgf/cm 2 , 80−100 psi)

  1. With the specified air pressure applied, rotate the crankshaft pulley clockwise, and visually check all intake primary rocker arms (A) and intake secondary rocker arms (B) move together.
    • If the intake primary rocker arm and the intake secondary rocker arm move independently, remove the intake primary rocker arm and the intake secondary rocker arm as an assembly, and check that the pistons in the rocker arms move smoothly. If any intake rocker arm needs replacing, replace the primary and secondary rocker arms as an assembly, then retest.
    • If the intake primary rocker arm and the intake secondary rocker arm move together, go to Step 12 .
  1. With the specified air pressure applied, rotate the crankshaft pulley clockwise, and visually check all exhaust rocker primary arms (A) and exhaust secondary rocker arms (B) move together.
    • If the exhaust primary rocker arm and the exhaust secondary rocker arm move independently, remove the exhaust primary rocker arm and the exhaust secondary rocker arm as an assembly, and check that the pistons in the rocker arms move smoothly. If any exhaust rocker arm needs replacing, replace the primary and secondary rocker arms as an assembly, then retest.
    • If the exhaust primary rocker arm and the exhaust secondary rocker arm move together, go to Step 13 .
  1. Remove the air supply, the air adapter, the VTEC air stop tool A, and the VTEC air stop tool B.
  1. Tighten the sealing bolts to 22 N·m (2.2 kgf·m, 16 lbf·ft).
  1. Install the cylinder head covers.
  1. Install the six spark plugs.
