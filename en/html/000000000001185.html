SRS DTC Troubleshooting: 74-2x
DTC 74-2x (74-20 to 74-29, 74-2A to 74-2F):
 Open in Right Side E-pretensioner Motor (Except KD model)
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 74-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect e-pretensioner subharness 14P connector B from the e-pretensioner unit.
  1. Disconnect e-pretensioner subharness 2P connector from the right side e-pretensioner motor.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 13 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) and the No. 2 terminal of right side e-pretensioner motor 2P connector, and between the No. 14 terminal of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) and the No. 1 terminal of right side e-pretensioner motor 2P connector. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Open in the e-pretensioner subharness or right side wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Reconnect the front right side e-pretensioner motor 2P connector.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 13 and No. 14 terminals of e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P). There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Faulty e-pretensioner unit; replace the e-pretensioner unit.
NO -
Faulty e-pretensioner motor; replace the right side e-pretensioner motor. ■
