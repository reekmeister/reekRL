Hands Free Telephone System Symptom Troubleshooting - The communication with connected device becomes interrupted or it changes
The communication with connected device becomes interrupted or it changes
NOTE:
  • Check the vehicle battery condition first.
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  • Check if the audio system functions normally.
  • The connected device may not function properly when it is in the trunk or in a metallic case.
  1. Turn on the audio unit, and check for sound in each mode (AM, FM, and CD).
Does the symptom occur?
YES -
Substitute a known-good audio unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original audio unit. ■
NO -
