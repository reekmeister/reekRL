Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
Front Seat Belt - Except KD Model
NOTE: Check the front seat belts for damage, and replace them if necessary.
  1. Passenger's: Slide the front seat forward fully.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the audio and navigation systems, then write down the audio presets.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Driver's: Remove the front seat.
  1. Driver's: Prepare a sheet of cardboard which is 2—2.5 mm (0.08—1.0 in.) thick.
    NOTE: If there is no suitable cardboard, put some seats of thin paper together until they will be required thick.
  1. Driver's: Make a jig (A) from the prepared cardboard (B).
Dimensions of the jig
  1. Driver's: Slowly insert the jig (A) into holes (B) in the back of the lap connector cover (C).
    NOTE: Make sure the jig is perpendicular to the lap connector cover.
  1. Driver's: With the jig (A) still inserted in the lap connector cover (B), carefully insert the tip of a small screwdriver (C) into the hole in the lap connector cover, and unlock the lap connector by pushing in on the screwdriver.
  1. Driver's: Unbuckle the front seat belt lap connector (A).
    -1.
    While pulling the front seat belt (B) upward, remove the screwdriver (C). As soon as you remove the screwdriver from the lap connector (D), the anchor connector will come off the lap connector.
    -2.
    Remove the jig (E).
  1. Passenger's: Pull the lower anchor cover (A) back, and remove the lower anchor bolt (B).
  1. Remove the center pillar lower trim.
  1. Driver's: Pull the carpet back as needed.
  1. Driver's: Disconnect the seat belt outer lap tensioner connector (A), and remove the lower anchor bolt (B), then remove the outer lap tensioner (C).
  1. Remove the center pillar upper trim and slider.
  1. Remove the upper anchor bolt (A).
  1. Disconnect the seat belt tensioner connector (A), seat belt tensioner reducer connector (B), and seat belt motor connector (C) (for some models). Remove the upper retractor mounting bolt (D) and the lower retractor bolt (E), then remove the front seat belt (F) and retractor (G).
  1. If necessary, remove the front seat belt protector (H).
  1. Remove the shoulder anchor adjuster (A).
  1. Install the seat belt in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the anchor bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Check that the retractor locking mechanism functions as described.
    • Assemble the washers, collars, and bushings on the upper and lower anchor bolts as shown.
    • If the seat belt tensioner has been deployed, replace the front seat belt protector with a new one.
    • Before installing the anchor bolts, make sure there are no twists or kinks in the seat belt.
    • Make sure the seat belt tensioner connector, tension reducer connector, and seat belt motor connector (for some models) are plugged in properly.
    • Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
    • Check for any DTCs, and clear them using the HDS. If the DTCs will not clear, perform the necessary troubleshooting.
    • Enter the anti-theft codes for the audio and navigation systems, then enter the audio presets.
    • Do the steering column position memorization.

Front Seat Belt - KD Model
NOTE: Check the front seat belts for damage, and replace them if necessary.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Slide the front seat forward fully, and remove the anchor cover (A).
  1. Remove the lower anchor bolt (A).
  1. Remove the center pillar lower trim.
  1. Remove the center pillar upper trim and slider.
  1. Remove the upper anchor bolt (A).
  1. Disconnect the seat belt tensioner connector (A) and seat belt tensioner reducer connector (B). Remove the upper retractor mounting bolt (C) and the lower retractor bolt (D), then remove the front seat belt (E) and retractor (F).
  1. If necessary, remove the front seat belt protector (G).
  1. Remove the shoulder anchor adjuster (A).
  1. Install the seat belt in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the anchor bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Check that the retractor locking mechanism functions as described.
    • Assemble the washer and bushing on the upper anchor bolt as shown.
    • If the seat belt tensioner has been deployed, replace the front seat belt protector with a new one.
    • Before installing the anchor bolts, make sure there are no twists or kinks in the seat belt.
    • Make sure the seat belt tensioner and tension reducer connectors are plugged in properly.
    • Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
    • Check for any DTCs, and clear them using the HDS. If the DTCs will not clear, perform the necessary troubleshooting.
    • Do the steering column position memorization.

Seat Belt Buckle
  1. Except KD model: Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the audio and navigation systems, and then write down the audio presets.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Release the hook (A) of the seat cushion cover, then disconnect the seat belt switch connector (B). Except KD model is shown; KD model is similar.
  1. Remove the center anchor bolt (A), then remove the seat belt buckle (B). Except KD model is shown; KD model is similar.
  1. Pull the seat belt switch harness (C) out through the space between the seat cushion and the seat linkage.
  1. Install the buckle in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Assemble the washers on the center anchor bolt as shown.
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the center anchor bolt before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
    • Except KD model: Enter the anti-theft codes for the audio and navigation systems, and then enter the audio presets.
    • Do the steering column position memorization.
