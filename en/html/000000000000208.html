Transfer Cover Tapered Roller Bearing Outer Race Removal/Installation
Special Tools Required

Handle driver
 07749-0010000

Driver attachment, 78 x 80 mm
 07NAD-PX40100
NOTE: Replace the bearing with a new one whenever the outer race is replaced.
  1. Remove the bearing outer race (A) and the 80 mm thrust shim (B) from the transfer cover (C). If the bearing outer race is press-fitted, remove the outer race by heating the cover to about 100 °C (212 °F) with a heat gun (D). Do not heat the cover more than 100 °C (212 °F).
  1. Install the 80 mm trust shim (A) and the bearing outer race (B) in the transfer cover (C).
  1. Drive the outer race securely in the cover with the special tools so there is no clearance between the outer race, thrust shim, and cover.
