Cowl Cover Replacement
With POP-UP HOOD: POP-UP HOOD system components are located in this area. Review the POP-UP HOOD system component locations and the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the cowl cover and body.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Use a clip remover to remove the hood rear seal clips.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Detach the clips (A, B, C), then remove the cowl cover upper trim (D) by releasing hooks (E) on both sides.
  1. Disconnect the windshield washer tubes (F).
  1. Remove the windshield wiper arms.
  1. Remove the rear hood seal (A), detach the clips (B, C, D) by carefully pulling the passenger's cowl cover (E) upward, and release the hook (F) on the passenger's cowl cover from the driver's cowl cover (G), then remove the cover by releasing the hooks (H).
  1. Remove the rear hood seal (A), detach the clips (B, C, D) by carefully pulling the driver's cowl cover (E) upward, then remove the cover by releasing the hooks (F).
  1. With POP-UP HOOD: If the POP-UP HOOD has been activated, replace the cowl covers and the cowl cover upper trim.
  1. Install the covers in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Make sure the washer tubes are connected securely.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
