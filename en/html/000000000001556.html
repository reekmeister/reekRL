Rear Differential Side Case Oil Seal Replacement
Special Tools Required

Driver C
 070AD-RJC0130

Driver, 56 x 62 mm
 070AD-PYZA100
  1. Remove the rear differential.
  1. Remove the 40 x 72 x 7.5 mm dust seal (A) with a commercially available tool.
  1. Remove the thrust washer.
  1. Install a 6 mm screw (P/N 93901-16580) (A) at each • mark (4 places, 90 degrees apart) (B) of 35.5 x 70 x 9 mm oil seal (C), then remove the 35.5 x 70 x 9 mm oil seal as shown.
    NOTE: Do not thread the 6 mm screws into the 35.5 x 70 x 9 mm oil seal more than 5 mm (0.197 in.).
  1. Install the new 35.5 x 70 x 9 mm oil seal (A) with the special tools.
  1. Install the thrust washer (A), then install the new 40 x 72 x 7.5 mm dust seal (B) with the special tools.
  1. Install the rear differential.
