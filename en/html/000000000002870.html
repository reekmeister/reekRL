Entry Lights Control System (MICU, Driver's and Passenger's MPCS Unit, Left and Right Rear Window Switches) Input Test
MICU
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the driver's side kick panel.
  1. Disconnect driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connectors E, H, P, and W.
    NOTE: All connector views are wire side of female terminals.
  1. Inspect the connector and socket terminals to be sure they are all making good contact.
    • If the terminals are bent, loose or corroded, repair them as necessary and recheck the system.
    • If the terminals look OK, go to Step 5 .
  1. With the connectors still disconnected, make these input tests at the connectors.
    • If any test indicates a problem, find and correct the cause, then recheck the system.
    • If all the input tests prove OK, go to Step 6 .
    Cavity
    Wire
    Test condition
    Test: Desired result
    Possible cause if result is not obtained
    P1 [P14]

    Check for continuity to ground: There should be continuity.
    • Poor ground (G502)
    • An open in the wire
    E9·H9
    BLK
    Under all conditions
    Check for continuity to ground: There should be continuity.
    • Poor ground (G601)
    • An open in the wire
    P8[P7]
    PNK
    Under all conditions
    Attach to ground: The driver's and front passenger's footwell lights should come on.
    • Blown No. 6 (7.5 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box
    • Faulty footwell light
    • An open in the wire
    P24 * [P21]
    LT GRN
    Under all conditions
    Attach to ground: The left rear and right rear footwell lights should come on.

    • An open in the wire
    H14
    ORN

    • Faulty ignition key light
