DTC Troubleshooting: B1805
DTC B1805:
 Power Seat Control Unit Lost Communication with Gauge Control Module (VSP/NE Message)
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1805 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The driving position memory system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections, between the power seat control unit connector A (40P) and the gauge control module connector B (28P).■
  1. Check the DTCs with the HDS.
Are DTCs B1011, B1060, B1205, B1408, B1805 and/or B2160 also indicated?
YES -
Turn the ignition switch OFF, and do the Gauge Control Module Input Test. ■
NO -
Turn the ignition switch OFF, and do the Power Seat Control Unit Input Test. ■
