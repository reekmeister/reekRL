DTC Troubleshooting: B1181
DTC B1181:
 Gauge Control Module Lost Communication with Driver's MPCS Unit (DOORSW Message)
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Wait for 6 seconds.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1181 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The gauge control system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections between the gauge control module connector B (28P) and driver's MPCS unit 40P connector.■
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Are DTCs B1014, B1064, and B1811 also indicated?
YES -
Go to the Driver's MPCS Input Test. ■
NO -
Go to the Gauge Control Module Input Test. ■
