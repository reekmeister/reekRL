Rear Door Glass, Quarter Glass, and Regulator Replacement
NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Door panel
    • Door glass inner weatherstrip
    • Door sash outer trim
  1. Remove the screws, then remove the door ashtray bracket (A). Disconnect the latch actuator connector (B), and remove the cap (C), and then remove the plastic cover (D).
  1. Carefully move the glass (A) until you can see the bolts through the oval holes in the door, then remove them.
  1. Pull the glass run channel (A) away as needed, then remove the screw (B). Remove the bolts (C, D) securing the center channel (E), then remove the collar (F).
  1. Pull the upper portion of the center channel (A) forward to remove it from the door quarter glass seal (B).
  1. Pull the door weatherstrip (A), and remove the screw, then remove the quarter glass (B). Take care not to damage the outer weatherstrip (C).
  1. Carefully remove the glass (A) through the window slot. Take care not to drop the glass inside the door.
  1. Disconnect the connector (A) from the regulator (B).
  1. Remove the bolts (C), and loosen the bolts (D), then remove the regulator through the hole in the door.
  1. Apply multipurpose grease to all the sliding surfaces of the regulator (A) where shown.
  1. Install the glass and regulator in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Special corrosion-resistant bolts are used in this area. The use of any other type of hardware may result in accelerated corrosion.
    • Make sure the motor connector is plugged in properly.
    • Roll the glass up and down to see if it moves freely without binding.
    • Make sure that there is no clearance between the glass and glass run channel when the glass is closed.
    • Adjust the position of the glass as necessary.
    • Reset the power window control unit.
    • Check for water leaks.
    • Test-drive and check for wind noise and rattles.
    • When reinstalling the door panel, make sure the plastic cover is installed properly and sealed around its outside perimeter to seal out water.
