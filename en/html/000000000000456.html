Door Molding Replacement
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Be careful not to pry too far or you may bend the molding.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front door panel and plastic cover
    • Rear door panel and plastic cover
  1. Release the clips (A), and gently pry the front door molding (B) or rear door molding (C) away from the door while separating the adhesive tape (D, E, F).
    Adhesive tape (D, E, F):
    Thickness 0.8 mm (0.03 in.)

  1. Install the moldings in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE: Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
