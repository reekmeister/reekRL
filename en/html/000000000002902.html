Seat Heater Test - Front Seat without Ventilated Seats
Driver's Seat
  1. Remove the driver's seat, then remove the seat-back.
  1. Disconnect the 4P connector from the seat cushion heater and 2P connector from the seat-back heater.
Seat Cushion Heater Test
  1. Check for continuity between the seat cushion heater 4P connector No. 1 and No. 2 terminals, and No. 2 and No. 3 terminals. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, replace the seat cushion heater.
Seat-back Heater Test
  1. Check for continuity between the seat-back heater 2P connector No. 1 and No. 2 terminals. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, replace the seat-back heater.
  1. Reconnect the 4P and 2P connectors to the seat heaters, and check for continuity between the seat cushion heater 4P connector No. 3 terminal and the seat-back heater 2P connector No. 2 terminal. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, repair an open in the wire between the seat cushion heater and the seat-back heater.
Front Passenger's Seat
  1. Remove the front passenger's seat.
  1. Disconnect the 3P connector from the seat cushion heater.
  1. Check for continuity between the seat cushion heater 3P connector No. 1 and No. 3 terminals, and No. 2 and No. 3 terminals. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, replace the seat cushion heater.
