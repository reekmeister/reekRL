A/T Shift Cable Replacement
NOTE: The illustration shows the LHD model; RHD is similar.
  1. Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is supported securely.
  1. Remove the center console cover.
  1. Shift the transmission into the R position.
  1. Remove the nut securing the shift cable end.
  1. Press the holder lock release (A), and pull out the socket holder (B) to remove the shift cable (C) from the shift lever bracket (D).
  1. Get the customer's radio and navigation anti-theft codes, and write down the radio presets.
  1. Remove the battery trim and left upper fender trim.
  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal, then disconnect the battery positive terminal.
  1. Remove the battery hold-down bracket, and remove the battery and battery tray.
  1. Remove the intake air duct and air cleaner housing.
  1. Remove the battery base.
  1. Remove the nuts securing the shift cable bracket (A).
  1. Remove the spring clip/washer (B) and control pin (C), then separate the shift cable end (D) from the control lever (E).
  1. Remove the shift cable bracket from the steering gearbox stiffener.
  1. Remove the heat shield under the shift cable grommet.
  1. Remove the shift cable grommet, and pull out the shift cable.
  1. Insert the new shift cable through the grommet hole, and install the grommet in its hole. Do not bend the shift cable excessively.
  1. Install the heat shield.
  1. Install the shift cable bracket on the steering gearbox stiffener.
  1. Apply molybdenum grease to the bore hole of synthetic resin bushing (A) in the shift cable end (B), and attach the shift cable end to the control lever (C), then insert the control pin (D) into the control lever hole through the shift cable end, and secure the control pin with the spring clip/washer (E).
  1. Secure the shift cable bracket (F) with the nuts.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and verify that the R position indicator comes on.
  1. If necessary, push the shift cable (A) until it stops, then release it. Pull the shift cable back one step so that the shift position is in R. Do not hold the shift cable guide (B) and damper (C) to adjust the shift cable.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Place the shift lever in the R position, then insert a 6.0 mm (0.24 in.) pin (A) through the positioning hole (B) on the shift lever bracket, through the positioning hole (C) on the shift lever, and into the positioning hole (D) on the shift lever bracket.
  1. Verify that the shift lever is secured in the R position.
  1. Align the socket holder (A) on the shift cable (B) with the slot in the shift lever bracket (C), then slide the holder into the bracket. Install the shift cable end (D) over the mounting stud (E) by aligning its square hole (F) with the square fitting (G) at the bottom of the stud. Push the holder until it snaps securely in place. Do not install the shift cable by holding the shift cable guide (H) and damper (I).
  1. Verify that the shift cable end (A) is properly installed on the mounting stud (B).
  1. If improperly installed, remove the shift cable from the shift lever bracket, and reinstall the shift cable. Do not install the shift cable end on the mounting stud while the shift cable is on the bracket.
  1. Install and tighten the nut.
  1. Remove the 6.0 mm (0.24 in.) pin that was installed to hold the shift lever.
  1. Install the battery base.
  1. Install the intake air duct and air cleaner housing.
  1. Install the battery tray, battery, and battery hold-down bracket, then connect the battery terminals.
  1. Install the left upper fender cover and battery trim.
  1. Move the shift lever to each position, and verify that the A/T gear position indicator follows the transmission range switch.
  1. Allow the all wheels to rotate freely.
  1. Start the engine, and check the shift lever operation in all gears.
  1. Push the shift lock release, and verify that the shift lever releases.
  1. Reinstall the center console cover.
  1. Enter the radio and navigation anti-theft codes, then enter the radio presets, and set the clock.
  1. Do the steering column position memorization procedure.
