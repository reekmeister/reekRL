Steering Angle Sensor Replacement
NOTE: Do not damage or drop the combination switch as the steering angle sensor is sensitive to shock and vibration.
  1. Remove the steering wheel and steering column cover.
  1. Remove the combination switch assembly.
  1. Remove the combination light (or wiper/washer) switch (A) and the wiper/washer (or combination) switch (B).
  1. Replace the combination switch body complete (C).
  1. Install the combination switch in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE: Do not remove the steering angle sensor from the combination switch body.
  1. Do the VSA sensor neutral position memorization.
  1. Do the SH-AWD steering neutral position memorization.
  1. For with AFS model: Do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure.
  1. For with LKAS model: Do the steering angle sensor neutral position memorization.
