Front Passenger's Airbag Replacement
Removal
NOTE: If the front passenger's airbag has been deployed, refer to the install for after a collision where the front passenger's airbag deployed.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Remove the glove box.
  1. Disconnect the front passenger's airbag 4P connector (A) from dashboard wire harness.
  1. Remove the front passenger's air conditioning duct (A).
  1. Remove the mounting nuts (A) from the bracket. Remove the front passenger's airbag (B).
Installation
  1. Place the new front passenger's airbag (A) into the bracket. Tighten the front passenger's airbag mounting nuts (B).
  1. Connect the front passenger's airbag 4P connector (A) to dashboard wire harness, then reinstall the glove box.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Connect the HDS and clear the DTCs.
  1. After installing the airbag, confirm proper system operation: Turn the ignition switch ON (II); the SRS indicator should come on for about 6 seconds and then go off.
Installation for after a collision where the front passenger's airbag deployed
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Remove the dashboard assembly from the vehicle.
  1. Place the new front passenger's airbag (A) into the bracket. Tighten the front passenger's airbag mounting nuts (B).
    NOTE:
    • Be sure to install the front passenger's airbag so it does not interfere with other parts.
    • When you install the front passenger's airbag, be careful not to damage the bag.
    • Be sure to keep the new airbag clean and do not unfold or tamper with the cloth cover on the airbag.
  1. Install the dashboard assembly in the vehicle.
  1. Connect the front passenger's airbag 4P connector (A) to the dashboard wire harness, then reinstall the removed parts.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Connect the HDS and clear the DTCs.
  1. After installing the airbag, confirm proper system operation: Turn the ignition switch ON (II); the SRS indicator should come on for about 6 seconds and then go off.
