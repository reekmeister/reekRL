EPS Symptom Troubleshooting - EPS indicator does not come on
EPS indicator does not come on
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and watch the indicators in the gauge control module.
Are all indicators going off?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Go to Step 2 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Connect the HDS to the data link connector (DLC).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check the WARNING LAMP in the EPS DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is ON indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Replace the EPS (LKAS) control unit. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect gauge control module connector B (28P).
  1. Disconnect EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P).
  1. Check for continuity between EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 4 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between the gauge control module and the EPS (LKAS) control unit.■
NO -
Substitute a known-good gauge control module, then go to Step 1 and recheck. If it is OK, replace the original gauge control module. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the No. 21 (10 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Reinstall the fuse, then go to Step 12 .
NO -
Replace the fuse, and recheck.■
  1. Disconnect gauge control module connector B (28P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between gauge control module connector B (28P) terminal No. 1 and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the No. 21 (10 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box and the gauge control module.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect gauge control module connector A (20P).
  1. Check for continuity between gauge control module A (20P) terminal No. 8 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Replace the gauge control module. ■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the gauge control module and body ground (G501).■
