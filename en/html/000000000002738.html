SH-AWD System Symptom Troubleshooting - The SH-AWD indicator does not go off, and no DTCs are stored
The SH-AWD indicator does not go off, and no DTCs are stored
NOTE:
  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information.
  • Make sure the No. 1 (30 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box is OK.
  • Make sure there are no loose connector terminals at the SH-AWD control unit.
  • Make sure the charging system is OK.
  • Make sure the PGM-FI system and the A/T system is not in the middle of updating.
  1. Test the battery . Charge or replace the battery if necessary.
  1. Check the COMPLETE MEMORIZATION in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
Does the data list show ‘‘complete''?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Do the SH-AWD system memorization procedures for each of the following, then go to Step 3 .
  • Differential clutch torque
  • Steering angle neutral position
  • Yaw rate-lateral acceleration neutral position
  • Control unit initialization
  1. Check for DTCs in the VSA system with the HDS.
Are any steering angle sensor DTCs indicated?
YES -
Go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check the SH-AWD indicator value with the HDS.
Is the SH-AWD indicator value OFF?
YES -
Go to the gauge control module self-diagnostic function. ■
NO -
Substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
