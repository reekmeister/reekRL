DTC Troubleshooting: B2190
DTC B2190:
 Front Passenger's Window Position Detect Circuit Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Open and close the front passenger's window by using the front passenger's window switch manually.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2177 or B2178 also indicated?
YES -
Troubleshoot the indicated DTC.■
NO -
Go to Step 5 .
  1. Reset the front passenger's window.
  1. Open and close the front passenger's window by using the front passenger's window switch manually.
  1. Check for the DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2190 also indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
The system is OK at this time.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Substitute a known-good passenger's MPCS unit.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Open and close the front passenger's window by using the front passenger's window switch manually.
  1. Check for the DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2190 indicated?
YES -
Replace the front passenger's window motor.■
NO -
Replace the passenger's MPCS unit.■
