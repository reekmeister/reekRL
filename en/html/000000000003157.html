ACC/LKAS Symptom Troubleshooting - LKAS does not function (NO DTC)
LKAS does not function (No DTC)
NOTE: The LKAS does not function when the temperature of LKAS camera/control unit is outside of regulation (from −10 °C to 80 °C).
By using the HDS (select DRIVING SUPPORT, LKAS CAMERA, and DATA LIST in sequence from the menu), you can confirm the history of abnormality for temperature and mileage in the LKAS camera/control unit when abnormality was detected and the current temperature of the LKAS camera/control unit.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), then MAIN switch on.
  1. Watch the following indicator (A) in the MID.
Does the indicator come on?
YES -
Intermittent unavailable as the temperature of LKAS camera/control unit was outside of regulation (from −10 °C to 80 °C). When the temperature of LKAS camera/control unit is in specified value (from −10 °C to 80 °C), the LKAS works normally.
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Watch the LKAS indicator (GREEN).
Does the LKAS indicator (GREEN) come on?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Go to the symptom troubleshooting, LKAS indicator (green) does not come on.
  1. Intermittent unavailable as the temperature of LKAS camera/control unit was outside of regulation (from −10 °C to 80 °C). The system is OK at this time.■
