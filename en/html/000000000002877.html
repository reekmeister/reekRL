Front Seat Lumbar Support Replacement
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to tear the seam or damage the seat covers.
  • Take care not to bend the cable.
  • LHD model is shown; RHD model is similar.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove the seat-back cover/pad.
  1. Release the hook strip as needed, then turn over the seat-back cover.
  1. Release the upper wire (A) from both seat-back side frames (B) of inside the seat-back.
  1. Release the lower wire (A) from both active head restraint links (B) of inside the seat-back.
  1. Remove the lumbar support motor cover (A).
    -1.
    Release the cover from the lower hooks (B).
    -2.
    Pull the cover upward to release it from the upper hooks (C).
Driver's seat
Passenger's seat
  1. Disconnect the lumbar support motor connector (A), and detach the harness clip (B). Remove the bolts(C), nuts (D, E) and disconnect the OPDS unit connector (F) (on passenger's seat), then remove the lumbar support motor (G).
  1. Install the lumbar support in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly.
    • To prevent wrinkles when installing a seat-back cover, make sure the material is stretched evenly over the pad before securing the hook strips.
    • If the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, replace them with new ones.
