SRS DTC Troubleshooting: 85-61, 85-62
DTC 85-61:
 No Signal from OPDS Unit
DTC 85-62:
 Non-stipulated Response Data
  1. Make sure nothing is on the front passenger's seat.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 85-61, 85-62 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures.
  1. Check the connection between the OPDS unit harness 8P connector and the OPDS unit.
Is the connection OK?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Repair the poor connection and retest. If DTC 85-61 is still present, go to Step 5 .
  1. Erase the DTC.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 85-61, 85-62 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the No. 10 (7.5 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Replace the fuse, then turn the ignition switch ON (II). If the fuse blows again, check for a short in the No. 10 (7.5 A) fuse circuit (dashboard wire harness A, side wire harness, or OPDS unit harness).■
  1. Disconnect the OPDS unit harness 8P connector (A) from the OPDS unit.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 4 terminal of the OPDS unit harness 8P connector and body ground. There should be battery voltage.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 16 .
NO -
  • LHD type: Go to Step 12 .
  • RHD type: Go to Step 16 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect dashboard wire harness 34P connector (A) from the right side wire harness (B).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 34 terminal of dashboard wire harness 34P connector and body ground. There should be battery voltage.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Open in the WHT wire of the right side wire harness or OPDS unit harness; replace the faulty harness.■
NO -
Open in the GRN wire of the dashboard wire harness; replace dashboard wire harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box connector F (8P) from the passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 3 terminal of the passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box connector F (8P) and body ground. There should be battery voltage.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Open in the WHT wire of the left side wire harness or OPDS unit harness; replace the faulty harness.■
NO -
Open in the WHT wire of the dashboard wire harness; replace dashboard wire harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 8 terminal of the OPDS unit harness 8P connector and body ground. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 22 .
NO -
Open in the OPDS unit harness or side wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect SRS unit connector A (28P) from the SRS unit.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and body ground. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 25 .
NO -
Go to Step 28 .
  1. Check resistance between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and the No. 7 terminal of the OPDS unit harness 8P connector. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Faulty OPDS unit or SRS unit; replace the OPDS unit. If the problem is still present, replace the SRS unit. ■
NO -
Go to Step 26 .
  1. Disconnect the side wire harness 4P connector (A) from the OPDS unit harness.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and the No. 4 terminal of right side (LHD type) or left side (RHD type) wire harness 4P connector. There should be 0−1.0 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Open in the OPDS unit harness; replace the OPDS unit harness.■
NO -
Open in dashboard wire harness or right side (LHD type) or left side (RHD type) wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Disconnect side wire harness 4P connector (A) from the OPDS unit harness.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 14 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and body ground. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Short to ground in the OPDS unit harness; replace the OPDS unit harness.■
NO -
Short to ground in dashboard wire harness or right side (LHD type) or left side (RHD type) wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
