Rear Seat Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the body or tear the seat covers.
  • Take care not to damage the rear seat belts, center seat belt, and seat belt buckles.
  1. Pull the seat hook knobs (A) while pushing down on the seat cushion (B), to release the hooks (C).
  1. Remove the seat cushion (A), and release the center seat belt buckles (B) from their slits (C).
  1. Release the seat belt (A) from their guides (B) (three places).
  1. Except KD model: Disconnect the right seat belt buckle switch connector (A), and detach the connector (B) from the rear seat-back frame (C).
  1. Remove the center anchor bolts, then remove the rear seat belt buckles and center seat belt buckle.
    Take care not to damage the left seat belt buckle and center seat belt buckle switch harness (except KD model).
  1. Remove the bolts from the base of the seat-back (A).
  1. Disconnect the power headrest connector (B). LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Lift the seat-back (A) up to release the wire loops (B) from the hooks (C) on the body, then remove the seat-back.
  1. Except KD model: Remove the left seat belt buckle and center seat belt buckle to prevent damage the wire harness by disconnecting the buckle switch connector.
  1. Install the seat in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Guide the belts over the front of the seat-back as you install it.
    • Before attaching the rear seat-back and cushion, make sure there are no twists or kinks in the seat belts.
    • Except KD model: Before attaching the seat-back, make sure the rear seat belt buckle switch wire harnesses are not pinched.
    • Make sure the power headrest connector and buckle switch connectors (except KD model) are plugged in properly.
    • When installing the seat cushion, slip the seat belt buckles through the slits in the seat cushion.
