Transmission Range Switch Test
  1. Get the customer's radio and navigation anti-theft codes, and write down the radio presets.
  1. Remove the battery trim and left upper fender cover.
  1. Disconnect the negative terminal from the battery, then disconnect the positive terminal.
  1. Remove the battery hold-down bracket, and remove the battery and battery tray.
  1. Remove the air intake duct and air cleaner housing.
  1. Remove the battery base.
  1. Disconnect the transmission range switch connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the terminals at the switch connector. There should be continuity between the terminals in the following table for each switch position.
  1. The transmission range switch test is finished if the test results are OK.
    If there is no continuity between any terminals, check that transmission range switch installation. If the switch installation is OK, replace the switch.
  1. Check the connector for rust, dirt, or oil, clean or repair if necessary, then connect the connector securely.
  1. Install the battery base.
  1. Install the intake air duct and air cleaner housing.
  1. Install the battery tray, battery, and battery hold-down bracket, then connect battery terminals.
  1. Install the left upper fender cover and battery trim.
  1. Enter the radio and navigation anti-theft codes, then enter the radio presets, and set the clock.
  1. Do the steering column position memorization procedure.
  1. Do the power window control unit reset procedure.
