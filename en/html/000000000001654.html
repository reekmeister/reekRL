Center Console Armrest Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the console.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  1. Remove the center console.
  1. Detach the clips, then remove the console trim (A).
  1. Remove the screws (A) and detach the clips (B) and hooks (C), then remove the left console side trim (D) and right console side trim (E).
  1. Remove the screws, and release the tabs (A), then remove the console outlet trim (B).
  1. Remove the screws, hinge bushings (A), and hinge stoppers (B) from both sides, then remove the console armrest (C).
  1. Remove the hinge springs (D) and bushings (E) from both sides.
  1. Install the armrest in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Replace any damaged clips with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
