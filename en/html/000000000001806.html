DTC Troubleshooting: P0705 (5-2)
DTC P0705:
 Short in Transmission Range Switch Circuit (Multiple Shift-position Input)
NOTE:
  • Record all freeze data and review General Troubleshooting Information before you troubleshoot.
  • This code is caused by an electrical circuit problem and cannot be caused by a mechanical problem in the transmission.
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Move the shift lever through all positions, stopping for at least 1 second in each position. Monitor the OBD status for P0705 in the DTCs/Freeze Data in A/T Mode Menu for a pass/fail.
Does the result indicate FAILED?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for an intermittent short in the wire between the transmission range switch and the PCM. If the HDS indicates NOT COMPLETED, return to Step 2 and recheck.■
  1. Inspect the transmission range switch.
Is the switch OK?
YES -
Reconnect the switch connector, then go to Step 5 .
NO -
Replace the transmission range switch, then go to Step 44 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between PCM connector terminals D13 and A3 or B15, in all positions other than P.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect PCM connector D (17P).
  1. Disconnect the transmission range switch connector.
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminal D13 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between PCM connector terminal D13 and the transmission range switch, then go to Step 44 .
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Disconnect PCM connectors A (31P) and B (24P).
  1. Check for continuity between transmission range switch connector terminals A3 and body ground, and between B15 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck for DTC P0705. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good PCM, replace the original PCM, then go to Step 44 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between PCM connector terminals A3, B15, and ground (G101, G102), or repair poor ground (G101, G102), then go to Step 44 .
