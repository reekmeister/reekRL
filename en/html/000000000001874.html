DTC Troubleshooting: P2101 (40)
DTC P2101:
 Throttle Actuator System Malfunction
Do not insert your fingers into the installed throttle body when you turn the ignition switch ON (II) or while the ignition switch is ON (II). If you do, you will seriously injure your fingers if the throttle valve is activated.

NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Do the ETCS TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P2101 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Go to Step 5 .
  1. Test-drive the vehicle for several minutes in the range of these recorded freeze data parameters:
    • ENGINE SPEED
    • VSS
    • APP SENSOR
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P2101 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the throttle body and the throttle actuator control module, then clean the throttle body. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the intake air duct from the throttle body.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Do the ETCS TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
  1. Visually check the throttle valve operation.
Does the throttle valve operate smoothly?
YES -
Clean the throttle body, then go to Step 22 and recheck. If DTC P2101 is indicated, go to Step 20 .
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the throttle body 6P connector.
  1. Disconnect the 13P connector between the PCM subharness and the throttle actuator control module subharness.
  1. Connect throttle body 6P connector terminals No. 5 and No. 6 with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between 13P connector terminals No. 6 and No. 13.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 18 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the throttle body and the 13P connector (motor drive lines), then go to Step 22 .
  1. Disconnect the throttle actuator control module 16P connector, LHD model, RHD model.
  1. Check for continuity between throttle actuator control module 16P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 8 and 13P connector terminals No. 6 and No. 13 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Substitute a known-good throttle actuator control module, LHD model, RHD model, then go to Step 22 and recheck. If DTC P2101 is not indicated, replace the original throttle actuator control module, LHD model, RHD model, then go to Step 22 . If DTC P2101 is indicated, go to Step 20 .
NO -
Repair open in the wires between the throttle actuator control module and the 13P connector (motor drive lines), then go to Step 22 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Replace the throttle body.
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Reset the PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle for several minutes in the range of these recorded freeze data parameter:
    • ENGINE SPEED
    • VSS
    • APP SENSOR
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P2101 indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the throttle body and the throttle actuator control module, then clean the throttle body, and go to Step 1 .
NO -
Troubleshooting is complete. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
