Trunk Lid Latch Replacement - With Power Trunk
With Power Trunk Lid
NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Trunk rear trim panel
    • Trunk side trim panel, right side
  1. Disconnect the actuator connector (A), then remove the connector clip (B). Remove the trunk lid opener cable (C) from the clips (D) on the body. Take care not to kink the opener cable.
  1. Remove the bolts (E) and loosen the bolt (F), then remove the latch.
  1. Pull the trunk lid latch (A) away to release the bolt (B) from the hole (C) in the body.
  1. Disconnect the trunk lid opener cable (A).
  1. Install the latch in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Align the marks (A) on the opener cable (B) with the cable clips (C) as shown.
    • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly and the opener cable is connected properly.
    • Make sure the trunk lid opens properly and locks securely.
    • Fix at the original position in the end of the cable on the cable bracket securely. Make sure the trunk lid latch unlocks when pulling the trunk lid opener cable. If necessary, adjust the position of the cable end.
