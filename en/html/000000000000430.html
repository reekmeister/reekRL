Rear Inner Fender Replacement
NOTE: Take care not to scratch the body.
  1. Remove the rear suspension damper unit.
  1. Remove the rear inner fender (A).
    -1.
    Remove the screws (B, C), then remove the rear splash guard (D) (for some models).
    -2.
    Remove the bolt (E) and screws (F) from the rear bumper (G) and from the rear of the rear inner fender.
    -3.
    Remove the clips (H, I).
    -4.
    On the front of the rear wheel arch, remove the screws (J) securing the side sill (K) and rear inner fender.
    -5.
    Pull out the rear inner fender.
    NOTE: Left side is shown, right side is similar.
  1. Install the inner fender in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
