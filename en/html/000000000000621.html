Immobilizer System Symptom Troubleshooting - Immobilizer indicator does not go off
Immobilizer indicator does not go off
NOTE: Before troubleshooting, check the items listed in ‘‘General Check before Troubleshooting''.
  1. Try to start the engine with the immobilizer key.
Does the engine start?
YES -
Go to Step 2 .
NO -
Go to engine does not start with the immobilizer troubleshooting. ■
  1. Check the system check with the HDS.
Is the indicated OK?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Troubleshoot the indicated result(s).■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the immobilizer control unit-receiver 7P connector.
  1. Check the immobilizer indicator operation.
Is the immobilizer indicator go off?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Check for the continuity between immobilizer control unit-receiver 7P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 5.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire.■
NO -
Replace the immobilizer control unit-receiver.■
  1. Disconnect the gauge control module connector B (28P).
  1. Check for continuity between No. 28 terminal of the gauge control module connector B (28P) and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire.■
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Check for continuity between gauge control module connector A (20P) terminals No. 8 and gauge control module connector B (28P) terminal No. 28.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire.■
NO -
Replace the gauge control module. ■
