Front Fog Light Replacement
Bulb Replacement
  1. Remove the bolts and clip, then push up the splash shield.
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (A) from the front fog light (B).
    Front Fog Light:
    55 W

  1. Turn the bulb socket (C) 45 ° counterclockwise to remove it from the housing.
  1. Install a new bulb in the reverse order of removal.
Replacement
  1. Remove the front bumper.
  1. Remove the front bumper duct trim.
  1. Remove the screws, then remove the front fog light (A).
  1. Install the light in the reverse order of removal.
  1. After replacement, adjust the front fog lights at the adjuster (A) to local requirements.
