Rear Differential Fluid Inspection and Replacement
Inspection
Clutch and Hypoid Chambers
  1. Park the vehicle on level ground, and turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Use solvent and a brush to wash any oil and dirt off the differential fluid inspection oil filler plugs.
  1. Remove each filler plug (A) and the sealing washer (B), then check the condition of the fluid, and make sure the fluid is at the proper level (C).
  1. The fluid level must reach up the bottom of the filler plug hole. If it is below the hole, add the recommended fluid until it runs out, then reinstall the filler plug with a new sealing washer.
Replacement
Clutch Chamber
  1. Park the vehicle on level ground, and turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the filler plug (A) and the sealing washer (B).
    Fluid Capacity
    2.7 L (2.9 US qt, 2.4 Imp qt) at fluid change
    3.1 L (3.3 US qt, 2.7 Imp qt) at overhaul

    Recommended fluid:
    Honda ATF-Z1

  1. Remove the drain plug (C) and the sealing washer (D), and drain the fluid.
  1. Reinstall the drain plug with a new sealing washer.
  1. Add fluid until it reaches the bottom of the filler plug hole.
  1. Reinstall the filler plug with a new sealing washer.
Hypoid Chamber
  1. Park the vehicle on level ground, and turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the filler plug (A) and the sealing washer (B).
    Fluid Capacity
    0.73 L (0.77 US qt, 0.64 Imp qt) at fluid change
    0.75 L (0.79 US qt, 0.66 Imp qt) at overhaul

    Recommended fluid:
    Honda ATF-Z1

  1. Remove the drain plug (C) and the sealing washer (D), and drain the fluid.
  1. Reinstall the drain plug with a new sealing washer.
  1. Add fluid until it reaches the bottom of the filler plug hole.
  1. Reinstall the filler plug with a new sealing washer.
