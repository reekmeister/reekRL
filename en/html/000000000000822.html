DTC Troubleshooting: B2180
DTC B2180:
 Driver's Door Lock Switch LOCK/UNLOCK Signal Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Lock and unlock the driver's door with the door lock switch operation.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2180 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The driver's door lock switch circuit is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
  1. With the driver's door lock switch in the neutral position, select the DOOR LOCK from the BODY ELECTRICAL menu, and enter the DATA LIST.
  1. Check the ON/OFF information of the Driver's door lock switch (LOCK) and Driver's door lock switch (UNLOCK).
Are both information indicators OFF?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Operate the driver's door lock switch in LOCK and UNLOCK positions, and check the ON/OFF information of the Driver's door lock switch (LOCK) and Driver's door lock switch (UNLOCK).
Are both Driver's door lock switch (LOCK) and Driver's door lock switch (UNLOCK) information indicators ON at the same time when the door lock switch is in LOCK or UNLOCK position?
YES -
Repair a short in the wire.■
NO -
Replace the driver's MPCS unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the driver's door lock switch 4P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check the ON/OFF information of the Driver's door lock switch (LOCK) and Driver's door lock switch (UNLOCK).
Are both information indicators OFF?
YES -
Faulty driver's door lock switch; replace it.■
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the driver's MPCS unit connector A (40P).
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the driver's door lock switch 4P connector terminals No. 3 and No. 4 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire.■
NO -
Replace the driver's MPCS unit.■
