A/T Housing Secondary Shaft ATF Feed Pipe Cap, Feed Pipe Replacement
  1. Remove the ATF feed pipe cap from the transmission housing.
  1. Remove the snap ring (A) from the feed pipe cap (B), then remove the feed pipe guide (C), O-rings (D) (E), and 1st clutch ATF feed pipe (F).
  1. Replace the 1st clutch ATF feed pipe or 1st-hold clutch ATF feed pipe/ATF feed pipe cap assembly. The 1st-hold clutch ATF feed pipe/ATF feed pipe cap is not available separately.
  1. Install the new O-ring (D) over the 1st clutch ATF feed pipe, then install the feed pipe over the 1st-hold clutch ATF feed pipe (G) while aligning the feed pipe tabs (H) with the guide in the cap.
  1. Install the new O-ring (E) in the cap and feed pipe guide, then secure the guide with the snap ring.
