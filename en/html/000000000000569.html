Sunroof Frame and Drain Tube Replacement
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Headliner
    • Sunroof glass
  1. Put on gloves to protect your hands. Disconnect the motor connector (A).
  1. Disconnect the drain tubes (A).
  1. With an assistant holding the frame (A), remove the bolts, starting at the rear, and release the rear hooks (B) by moving the frame forward.
Front Drain Tube
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front door sill trim
    • Kick panel
    • Driver's dashboard undercover
  1. Driver's side: Remove the bolt, and release the hook (A), then move the fuse box (B) as needed. LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. To remove a front drain valve (A) from the body, tie a string to the top end of the drain tube, then pull the front drain tube (B) down out of the front pillar. Leave the string in the pillar to use when reinstalling the drain tube.
Rear Drain Tube
  1. Remove these items:
    • Spare tyre lid
    • Trunk rear trim panel
    • Trunk front trim panel
    • Trunk side trim panel
  1. Tie a string to the top end of the rear drain tube (A), then pull the drain tube down out of the pillar. Leave the string in the pillar to use when reinstalling the drain tube.
  1. Install the frame and drain tube in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Before installing the frame, clear the drain tubes and drain valves using compressed air.
    • When installing, tie the string that was left in the pillar to the top end of the new drain tube and pull it up into the roof.
    • Check the frame seal.
    • Clean the surface of the frame.
    • When installing the frame, first attach the rear hooks into the body holes.
    • Make sure the connectors are plugged in properly.
    • When connecting the drain tube, slide it over the frame nozzle at least 10 mm (0.39 in.).
    • Install the tube clip (A) on the drain tube (B) as shown.
    • After all parts are installed, including the headliner, do the sunroof calibration.
    • Adjust the glass position alignment.
  1. Check for water leaks. Let the water run freely from a hose without a nozzle. Do not use a high-pressure spray.
