Dashboard Center Console Ashtray Housing Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Remove the center trim.
  1. Remove the screws.
  1. Gently pull out along the top of the ashtray housing (A) to release the clips, then lower the front side down to release the pins (B) from the select lever bracket (C).
  1. Disconnect the ambient light bulb socket (A), then remove the ashtray housing (B).
  1. Install the ashtray housing in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Make sure the bulb socket is connected securely.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
