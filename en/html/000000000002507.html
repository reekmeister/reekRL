Camshaft Replacement
Front
  1. Do the battery removal procedure.
  1. Drain the engine coolant.
  1. Disconnect the upper radiator hose (A) and the lower radiator hose (B).
  1. Remove the exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) valve.
  1. Remove the stud bolts.
  1. Remove the timing belt.
  1. Remove the rocker arm assembly.
  1. Remove the front camshaft pulley.
  1. Remove the thrust cover (A), then remove the front camshaft (B).
  1. Install the front camshaft in the reverse order of removal. Always use a new O-ring (C). Apply new engine oil to the journals and the cam lobes.
  1. Install the rocker arm assembly mounting bolts.
  1. Install the timing belt.
  1. Install the stud bolts, then install the EGR valve.
  1. Install the upper radiator hose and the lower radiator hose.
  1. Adjust the valve clearance.
  1. Do the battery reconnection procedure.
  1. Fill the radiator with engine coolant and bleed the air out.
  1. Do the crankshaft position (CKP) pattern clear/CKP pattern learn procedure.
Rear
  1. Relieve the fuel pressure.
  1. Remove the quick-connect fitting cover, then disconnect the fuel feed hose.
  1. Remove the intake manifold.
  1. Remove the purge joint (A).
  1. Remove the two bolts (B) securing the vacuum line.
  1. Remove the timing belt.
  1. Remove the rocker arm assembly.
  1. Remove the rear camshaft pulley.
  1. Remove the thrust cover (A), then remove the rear camshaft (B).
  1. Install the rear camshaft in the reverse order of removal. Always use a new O-ring (C). Apply new engine oil to the journals and cam lobes.
  1. Install the rocker arm assembly.
  1. Install the timing belt.
  1. Install the vacuum line and the purge joint.
  1. Install the intake manifold.
  1. Connect the fuel feed hose, then install the quick-connect fitting cover.
  1. Adjust the valve clearance.
  1. Inspect for fuel leaks. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II) (do not operate the starter) so the fuel pump runs for about 2 seconds and pressurizes the fuel line. Repeat this operation three times, then check for fuel leakage at any point in the fuel line.
  1. Do the crankshaft position (CKP) pattern clear/CKP pattern learn procedure.
