DTC Troubleshooting: P16BE (198)
DTC P16BE:
 Starter Cut Relay 1 Malfunction
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS and wait 5 seconds.
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P16BE indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the starter cut relay 1 (A) from the auxiliary under-dash relay holder.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between starter cut relay 1 4P connector terminal No. 2 and body ground.
Is there about 2.5 V?
YES -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at starter cut relay 1 and the PCM.■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the PCM (D5) and the starter cut relay 1, then go to Step 10 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Replace starter cut relay 1.
  1. Reconnect the all connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Reset the PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P16BE indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at starter cut relay 1 and the PCM, then go to Step 1 .
NO -
Troubleshooting is complete. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
