Trunk Opening Panel Replacement
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the body.
  1. Remove the taillight trim.
  1. Separate the double-sided adhesive tape (A), and use a trim tool to detach the pins (B) of the trunk opening panel (C).
  1. Check for damaged or stress-whitened grommets (D, E), and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
  1. Install the trunk opening panel in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Scrape off the remaining double-sided adhesive tape from the cover and body, then clean the cover and body surface with a sponge dampened in alcohol.
    • Apply new adhesive tape (A, B) to the trunk opening panel (C).
    • Push the adhesive portions and pins into place securely.
    Adhesive tape A:
    Thickness 0.5 mm (0.02 in.)
    Width 10 mm (0.39 in.)
    Length 100 mm (3.94 in.)
    Adhesive tape B:
    Thickness 0.5 mm (0.02 in.)
    Width 8 mm (0.3 in.)
    Length 270 mm (10.6 in.)
