Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) DTC Troubleshooting: 11
LKAS Control Unit DTC 11:
 F-CAN Communication with the PCM (A/T System) Malfunction
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0), then turn the ignition switch to ON (II), and wait for 20 seconds or more.
  1. Watch the LKAS indicator.
Does the LKAS indicator (AMBER) come on?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
  1. Check for DTC with the HDS.
Is DTC 11 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Troubleshoot the indicated DTC(s).■
  1. Check for adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit DTCs with HDS.
Is DTC 110 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Check for loose terminals in the LKAS camera/control unit connector, PCM connector A, and the ACC unit connector, and repair if necessary. If all connections are OK, replace the LKAS camera/control unit. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect the LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector and the PCM connector A (31P).
  1. Check for continuity between LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector terminal No. 4 and PCM connector A (31P) terminal No. 15.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire between the LKAS camera/control unit and the branch of the F-CAN line (CANH).■
  1. Check for continuity between LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector terminal No. 3 and PCM connector A (31P) terminal No. 26.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in PCM connector A and the LKAS camera/control unit connector, and repair if necessary. If all connections are OK, replace the PCM. ■
NO -
