Hood Opener Cable Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the body and related parts.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Upper grille cover
    • Headlight, driver's side
    • Radiator tank, as needed
    • Front inner fender, driver's side as needed
    • Kick panel, driver's side
    • Hood latch cover, without adaptive cruise control
  1. Disconnect the hood opener cable (A) from the hood latch (B).
  1. Using a clip remover, detach the clips, and remove the grommet (C) from the body, then remove the hood opener cable from the vehicle. Take care not to kink the cable.
  1. Install the cable in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Locate the cable sponge (D) under the separator (E) and front fender bracket (F).
