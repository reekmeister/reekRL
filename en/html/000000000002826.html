Upper Fender Trim Replacement
With POP-UP HOOD: POP-UP HOOD system components are located in this area. Review the POP-UP HOOD system component locations and the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the grille and body.
  1. Disconnect the washer reservoir tubes (A). Release the clips, then remove the right upper fender trim (B).
  1. Remove the clips and release the hooks (A, B), then remove the battery trim (C) from the upper grille cover (D).
  1. Release the clips and the hooks (A), then remove the left upper fender trim (B). And remove the left upper fender trim (C).
  1. With POP-UP HOOD: If the POP-UP HOOD has been activated, replace either of the upper fender trim and the battery trim with new ones.
  1. Install the upper fender trim in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • If the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Make sure the windshield washer tubes are connected properly.
    • Make sure the rear end of the upper fender trim (A) is installed under the body (B) properly.
    NOTE: Right side is shown, left side is similar.
