Fuel Cap Adapter Replacement
NOTE: Take care not to scratch the body.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Fuel fill door latch actuator
    • Rear inner fender, as needed
    • Fuel filler cap
  1. From under the rear bumper, release the drain tube (A) from the tube clip (B).
  1. Detach the hooks (C, D), then remove the fuel cap adapter (E) with the drain tube from the body.
  1. If necessary, disconnect the drain tube (A) from the fuel cap adapter (B).
  1. Install the fuel cap adapter in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Make sure the drain tube is connected securely.
    • Make sure the fuel cap adapter (A) is fully seated on the fuel fill pipe (B).
    • Pull the lower end of the drain tube (C) down fully, then fasten the tube with the tube clip (D).
      And make sure that water is drained through the tube.
