Interior Trim Removal/Installation - Trunk Lid
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Remove the trunk light.
  1. Pull up on the outside ends of the trunk lid cushions (A), slide them to the outside, and pull them out.
  1. Detach all the clips, then remove the trunk lid trim (A).
  1. If necessary, pivot the trunk lid handle (A) down on the forward hooks (B) while prying the rear hook (C), then remove the handle.
  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
