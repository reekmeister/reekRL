EPS DTC Troubleshooting: 33-01, 33-06
DTC 33-01:
 Lower FET Stuck ON (Initial diagnosis)
DTC 33-06:
 Lower FET Stuck ON (Regular diagnosis)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 33-01 or DTC 33-06 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
The system is OK at this time.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect EPS (LKAS) control unit connector B (2P).
  1. Disconnect EPS (LKAS) control unit connector C (2P).
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector B (2P) terminal No. 1, connector C (2P) terminal No. 1, and connector C (2P) terminal No. 2 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Replace the EPS (LKAS) control unit. ■
  1. Disconnect the motor 1P connector.
  1. Disconnect the motor 2P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector B (2P) terminal No. 1, connector C (2P) terminal No. 1, and connector C (2P) terminal No. 2 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between the EPS (LKAS) control unit and the motor.■
NO -
Replace the motor. The fault is a short in the motor wire harness, or motor internal circuit.■
