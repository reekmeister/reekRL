VSA DTC Troubleshooting: 83
DTC 83:
 PCM Communication
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 86 indicated?
YES -
Do the troubleshooting for DTC 86. ■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Cleat the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then disconnect the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 83 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
The system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures. ■
  1. Check for the fuel and emissions system DTCs with the HDS.
Are any PCM DTCs indicated?
YES -
Do the applicable troubleshooting for the PCM DTCs.
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Check for the automatic transmission system DTCs with the HDS.
Are any PCM DTCs indicated?
YES -
Do the applicable troubleshooting for the PCM DTCs.
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then disconnect the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 83 indicated and no PCM DTCs?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
The system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures. ■
  1. Substitute a known-good PCM, and recheck.
Is the problem gone?
YES -
Replace the original PCM. ■
NO -
Check for loose terminals or poor connections at the VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector. If the connectors are good, replace the VSA modulator-control unit. ■
