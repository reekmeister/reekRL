Rear Door Outer Handle Replacement
NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Door panel
    • Door glass inner weatherstrip
    • Plastic cover
    • Glass
  1. Disconnect the connector (A), and remove the screws (B, C), then lower the latch (D).
  1. Remove the access seal (A). Using a clip remover, disconnect the outer handle rod (B) from the outer handle.
  1. While holding the outer handle cover (A) from outside of the door, loosen the bolt, and then release the hooks (B) of the outer handle cover, then remove the cover and the outer handle holder from the outer handle base (C).
  1. Disconnect the outer handle light connector (A).
  1. Pull the outer handle (A) out, and back as shown to remove it from the door. Take care not to scratch the door.
  1. Release the hooks (A, B, C), then remove the outer handle front seal (D) and the outer handle rear seal (E).
  1. Remove the outer handle base (A).
    -1.
    Loosen the nut.
    -2.
    Release the tab (B), and slide the outer handle base forward to release the special bolt (C) from the door panel.
    -3.
    Detach the connector clip (D) and harness clip (E) from the outer handle base.
  1. Remove the joint bushing (A) from the outer handle (B), then replace it with a new one.
  1. Install the handle in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Reinstall the outer handle holder before installing the outer handle cover.
    • Make sure the outer handle light connector is plugged in properly, and the outer handle rod is connected securely.
    • Make sure the door lock operates properly.
    • Make sure the door handle works properly.
    • When reinstalling the door panel, make sure the plastic cover is installed properly and sealed around its outside perimeter to seal out water.
