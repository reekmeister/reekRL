SH-AWD System Symptom Troubleshooting - The SH-AWD indicator does not go off, and no DTCs are stored
The SH-AWD indicator does not go off, and no DTCs are stored
  1. Check for complete memorization in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
  1. Test the battery . Charge or replace the battery if necessary.
  1. Test the charging system.
Is the charging system OK?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Go to the Charging System Troubleshooting. ■
  1. Check these fuses:
    • No. 22 (70 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box.
    • No. 1 (30 A) fuse in the passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Are fuses OK?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Replace the No. 22 (70 A) fuse and/or the No. 1 (30 A) fuse, and recheck.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check the SH-AWD indicator value with the HDS.
Is the SH-AWD indicator value OFF?
YES -
Go to the gauge control system DTC troubleshooting. ■
NO -
Replace the SH-AWD control unit. ■
