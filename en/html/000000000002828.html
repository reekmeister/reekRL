Rear License Trim Replacement
NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove the trunk lid trim.
  1. Remove the screws from the rear license trim (A).
  1. From inside the trunk lid, remove the bolts (A) and nuts (B) securing the rear license trim and both back-up lights (C).
  1. Remove the back-up or rear fog (if equipped) lights from both sides.
  1. Gently close the trunk lid, and pull the rear license trim (A) out to detach the clips, then remove the trim. Take care not to scratch the trunk lid.
  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • If the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clip into place securely.
