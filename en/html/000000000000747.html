EPS DTC Troubleshooting: 21-01, 21-02
DTC 21-01:
 Excessive Change of the Vehicle Speed Signal (Regular diagnosis)
DTC 21-02:
 Comparison between the Vehicle Speed and the Engine Speed Signal (Regular diagnosis)
NOTE:
  • If the MIL stays on, or the D indicator blinks at the same time as DTC 21-01 or DTC 21-02 is indicated, troubleshoot the PGM-FI system or the automatic transmission system first, then recheck for DTC 21-01 or DTC 22-02.
  • Even through the system is operating normally, the EPS indicator will come on caused by the detecting condition of DTC 21-01 or DTC 21-02.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check the ENGINE SPEED in the EPS DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is 0 rpm indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 20 .
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Raise the vehicle, and support it with safety stands in the proper locations, and allow all wheels to rotate freely.
  1. Start the engine, then run the vehicle in the D position.
  1. Check the VEHICLE SPEED in the EPS DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is 0 km/h (mph) indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the PCM and the EPS (LKAS) control unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Short the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect PCM connector E (31P).
  1. Disconnect the EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between body ground and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 5.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Repair short to power in the wire between the EPS (LKAS) control unit and the PCM.■
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 5.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between the EPS (LKAS) control unit and the PCM.■
NO -
Go to Step 15 .
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector E (31P) terminal No. 11 and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 5.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 16 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the EPS (LKAS) control unit and the PCM.■
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Make sure the driving wheels are stopped, then run the vehicle in the D position.
  1. When the driving wheels are stopped and rotated, measure voltage between body ground and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 5.
    NOTE: When it is a normal, the VSP signal is 0 V−5 V pulses voltage.
Does the measurement voltage change?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the EPS (LKAS) control unit connectors, and repair if necessary. If no poor connections are found, replace the EPS (LKAS) control unit. ■
NO -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the PCM. If necessary, substitute a known-good PCM and recheck.■

  1. Disconnect EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P).
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Measure voltage between body ground and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 3.
