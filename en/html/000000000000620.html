Immobilizer indicator does not come on
NOTE: Before troubleshooting, check the items listed in ‘‘General Check before Troubleshooting''.
  1. Try to start the engine with the immobilizer key.
Does the starter motor run?
YES -
Go to Step 2 .
NO -
Go to engine does not start with the immobilizer troubleshooting. ■
  1. Check the No. 7 (10 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Replace the blown fuse and recheck the system. If the sympton goes away, repair a short to ground in the WHT [LT GRN] wire.■

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the immobilizer control unit-receiver 7P connector.
  1. Connect immobilizer control unit-receiver 7P connector terminal No. 5 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check the immobilizer indicator operation.
Is the immobilizer indicator come on?
YES -
Check for loose or poor connections at the immobilizer control unit-receiver connector. If the connections are good, replace the immobilizer control unit-receiver.■
