Hands Free Telephone System Symptom Troubleshooting - Connection guidance is not displayed on the screen
Connection guidance is not displayed on the screen
NOTE:
  • Check the vehicle battery condition first.
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  • Some mobile phones do not support the function.
  • Check that the connection guidance has been displayed at the time of initial registration.

  1. Check if any system(s) other than Bluetooth audio system is displayed.
