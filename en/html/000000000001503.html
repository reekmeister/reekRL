ATF Cooler Hose Replacement
  1. Slide the ATF cooler outlet hose (A) over the ATF cooler line (B) until the hose end contacts with the bulge (C) of the ATF cooler line. Align the dot (D) on the hose end with the dot (E) on the cooler line, and secure the hose with the clip (F) at 6−8 mm (0.2−0.3 in.) (G) from the hose end.
  1. Slide the ATF cooler outlet hose (H) over the joint line (I) until the hose end contacts with the bulge (J) of the joint line. Face down the dot (K) on the hose end, and secure the hose with the clip (L) at 6−8 mm (0.2−0.3 in.) (M) from the hose end.
  1. Slide the ATF cooler hoses (N) over the ATF cooler lines (O) until the hose ends contact with the bulge (P) of the ATF cooler line, and secure the hose with the clips (Q) at 6−8 mm (0.2−0.3 in.) (R) from the hose end.
  1. Slide the ATF cooler hoses (S) over the ATF cooler lines (T) until the hose ends contact to the red line (U), and secure the hoses at the ATF cooler with the clips (V) at 2−4 mm (0.1−0.2 in.) (W) from the hose ends.
