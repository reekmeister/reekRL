DTC Troubleshooting: B1835
DTC B1835:
 Tilt/Telescopic Switch Signal Input Error
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Operate the tilt/telescopic switch in each direction for more than 3 seconds each.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1835 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The power tilt/telescopic steering system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at the tilt/telescopic switch connector and at the power tilt/telescopic control unit connectors.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Test the tilt/telescopic switch.
Is the switch OK?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Replace the switch.■
  1. Disconnect power tilt/telescopic steering control unit connector A (16P).
  1. Disconnect the tilt/telescopic switch 13P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and tilt/telescopic switch 13P connector terminals No. 1, No. 7, and No. 10 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short in the wire.■
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Check for continuity between power tilt/telescopic steering control unit connector A (16P) terminals No. 7, No. 6, and No. 14 and the tilt/telescopic switch 13P connector terminals No. 1, No. 7, and No. 10 respectively.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to the power tilt/telescopic steering control unit input test.
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
