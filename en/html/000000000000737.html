DTC Troubleshooting: B1279
DTC B1279:
 Headlight Switch DIMMER Position Circuit Malfunction
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Turn the headlight switch ON.
  1. Change the DIMMER switch from low beam to high beam and wait for 2 seconds.
  1. Pull and hold the combination light switch lever, and hold the passing switch position for more than 2 seconds.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1279 indicated?
YES -
  • LHD type and KE model: Go to Step 6 .
  • RHD type except KE model: Faulty combination switch control unit; replace the combination light switch.■
NO -
Intermittent failure, the combination switch and the combination switch control unit are OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections, between the combination light switch 8P connector, and the wiper/washer switch 12P connector (LHD type).■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the combination light switch 12P connector from the wiper/washer switch.
  1. Do the combination light switch test.
Is the combination light switch OK?
YES -
Faulty combination switch control unit , replace the wiper/washer switch.■
NO -
Replace the combination light switch.■
