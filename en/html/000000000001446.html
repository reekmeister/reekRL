Injector Test
NOTE: Check the following items before testing: idle speed, ignition timing and idle CO %.
  1. Try to start the engine.
    • If the engine starts, go to Step 2 .
    • If the engine does not start, go to Step 6 .
  1. Select ONE INJECTOR in INSPECTON menu with the HDS.
  1. Disable each injector at a time by selecting number of a injector which you want to stop.
  1. Inspect the change in the idle speed.
    • If the idle speed drop is almost the same for each cylinder, the fuel injectors are normal.
    • If the idle speed or quality remains the same when you disable a particular injector, faulty injector is suspected. Replace the injector and retest.
  1. Check the clicking sound of each fuel injector by means of a stethoscope when the engine is idling.
    • If any injector fails to make the typical clicking sound, check the sound again after replacing the injector.
    • If clicking sound is still absent, check the following.
      • -
      Whether there is wire breakage or poor connection in the YEL/BLK wire between the PGM-FI main relay 1 (FI MAIN) and the 24P junction connector.
      -
      Whether the 24P junction connector is open or corroded.
      -
      Whether there is wire breakage or poor connection in the YEL/BLK wire between the 24P junction connector and the injector.
      -
      Whether there is any short-circuiting, wire breakage or poor connection in the wire between the fuel injector and the PCM.
    • If all is OK, the test is complete.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the intake manifold.
  1. Remove the injector connector.
  1. Check that the resistance between injector (A) terminals No. 1 and No. 2 is between 10−13 Ω.
    • If the measurment is between specification, go to Step 10 .
    • If the measurment is out of specification, replace the injector.
  1. Check the fuel pressure.
    • If the fuel pressure is as specified, check the following:
      • -
      Whether there is wire breakage, or poor connection in the YEL/BLK wire between the PGM-FI main relay 1 (FI MAIN) and the 24P junction connector.
      -
      Whether the 24P junction connector is open or corroded.
      -
      Whether there is wire breakage, or poor connection in the YEL/BLK wire between the 24P junction connector and the injector.
      -
      Whether there is any short-circuiting, wire breakage or poor connection in the wire between the injector and the PCM.
    • If the fuel pressure is not as specified, recheck the fuel pressure.
