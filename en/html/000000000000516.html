Instrument Fascia Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and panels.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Tilt the steering column down, and telescope it out.
  1. Remove the instrument fascia (A).
    -1.
    Gently pull out the bottom to release the clips (B, C).
    -2.
    Disconnect the illumination switch connector (D) and CMBS OFF (for some models)/VSA OFF/AFS OFF switch connector (E).
  1. Install the instrument fascia in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
