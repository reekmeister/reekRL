SRS DTC Troubleshooting: 25-9x
DTC 25-9x (25-90 to 25-99, 25-9A to 25-9F):
 Short to Ground in Left Side Seat Belt Outer Lap Tensioner (LHD type)
Special Tools Required

SRS inflator simulator
 07SAZ-TB4011A

SRS simulator lead M
 070AZ-SNA0400
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and check that the SRS indicator comes on for about 6 seconds and then goes off.
Does the SRS indicator stay on, and is DTC 25-9x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index for troubleshooting details.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect the left side wire harness 2P connector (A) from the left side seat belt outer lap tensioner.
  1. Connect the SRS inflator simulator (2 Ω connector) and simulator lead M to the left side wire harness.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 25-9x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Short to ground in the left side seat belt outer lap tensioner; replace the left side seat belt. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect the both seat belt tensioner 4P connectors.
  1. Disconnect SRS unit connector B (28P) from the SRS unit.
  1. Disconnect the SRS inflator simulator from the SRS simulator lead.
  1. Check resistance between each terminal of the SRS simulator lead and body ground. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Faulty SRS unit; replace the SRS unit. ■
NO -
Go to Step 14 .
  1. Disconnect right side wire harness 16P connector A from left side wire harness connector B.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 13 terminal of the right side wire harness 16P connector and body ground, and between the No. 14 terminal and body ground. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Short to ground in the left side wire harness; replace the left side wire harness.■
NO -
Short to ground in the right side wire harness; replace the right side wire harness.■
