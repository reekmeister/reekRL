Glove Box Lock Cylinder Replacement
NOTE: Take care not to scratch the glove box.
  1. Remove the glove box.
  1. Remove the document tray.
  1. Remove the screw securing the glove box damper wire to the glove box.
  1. Remove the spring pins (A) from both sides, then remove the glove box lid (B) from the glove box housing (C).
  1. Remove the screws, and release the hooks (A) from both sides, then remove the glove box front trim (B) from the glove box (C).
  1. Remove the glove box lock knob body (A) by removing the screws. Disconnect the pin (B) from the link.
  1. Using a flat-tip screwdriver, pry up the retainer (A), then remove the glove box lock cylinder (B).
    NOTE: Wear eye protection when doing this step.
  1. Set the retainer (A) into the lock knob body, then push the lock cylinder into the lock knob body until it snaps into the retainer.
  1. Install the lock knob body in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Before installing the lock knob body to the glove box, make sure the lock cylinder operates properly.
    • Replace the spring pins with new ones.
    • Before installing the spring pins, apply grease to them.
