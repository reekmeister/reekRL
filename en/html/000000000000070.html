Crankshaft Oil Seal (Transmission End) Installation - In Car
Special Tools Required

Handle driver
 07749-0010000

Driver attachment, 106 mm
 070AD-RCA0200
  1. Remove the transmission and the drive plate.
  1. Remove the transmission end crankshaft oil seal.
  1. Clean and dry the crankshaft oil seal housing.
  1. Apply a light coat of multipurpose grease to the crankshaft and to the lip of the seal.
  1. Using the special tools, drive in the crankshaft oil seal until the driver attachment bottoms against the engine block end cover. Align the hole in the driver attachment with the pin on the crankshaft.
  1. Clean any excess grease off the crankshaft, and check that the oil seal lip is not distorted.
  1. Install the drive plate, and the transmission.
