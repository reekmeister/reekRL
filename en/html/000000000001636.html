Front Air Spoiler Replacement - With POP-UP HOOD System
With POP-UP HOOD
NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the front bumper.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove the bolts (A) and clips (B, C) from under the front bumper (D).
  1. Pull the front air spoiler (E) out to release the hooks (F), then remove the spoiler.
  1. Install the spoiler in the reverse order of removal. Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
