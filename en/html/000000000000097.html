Rear Damper Replacement

Exploded View
Removal
  1. Raise the rear of the vehicle, and support it with safety stands in the proper locations.
  1. Remove the rear wheel.
  1. Place a floor jack at the connecting point of lower arm B and the stabilizer link to support them.
  1. Disconnect the headlight leveling sensor linkage (A) from the damper.
  1. Remove the rear shelf.
  1. Remove the two flange nuts (A).
  1. Remove the damper lower mounting bolt (A) from the knuckle.
  1. Lower the rear suspension, then remove the damper (A) from the vehicle.
Inspection
  1. Disassemble the damper and check the all parts as shown in the Exploded View.
  1. Install the damper mounting base, washer, and 10 mm nut to the damper unit.
  1. Push on the damper by hand as shown.
  1. Compress the damper assembly by hand, and check for smooth operation through a full stroke, both compression and extension. The damper should extend in a smooth, continuous motion when the compression is released. If it does not (no compression or no extension), the gas is leaking, and the damper should be replaced.
  1. Check for oil leaks, abnormal noises, or binding during these tests.
Installation
  1. Place a floor jack at the connecting point of lower arm B and the stabilizer link.
  1. Compress the damper (A) by hand, and move it into position.
  1. Loosely tighten the new damper lower mounting bolt (A).
  1. Loosely install the new flange nuts.
  1. Raise the rear suspension with a floor jack to load it with the vehicle's weight.
  1. Tighten the flange nuts and the damper lower mounting bolt to the specified torque value.
  1. Install the rear shelf.
  1. Connect the headlight leveling sensor linkage (A) to the damper with the self-locking nut (B).
  1. Clean the mating surface of the brake disc/drum and the inside of the wheel, then install the rear wheel.
  1. Check the rear wheel alignment, and adjust it if necessary.
  1. Do the adaptive front lighting control unit learning procedure.
