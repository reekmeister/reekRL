SH-AWD DTC Troubleshooting: 53-1, 53-3
DTC 53-1, 53-3:
 Left Clutch Electromagnetic Coil (Open)
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Make sure the HDS communicates with vehicle and the SH-AWD control unit. If it doesn't, troubleshoot the DLC circuit.
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Do the direct electromagnetic clutch test.
    • With the brake tester.
    • With the torque wrench.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 53-1 or 53-3 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect SH-AWD control unit connector B (8P).
  1. Disconnect the left clutch electromagnetic coil 2P connector.
  1. Connect between the left clutch electromagnetic coil 2P connector terminal No. 1 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between SH-AWD control unit connector terminal B5 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Repair open in the wires between the SH-AWD control unit and the left clutch electromagnetic coil.■
  1. Remove the jumper wire from the left clutch electromagnetic coil 2P connector terminal No. 1.
  1. Connect between the left clutch electromagnetic coil 2P connector terminal No. 2 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between SH-AWD control unit connector terminal B4 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Repair open in the wires between the SH-AWD control unit and the left clutch electromagnetic coil.■
  1. Remove the jumper wire from the left clutch electromagnetic coil 2P connector terminal No. 2.
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Check the REAR DIFFERENTIAL FLUID TEMPERATURE in the DATA LIST is above 20 °C (68 °F) with the HDS before proceeding to the next step. If the temperature is below 20 °C (68 °F), drive the vehicle to warm up the rear differential until the fluid temperature becomes above 20 °C (68 °F).
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect the left clutch electromagnetic coil 2P connector.
  1. Measure resistance between the left clutch electromagnetic coil 2P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 2.
Is there 1.5 Ω or more?
YES -
Replace the rear differential side case. ■
NO -
Substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
