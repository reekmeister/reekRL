Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) DTC Troubleshooting: 44
Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) unit DTC 44:
Cal FL Brake Fluid Pressure Sensor Circuit Open/Short
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Watch the adaptive cruise control (ACC)/LKAS indicator.
Does the adaptive cruise control (ACC)/LKAS indicator stay off?
YES -
Intermittent failure. The system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 44 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Troubleshoot the indicated DTC.■
  1. Check the left front brake fluid pressure voltage (VPRFL) with the HDS.
Is there about 0.5 V?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Do the brake booster inspection.
  1. Press the brake pedal with about 314 N (32 kgf, 71 lbf) force.
  1. Check the left front brake fluid pressure voltage (VPRFL) with the HDS.
Is there about 2.5 V?
YES -
The left front brake fluid pressure signal is OK, check for loose or poor connections. If all connections are OK, replace the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit. ■
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 20P connector from the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit.
  1. Check for continuity between the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit 20P connector No. 5 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire between the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit and the left front pressure sensor.■
NO -
Go to Step 11 .
  1. Disconnect the 3P connector from the left front pressure sensor.
  1. Connect the left front pressure sensor 3P connector No. 2 terminal to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit 20P connector No. 5 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire between the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit and the left front pressure sensor.■
  1. Remove the jumper wire from the left front pressure sensor.
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit 20P connector No. 3 and No. 5 terminals.
Is there about 5 V?
YES -
Go to Step 18 .
NO -
Check for loose or poor connections. If all connections are OK, replace the adaptive cruise control (ACC) unit. ■
