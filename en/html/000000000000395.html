Speaker Replacement
Front Door Speaker
  1. Remove the front door panel.
  1. Pull the speaker (A) straight out, just enough to release the upper clips. Then lift the speaker straight up to release the lower clips (B).
    If you pull the speaker out too far from the door, you will damage the lower clips.
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (C), and remove the speaker.
  1. Install the speaker in the reverse order of removal.
Rear Door Speaker
  1. Remove the rear door panel.
  1. Remove the three mounting screws from the rear speaker (A).
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (B), and remove the rear door speaker.
  1. Install the speaker in the reverse order of removal.
Front Door Tweeter
  1. Remove the front door sash inner trim.
  1. Remove the screws and tweeter speaker (A) from the quater inner trim (B).
  1. Install the speaker in the reverse order of removal.
Rear Twiddler
  1. Remove the rear shelf.
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (A), then remove the connector (B) from the rear twiddler bracket (C).
  1. Remove the screws and the rear twiddler (D).
  1. Install the speaker in the reverse order of removal.
Front Center Speaker
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not scratch the dashboard.
  1. Remove the dashboard upper visor (A).
  1. Disconnect the connectors (B).
  1. Remove screws, connector (A), and the front center speaker (B).
  1. Install the speaker in the reverse order of removal.
Subwoofer
  1. Remove the rear shelf and rear shelf under cover.
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector (A), then remove connector clip (B) from the subwoofer duct (C).
  1. Remove the nuts and the subwoofer (D).
  1. Install the speaker in the reverse order of removal.
