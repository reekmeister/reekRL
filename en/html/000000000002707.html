A/T Shift Lever Boot and A/T Gear Position Indicator Panel Base Replacement
  1. Remove the center console panel.
  1. Remove the shift lever knob.
  1. Remove the A/T gear position indicator panel light bulb socket from the indicator panel, and remove the indicator panel/shift lever boot assembly.
  1. Disassemble the indicator panel upper (A), lower (B), and boot (C).
  1. Replace the shift lever boot or A/T gear position indicator panel.
  1. Install the shift lever boot (A) on the A/T gear position indicator lower panel (B) with aligning the corner slots (C) with the tabs (D) on the lower panel.
  1. Install the upper panel on the lower panel.
  1. Install the indicator panel/boot assembly on the shift lever bracket.
  1. Install the A/T gear position indicator panel light bulb socket in the indicator panel.
  1. Install the shift lever knob.
  1. Install the center console panel.
