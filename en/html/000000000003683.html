Navigation System Symptom Troubleshooting - Picture has lines or rolls
Picture has lines or rolls
Diagnostic Test: Monitor Check
NOTE:
  • Check the vehicle battery condition first.
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  • Always compare the screen image to a known-good vehicle. If the screen looks the same, inform the customer that it is a characteristic of the system.
  • Make sure that the correct version DVD is installed.
  • Inspect the DVD for dirt or damage.
  • Check any official Honda service website for more service information about the navigation system.
  • Check the navigation screen settings for brightness, contrast, and black level, and check the color screen for map color and menu color.
  1. Check for electronic aftermarket accessories (possibly hidden) mounted near the navigation display unit or the navigation unit.
Are there any electronic accessories?
YES -
Disable the accessories, and recheck.■
NO -
Go to Step 2 .
  1. Start up the navigation screen.
Is the picture scrolling horizontally (left to right or right to left)?
YES -
Check for an open or short to ground in the C SIG wire from navigation unit connector B (32P) No. 18 terminal to display unit 20P connector No. 19 terminal. Also check for a short to ground between display unit 20P connector No. 19 and No. 20 terminals. If the wire is OK, substitute a known-good navigation unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original navigation unit. If the symptom/indication does not go away, replace the display unit. ■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Go into the Detail Information & Settings select Monitor Check, and use RGB Color diagnostic under Monitor Check.
Is the picture missing a red, green or blue color?
YES -
Do troubleshooting for the picture is missing a color or tone or is an odd color. ■
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Substitute a known-good display unit, and recheck.
Is the picture OK?
YES -
Check for loose connections, then replace the original display unit. ■
NO -
Check for loose connections and recheck. If a poor connection or loose terminal is found, replace the shielded harness and recheck. If the problem is gone, replace the original display unit. ■
