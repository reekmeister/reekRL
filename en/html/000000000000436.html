Fuel Fill Door Latch/Actuator Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the rear bumper.
  1. Remove the left trunk side trim panel.
  1. Remove the fuel door latch/actuator (A).
    -1.
    Remove the fuel door latch by turning it 90 ° from the body.
    -2.
    Disconnect the actuator connector (B).
    -3.
    Loosen the screws, then remove the actuator (C) by sliding it forward.
  1. Install the rear fuel door latch/actuator in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply multipurpose grease to the hood latch as indicated by the arrows.
    • Make sure the door opens properly and locks securely.
