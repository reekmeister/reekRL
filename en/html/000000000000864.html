DTC Troubleshooting: B1886
DTC B1886:
 Power Trunk Sensor Pulse A Circuit Malfunction
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Open the trunk lid fully.
  1. Manually close the trunk lid fully.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1886 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The power trunk system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at power trunk control unit connectors and at drive unit connector.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Connect the voltmeter to the power trunk control unit connector B (20P) No. 14 and No. 18 terminals.
  1. While the trunk lid is opening and closing maunally, measure voltage between the power trunk control unit connector B (20P) No. 14 and No. 18 terminals.
Does it alternate between about 0 V and 5 V (Digital voltmeter will read about 2.5 V)?
YES -
Replace the power trunk control unit .■
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Disconnect the 20P and 14P connectors from the power trunk control unit.
  1. Disconnect the 6P connector from the drive unit.
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector B (20P) No. 14 terminal and connector C (14P) No. 13 terminal and body ground individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire.■
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector B (20P) No. 14 and No. 18 terminals and drive unit connector A (6P) No. 5 and No. 6 terminals.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Faulty power trunk sensor A, replace the drive unit.■
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
