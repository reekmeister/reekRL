Multi-information Display Select/Reset/Information Switch Test/Replacement
  1. Remove the driver's airbag assembly.
  1. Disconnect the 6P connector (A) from the select/reset/information switch (B).
  1. Measure resistance between the select/reset/information switch 6P connector No. 6 and No. 5 terminals in each switch position according to the table.
    Position
    Resistance
    ▲
    About 33 Ω
    SEL/RESET
    About 1.043 Ω
    ▼
    About 363 Ω

  1. If the resistance is not as specified, remove the two screws and replace the switch.
  1. If the switch is OK, but there is a problem with the switch function, check the steering switches harness and cable reel.
