Trunk Lid Opener Cable Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the body and related parts.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Spare tyre lid
    • Trunk rear trim panel
    • Trunk front trim panel
    • Trunk side trim panel, right side
    • Trunk upper trim panel
  1. Disconnect the trunk lid opener cable from the trunk lid latch.
  1. Remove the trunk lid opener cable (A) and the clip (B) from the opener cable bracket (C).
  1. Use a clip remover to release the clip (D) (except KE model). Remove the cushion tapes (E).
  1. Remove the trunk lid opener cable from the vehicle. Take care not to kink the cable.
Except KE model
KE model
  1. Install the opener cable in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Align the marks (A) on the opener cable (B) with the cable clip (C) and cushion tape (D) as shown.
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • KE model: When installing the right trunk side trim panel (E) and trunk rear trim panel (F), pass the trunk lid opener cable through the inside from the hook (G) of the trunk rear trim panel as shown.
