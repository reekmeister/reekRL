SRS DTC Troubleshooting: 62-11
DTC 62-11:
 Open in Right Side Seat Belt Buckle Switch (Except KD model)
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), then buckle and unbuckle the left side seat belt several times.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 62-11 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Install a jumper wire between the No. 15 terminal of SRS unit connector B (28P) and body ground.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 62-21 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Faulty SRS unit or poor connection at SRS unit connector B (28P) and the SRS unit. Check the connection between the connector and the SRS unit. If the connection is OK, replace the SRS unit. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove a jumper wire from SRS unit connector B (28P).
  1. Disconnect passenger's (LHD type) or driver's (RHD type) seat wire harness 4P connector from the right side seat belt buckle switch 4P connector (A).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 4 terminal of right side seat belt buckle switch and body ground. There should be more than 10 V.
Is the voltage as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Open in the passenger's (LHD type) or driver's (RHD type) seat wire harness, or right side wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Check resistance between the No. 1 terminal of the seat wire harness 4P connector and body ground, and between the No. 3 terminal and body ground. There should be 0−1 Ω.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Replace the right side seat belt buckle assembly, then clear the DTC.■
NO -
Open in the passenger's (LHD type) or driver's (RHD type) seat wire harness, right side wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
