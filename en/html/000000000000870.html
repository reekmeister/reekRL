POP-UP HOOD System DTC Troubleshooting: 11-1x, 11-2x
DTC 11-1x (11-10 to 11-19, 11-1A to 11-1F):
Open in Left Side Power Unit Component
DTC 11-2x (11-20 to 11-29, 11-2A to 11-2F):  
Increased Resistance in Left Side Power Unit Component
Special Tools Required

SRS simulator lead E
 07XAZ-S1A0200
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 11-1x or 11-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the both power unit component 2P connectors.
  1. Connect the jumper wire and simulator lead E to the engine compartment wire harness 2P connector.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 11-1x or 11-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Faulty left side power unit component; replace the left side power unit.■
  1. Disconnect the right side (LHD type) or left side (RHD type) wire harness 16P connector (A) from engine compartment wire harness connector (B).

RHD type

    []: RHD type
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 11-1x or 11-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Open in the engine compartment wire harness; replace the engine compartment wire harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector from the POP-UP HOOD unit.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 12 terminals and No. 13 terminal of the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector. There should be less than 1.0 Ω.
Is the resistances specified?
YES -
Faulty POP-UP HOOD unit or poor connection at POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector and the POP-UP HOOD unit. Check the connection between the connector and the POP-UP HOOD unit. If the connection is OK, replace the POP-UP HOOD unit. ■
NO -
Open in the right side (LHD type) or left side (RHD type) wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
