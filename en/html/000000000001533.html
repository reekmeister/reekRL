A/T Differential Carrier Bearing Preload Inspection
Special Tools Required

Handle driver
 07749-0010000

Driver attachment, 83 mm
 07HAD-SG00100

Preload inspection tool
 070AJ-0020101
NOTE: If the transmission housing, torque converter housing, differential carrier, tapered roller bearing, bearing outer race, or thrust shim were replaced, the bearing preload must be adjusted.
  1. Remove the bearing outer race (A), spacer (B), and 85 mm thrust shim (C) from the transmission housing (D) by heating the housing to about 100 °C (212 °F) with a heat gun (E). Do not heat the housing more than 100 °C (212 °F).
    NOTE: Let the transmission housing cool to room temperature before adjusting the bearing preload.
  1. Replace the tapered roller bearing when the outer race is to be replaced.
  1. Do not use a shim on the torque converter housing side.
  1. Install the 85 mm thrust shim (A) in the transmission housing (B). If you replace the 85 mm thrust shim with a new one, use the same thickness shim as the old one.
  1. Install the spacer (C), and the bearing outer race (D) in the transmission housing.
  1. Drive the outer race securely in the housing with the special tools so there is no clearance between the outer race, spacer, shim, and housing.
  1. Install the differential assembly (A) in the torque converter housing (B), and install the gasket (C) and the two dowel pins (D) on the housing.
  1. Install the transmission housing (E), and install the mounting bolts (24 bolts) with the transmission hangers (F) and the ground terminal bracket (G), then tighten the bolts.
  1. Rotate the differential assembly in both directions to seat the bearings.
  1. Measure the starting torque of the differential assembly with the special tool, a torque wrench (A), and a socket (B). Measure the starting torque at normal room temperature in both directions.
    Standard
    New bearings:
    3.9−5.1 N·m (40−52 kgf·cm, 35−45 lbf·in.)
    Reused bearings:
    3.6−4.8 N·m (37−49 kgf·cm, 32−43 lbf·in.)

  1. If the measurement is out of standard, remove the thrust shim and measure its thickness.
  1. Select a new thrust shim. To increase the starting torque, increase thickness of the thrust shim. To decrease the starting torque, decrease the thickness of the thrust shim.
    Changing the shim to the next size will increase or decrease the starting torque about 0.5−0.6 N·m (5−6 kgf·cm, 5−5 lbf·in.).
