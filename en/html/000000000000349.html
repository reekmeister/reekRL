POP-UP HOOD Power Unit Component Disposal
Deploying Components Out of the Vehicle
Special Tools Required

Deployment tool
 07HAZ-SG00500
Before scrapping the power unit component (including those in a whole vehicle to be scrapped), the part(s) must be deployed. If the vehicle is still within the warranty period, the Honda District Manager must give approval and/or special instruction before deploying the part(s). Only after the part(s) have been deployed (as the result of vehicle collision, for example), can they be scrapped. If the parts appear intact (not deployed), treat them with extreme caution. Follow this procedure.
Things to prepare:
  • 3 tyres with a diameter of at least 15 mm, and 2 wheel and tire combination (the wheel and tire combination should have the same diameter as the other 3 tires)
  • Automobile wire harness (core cross-sectional area of 1.25 mm 2 or more) or wire (used for fixing the tyre) with a diameter of 1.2 mm or more
  • 12V battery
If using a deployment tool
  • Deployment tool
If not using a deployment tool
  • Wire harness 12-15 m
  • Insulating tape
  1. Make a set comprising of 2 tyres (A). Make another set composed of a wheel-and-tyre combination (B) and a tyre (C). Bind each set twice with automobile wire harness (core cross-sectional area of 1.25 mm 2 or more) or wire (D) with a diameter of 1.2 mm or more.
  1. Insert the power unit component to be deployed, to the wheel hole of the wheel and tyre combination. Fix in place by binding at least three times with an automobile wire harness or wire.
  1. Pile the two sets of tyres on a flat surface. Put the wheel and tyre combination containing the power unit component top of this set of tyres.
  1. Cut the harness of the power unit component on the part near the connector. Strip the insulation about 15mm from the end and twist together the wires with the removed insulation.

With deployment tool

  1. Unknot the twisted wire of the power unit component, and then attach an alligator clip (A) of the deployment tool to each wire. Cover with insulating tape.

Without deployment tool

  1. Unknot the twisted wires of the power unit component, and then connect the parts unconnected to electric wires to the power unit component. Cover with insulating tape.
  1. Pile the set containing the wheel-and-tyre combination over the combination with the power unit component, and tie them together with the set of tyres beneath them.
    NOTE:
    • Do not pass the cord or wire of the deployment tool through the piston or inflator body of the power unit component.
    • Make sure that the exposed side of the rod faces downward. Pile the tyres.
    • Set up the battery at least 10 meters (30 feet) away from the tyres.

With deployment tool

  1. Connect an alligator clip to the 12 V battery terminal. When the red lamp illuminates, press the switch, and deploy the power unit component.

Without deployment tool

  1. Unknot the twisted wires at the battery side, and then connect to the battery terminal. Deploy the power unit component.
  1. After performing the procedure let sit for 30 minutes, and them cool. Dispose of the complete power unit component. No part of it can be reused. Place it in a sturdy plastic bag (A), and seal it securely. Dispose of deployed power unit component according to your local regulations.
Deploying components in the vehicle
NOTE: If components have deployed while the hood was open, the hood cannot be closed later. Also, the hood may detach and fly up, so be sure to close the hood before performing the procedure.
Things to prepare:
  • 12V battery
If using a deployment tool
  • Deployment tool
If not using deployment tool
  • Wire harness 12−15m
  • Insulating tape
  1. Disconnect negative cable from the battery.
  1. Close the hood.
  1. Disconnect the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector from the POP-UP HOOD unit.
  1. Cut the No. 10 terminal LT GRN wire, No. 11 terminal LT BLU wire, No. 12 terminal GRN wire and No. 13 terminal BLU wire of the POP-UP HOOD unit connector on the part near the connector. Strip the insulation about 30 mm from the end and twist together the wires with the insulation removed.

With deployment tool

  1. Unknot the connection in the harness. Pair and connect LT GRN wire with GRN wire; and the LT BLU wire with BLU wire. Connect an alligator clip to each pair, and cover with insulating tape.
  1. Make sure that the hood is closed. Connect an alligator clip (B) to the 12V battery terminal (A). When the red lamp illuminates, press the switch, and deploy the power unit component.

Without deployment tool

  1. Unknot the connection in the harness. Connect the non-twisted ends of the LT GRN wire, GRN wire, and electrical wire. Also, connect the non-twisted ends of the LT BLU wire, BLU wire, and electrical wire. Cover with insulating tape.
  1. Make sure the hood is closed. Unknot the connection at the other end of the electrical wire (A), and connect it to battery terminal (B) to deploy the power unit component.
  1. After performing the procedure let sit for 30 minutes, and then cool. When moving the vehicle, remove the hood, and attach the battery cable.
  1. Scrap the vehicle with the power unit component attached.
Deployment Tool Check
  1. Connect the yellow clips to both switch protector handles on the tool; connect the tool to a battery.
  1. Push the operation switch: green means the tool is OK; red means the tool is faulty.
  1. Disconnect the yellow clips from the battery.
