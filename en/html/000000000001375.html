Timing Belt Removal
  1. Remove the right upper fender trim.
  1. Turn the crankshaft so its white mark (A) lines up with the pointer (B).
  1. Check that the No. 1 piston top dead center (TDC) mark (A) on the front camshaft pulley and the pointer (B) on the front upper cover are aligned.
  1. Remove the right front wheel.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Remove the drive belt auto-tensioner.
  1. Support the engine with a jack and wood block under the oil pan.
  1. Remove the ground cable (A), then remove the side engine mount bracket (B).
  1. Remove the front upper cover (A) and rear upper cover (B).
  1. Remove the crankshaft pulley.
  1. Remove the lower cover.
  1. Remove one of the battery clamp bolts from the battery tray, and grind the end of it as shown.
  1. Screw the battery clamp bolt in as shown to hold the timing belt adjuster in its current position. Tighten it by hand; do not use a wrench.
  1. Remove the engine mount bracket.
  1. Remove the idler pulley bolt (A) and idler pulley (B), then remove the timing belt. Discard the idler pulley bolt.
