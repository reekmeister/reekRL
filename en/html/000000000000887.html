POP-UP HOOD System DTC Troubleshooting: A1-1x
DTC A1-1x (A1-10 to A1-19, A1-1A to A1-1F):
 Faulty Power Supply (VA line)
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC A1-1x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the No.31 (7.5 A) fuse.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Replace the No.31 (7.5 A) fuse. If problem is still present, check for short to ground of the VA line.■
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector from the POP-UP HOOD unit.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 2 terminal of POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector and body ground. There should be battery voltage.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Faulty POP-UP HOOD unit or poor connection at POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector and the POP-UP HOOD unit. Check the connection between the connector and the POP-UP HOOD unit. If the connection is OK, replace the POP-UP HOOD unit. ■
NO -
Go to Step 11 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connector X (39P).
  1. Check resistance between the No. 2 terminal of POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector and No. 16 terminal of driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connector X 39P. There should be less than 1.0 Ω.
Is the resistances specified?
YES -
Faulty driver's under-dash fuse/relay box or poor connection between connector X (39P) and the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box; check the connection. If the connection is OK, replace the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.■
NO -
Open in the right side wire harness (LHD type), or left side wire harness (RHD type), or dashboard wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
