Fuel Tank Replacement
KD model
  1. Drain the fuel tank.
  1. Reinstall the fuel tank unit and secondary fuel gauge sending unit without connecting the fuel tank unit 5P connector, the secondary fuel gauge sending unit 5P connector and the quick-connect fitting.
  1. Remove the propeller shaft.
  1. Remove the rear differential.
  1. Lift up the vehicle, and support it with jackstands.
  1. Remove the fuel tank covers (A) and parking brake cable bolts (B).
KD model
  1. Drain the fuel tank.
  1. Reinstall the fuel tank unit and secondary fuel gauge sending unit without connecting the fuel tank unit 5P connector, the secondary fuel gauge sending unit 5P connector and the quick-connect fitting.
  1. Remove the propeller shaft.
  1. Remove the rear differential.
  1. Lift up the vehicle, and support it with jackstands.
  1. Remove the fuel tank covers (A) and parking brake cable bolts (B).
  1. Remove the clip (A) from the EVAP canister bracket.
  1. Disconnect the hoses (B). Slide back the clamps, then twist the hoses as you pull to avoid damaging them.
  1. Place a jack, or other support, under the fuel tank (C).
  1. Remove the strap bolts and straps (D).
  1. Remove the fuel tank. If it sticks to the undercoat on its mount, carefully pry it off the mount.
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:
    • New fuel vent hose and fuel vent return hose have ring pull (E) at the connector. When you connect the hose and confirm that the connection is secure, remove the ring pull by pulling it down.
    • Before connecting the fuel fill pipe and the quick-connect fitting, check for dart, and clean it if needed, talking care no to damage the fuel fill pipe and other parts.
