Immobilizer Control Unit-Receiver Replacement
NOTE: All registerd keys are necessary for replacement of the immobilizer control unit-receiver.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard lower cover.
  1. Remove the steering column covers.
  1. Disconnect the 7P connector (A) from the immobilizer control unit-receiver (B).
  1. Remove the two screws and the immobilizer control unit-receiver from the ignition key cylinder (C).
  1. Install the immobilizer control unit-receiver in the reverse order of removal.
  1. After replacement, turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Connect the HDS to the data link connector.
  1. Select ‘‘IMMOBI'' from the ‘‘System Selection Menu''.
  1. Select ‘‘Immobilizer Setup'' from the ‘‘Mode Menu''.
  1. Select ‘‘Replace Imm Unit''.
  1. Operate according to instructions of a screen of HDS.
  1. After HDS operation, check the immobilizer system.
