MIL Circuit Troubleshooting
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Do the gauge self-diagnostic function.
Does the MIL indicator flash?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Substitute a known-good gauge control module, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good gauge control module, replace the original gauge control module. ■
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC.
  1. Check the SCS in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is a short indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good PCM, replace the original PCM. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect PCM connector E (31P), then disconnect the HDS.
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminal E16 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between the PCM (E16) and the DLC.■
NO -
Update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good PCM, replace the original PCM. ■
