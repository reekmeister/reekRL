ATF Cooler Replacement
For KD Model
  1. Disconnect the ATF cooler hose from the ATF cooler, and remove the ATF cooler and line.
  1. Install the new ATF cooler (A) on the body.
  1. Connect the ATF cooler hose (B) to the ATF cooler line, and secure the hose with the clip. Slide the hose over the joint line (C) until the hose end contacts with the bulge (D), and secure the hose with the clip (E) at 6−8 mm (0.2−0.3 in.) (F) from the hose end.
  1. Install the joint line on the body.
  1. Slide the ATF cooler outlet hose (G) over the joint line until the hose end contacts with the bulge (H) of the joint line. Face down the dot (I) on the hose end, and secure the hose with the clip (J) at 6−8 mm (0.2−0.3 in.) (K) from the hose end.
  1. Connect the hose (L) to the ATF cooler line, and secure the hose with the clip.
  1. Install the cooler hose on the clamp (M).
