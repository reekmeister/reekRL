Keyless/Power Door Locks/Security System Function Test
Ultrasonic sensor and internal battery of alarm control siren
  1. Park the vehicle in the place where is no wind.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft code for the radio and the navigation system, then write down the radio presets.
  1. Open the driver's window.
  1. Close all doors.
  1. Lock the doors with the transmitter or the key cylinder.
  1. Wait 25 seconds to arm the security alarm system, and make sure the security indicator blinks.
  1. Put your hand into the cabin from the driver's window.
    • If the siren sounds and hazard warning lights flash, go to Step 8 .
    • If the siren doesn't sound and the hazard warning lights don't flash, go to the ultra sonic sensor input test.
  1. While the siren is sounding, open the hood.
  1. Disconnect the battery terminals.
    • If the siren is still sounding, the internal battery is OK.
    • If the siren turns off, the internal battery is faulty, replace the alarm control siren.
  1. After reconnecting the battery, enter the anti-theft code for the radio and the navigation system, then enter the customer's radio presets.
    NOTE:
    • The alarm control siren is self-powered system with its internal Ni-MH battery back-up. Even if the vehicle's battery voltage drops, the alarm control siren can work properly.
    • If the lock knob is pushed before the door is closed, the buzzer sounds 1 time and the ultrasonic system doesn't work.
