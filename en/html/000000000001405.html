EVAP Canister Vent Shut Valve Replacement
KD model
  1. Remove the trunk floor.
  1. Remove the access panel (A) from the floor.
  1. Disconnect the EVAP canister vent shut valve 2P connector (A), FTP sensor 3P connector (B), and the hose (C).
  1. Pry the lock tabs (D) outward, and remove the EVAP canister vent shut valve (E).
    NOTE: Be careful not to damage the lock tabs.
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal with a new O-ring (F).
    NOTE: Do not coat the new O-ring with oil or other lubricants.
