Master Cylinder Replacement
Do not spill brake fluid on the vehicle; if may damage the paint; if brake fluid gets on the paint, wash it off immediately with water.
  1. Remove the engine cover (A).
  1. Remove the upper fender trims (B).
  1. LHD type: Remove the under-hood fuse/relay box, and move it aside.
  1. Remove the front tower bar.
  1. Remove the reservoir cap and brake fluid from the master cylinder reservoir with a syringe.
  1. RHD type: Remove the A/C suction hose (A) bracket mounting bolt (B).
  1. RHD type: Remove the A/C line (C) from the clamps (D).
  1. RHD type: Remove the A/C lines (A) from the clamp (B).
  1. Disconnect the brake fluid level switch connector (A).
    NOTE: The illustration shows LHD type, RHD type is similar.
  1. Disconnect the brake lines (B) from the master cylinder (C). To prevent spills, cover the line joints with rags or shop towels.
  1. Remove the master cylinder mounting nuts (D).
  1. Remove the master cylinder from the brake booster (E). Be careful not to bend or damage the brake lines when removing the master cylinder.
  1. Remove the O-ring (F) from the master cylinder.
    NOTE: Replace the O-ring whenever the master cylinder is removed.
  1. Install the master cylinder in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check the brake booster pushrod clearance
    • Use the new O-ring on reassembly.
    • Coat the O-ring with clean silicone oil.
    • Check the brake pedal height and free play after installing the master cylinder, and adjust it if necessary.
  1. Bleed the brake system.
  1. Spin the wheels to check for brake drag.
