DTC Troubleshooting: P0497 (90)
DTC P0497:
 EVAP System Low Purge Flow Detected
Special Tools Required

Combination gauge kit 20/−100 kpa
 07YAJ-0010610

Tube joint adapter
 07410-5790501
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Select EVAP FUNCTION TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
Is the result OK?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the EVAP canister purge valve, the FTP sensor, and the PCM.■
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Check for poor connection, blockage, or damage at the EVAP canister purge line between the throttle body and the EVAP canister.
Is the line OK?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Reconnect or repair the EVAP canister purge line, then go to Step 24 .
  1. Disconnect the vacuum hose (A) from the EVAP canister purge valve (B).
  1. Disconnect the vacuum hose from the purge line (at the EVAP canister side), and connect the combination gauge kit and tube joint adapter, and the vacuum pump/gauge, 0−30 in.Hg (A), to the hose as shown.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Apply about 2 kPa (15 mmHg, 0.6 in.Hg) of vacuum to the hose.
  1. Select EVAP PCS ON in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
Does the vacuum release immediately?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Go to Step 11 .
  1. Select EVAP PCS OFF in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect the vacuum hose (A) from the EVAP canister purge valve (B). Connect the combination gauge kit and tube joint adapter and the vacuum pump/gauge, 0−30 in.Hg, and EVAP canister purge valve as shown.
  1. Apply about 2 kPa (15 mmHg, 0.6 in.Hg) of vacuum to the hose.
  1. Select EVAP PCS ON in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
Does the vacuum release immediately?
YES -
Check for blockage in the vacuum hose between the EVAP canister purge valve and the EVAP canister, then go to Step 24 .
NO -
Replace the EVAP canister purge valve, then go to Step 24 .
  1. Connect the vacuum pump/gauge, 0−30 in.Hg, to the vacuum hose (A) as shown.
  1. Start the engine, and let it idle.
Is there vacuum?
YES -
Go to Step 17 .
NO -
Check for blockage in vacuum hose between the EVAP canister purge valve and the intake manifold then go to Step 24 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the FTP sensor with its connector connected.
  1. Connect the combination gauge kit and tube joint adaptor, and the vacuum pump/gauge, 0−30 in.Hg (A), to the FTP sensor (B) as shown.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Check and record the FTP SENSOR reading in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
  1. Slowly apply about 1.3 kPa (10 mmHg, 0.4 in.Hg) of vacuum to the hose.
  1. Check the FTP SENSOR in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
Does the value change?
YES -
Check for debris or blockage at the EVAP canister port, then go to Step 24 .
NO -
Replace the FTP sensor, then go to Step 24 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Reset the PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Do the EVAP FUNCTION TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
Is the result OK?
YES -
Go to Step 28 .
NO -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the EVAP canister purge valve, the FTP sensor, and PCM, then go to Step 1 .
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P0497 indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the EVAP canister purge valve, the FTP sensor, and the PCM, then go to Step 1 .
NO -
Troubleshooting is complete. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
