Center Console Armrest Disassembly/Reassembly
NOTE: Take care not to scratch the armrest.
  1. Remove the console armrest.
  1. Disassemble the console armrest.
    -1.
    Remove the armrest tray cover (A) by detaching the clips (B, C).
    -2.
    Remove the screws (D), then remove the armrest lids (E).
    -3.
    Remove the screws (F, G), then remove the armrest tray (H) from the armrest bracket (I).
  1. Disassemble the armrest lid.
    -1.
    Pry out the lock cover (A).
    -2.
    Remove the screws (B), then remove the armrest lid lock (C).
    -3.
    Release the seals (D), and remove the screws (E), then remove the armrest lid cover (F).
    -4.
    Remove the screws (G), then remove the armrest hinges (H) and armrest lid (I) from the armrest lid bracket (J).
  1. Reassemble the armrest in the reverse order of disassembly.
