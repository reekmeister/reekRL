SH-AWD System Symptom Troubleshooting - Does not become snow mode or can not cancel snow mode
Does not become snow mode or can not cancel snow mode
NOTE:
  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information.
  • Snow mode operation these conditions: engine speed above 400 rpm (min −1 ), vehicle at a speed of below 100 km/h (54 rpm), steering wheel at straight position, accelerator pedal is not pressed.
  1. Push the snow mode switch.
Does the snow mode operate?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is Ok at this time.■
NO -
Go to Step 2 .
  1. Test the snow mode switch.
Is the snow mode switch OK?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Replace the dash lights brightness controller. ■
  1. Disconnect SH-AWD control unit connector A (26P).
  1. Check for continuity between SH-AWD control unit connector terminals A7 and A12, by the snow mode switch is not pushed.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to ground in the wire between the SH-AWD control unit and the dash lights brightness controller.■
NO -
Go to Step 5 .
  1. Push the snow mode switch.
  1. Check for continuity between SH-AWD control unit connector terminals A7 and A12.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the SH-AWD control unit and the dash lights brightness controller.■
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check for engine speed of the SH-AWD control unit with the HDS.
Is engine speed above 400 rpm (min −1 )?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Go to Step 11 .
  1. Check for vehicle at a speed of the SH-AWD control unit with the HDS.
Is the vehicle at a speed of below 2 km/h (1 mph)?
YES -
Substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Check for DTCs VSA system with the HDS.
Are any VSA system DTCs indicated?
YES -
Go to the indicated DTCs troubleshooting.
NO -
Substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
  1. Check for DTCs in the PGM-FI system with the HDS.
Are any PGM-FI system DTCs indicated?
YES -
Go to the indicated DTCs troubleshooting. ■
NO -
Substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
