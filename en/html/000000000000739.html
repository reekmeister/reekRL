DTC Troubleshooting: B1281
DTC B1281:
 Windshield Wiper Switch MIST Position Circuit Malfunction
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Turn the windshield wiper switch in MIST and wait for 2 seconds.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1281 indicated?
YES -
  • LHD type and KE model: faulty combination switch control unit; replace the wiper/washer switch.
  • RHD type except KE model: Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the windshield wiper/washer switch and the combination switch control are OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the wiper/washer switch 12P connenctor from combination light switch.
  1. Perform the wiper/washer switch test at the MIST position.
Is wiper/washer switch OK?
YES -
Faulty combination switch control unit; repalce the combination light switch.■
NO -
Repalce the wiper/washer switch.■
