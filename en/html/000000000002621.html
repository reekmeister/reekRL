A/T System Service Precautions
Precautions for Super Handling All Wheel Drive (SH-AWD) System
This vehicle is equipped with the Super Handling All Wheel Drive (SH-AWD) system. The SH-AWD system distributes driving torque between the front and rear wheels when accelerating and when wheel spin occurs.
The SH-AWD system does not have a manual switch to disable the AWD system. Do not drive the vehicle with rear wheels on the ground even though the front wheels are off the ground. The front wheel power is conveyed to the rear wheels, and the vehicle will start off.
Whenever service work requires spinning the front or rear wheels with the engine such as Pressure Tests, always lift up and support the vehicle so all four wheels are off the ground.
Precautions on Using Free Rollers
Use the free rollers under the rear wheels when performing test the vehicle with the speedometer tester.
  • Inspecting and testing using a chassis dynamometer is not feasible.
  • Make sure to place free rollers parallel to the roller of each speedometer tester. The free rollers are to be set under the rear wheels.
  • Putting the front and rear wheels on the speedometer testers and free rollers inappropriately may cause the vehicle to roll off or over the free rollers and create a hazardous condition.
  • When attaching the side anchor wires and center tie down wire, make sure they are not interfering with the bumper and other parts of the vehicle body. The anchor wires and tie-down wire must be appropriately tensioned. If the wires have too much slack, the expected tie-down efficiency cannot be obtained.
  • Do not operate the speedometer testers at a speed over 50 km/h (31 mph), and for more than 3 minutes.
  • Do not operate the accelerator, brake pedal or steering wheel abruptly. It may cause the vehicle to roll and create a hazardous condition.
  1. Set the free rollers according to the wheelbase and tread of the vehicle.
  1. Move the vehicle to position the front wheels on the speedometer tester and the rear wheels on the free rollers. Make sure to align the center of the wheels to the center of the speedometer tester and the free rollers.
  1. Tie down the vehicle securely using the towing hooks to prevent the vehicle from rolling or over the free rollers.
  1. Start the engine. Shift the transmission into the D position, accelerate the vehicle gradually, and measure the vehicle speed.
  1. After measurement, use the brake pedal to gradually decelerate and stop the vehicle.
