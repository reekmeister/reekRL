DTC Troubleshooting: B2179
DTC B2179:
 Driver's Door Key Cylinder Switch LOCK/UNLOCK Signal Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Turn the driver's door key cylinder to LOCK and UNLOCK positions with the ignition key.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2179 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The driver's door key cylinder switch circuit is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
  1. With the driver's door key cylinder switch in neutral position, select DOOR LOCK from the BODY ELECTRICAL menu, and enter the DATA LIST.
  1. Check the ON/OFF information of the Driver's door key cylinder switch (LOCK) and Driver's door key cylinder switch (UNLOCK) in the DATA LIST.
Are both information indicators OFF?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Turn the driver's door key cylinder to LOCK and UNLOCK positions with the ignition key, and check the ON/OFF information of the Driver's door key cylinder switch (LOCK) and Driver's door key cylinder switch (UNLOCK) in the DATA LIST.
Are both Driver's door key cylinder switch (LOCK) and Driver's door key cylinder switch (UNLOCK) information indicators ON at the same time with the door key cylinder switch in LOCK or UNLOCK position?
YES -
Repair a short in the wire.■
NO -
Replace the driver's MPCS unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the driver's door lock actuator 10P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check the ON/OFF information of the Driver's Door Key Cylinder Switch (LOCK) and Driver's Door Key Cylinder Switch (UNLOCK) in the DATA LIST.
Are both information indicators OFF?
YES -
Faulty driver's door key cylinder switch; replace the driver's door lock actuator assembly.■
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the driver's MPCS unit connector A (40P).
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the driver's door lock actuator 10P connector terminals No. 8 and No. 9 [No. 6 and No. 7] individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire.■
NO -
Replace the driver's MPCS unit.■
