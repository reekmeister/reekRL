Cylinder Head Cover Removal
  1. Remove the intake manifold.
  1. Remove the six ignition coils.
  1. Remove the three bolts (A) securing the harness holder, and remove the dipstick (B).
  1. With power steering: Remove the bolt (A) securing the power steering hose clamp.
  1. Remove the two bolts (B) securing the harness holder.
  1. Remove the breather hose (C).
  1. Remove the cylinder head covers.
