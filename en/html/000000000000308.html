In-car Temperature Sensor Test
  1. Remove the in-car temperature sensor.
  1. Compare the resistance reading between the No. 2 and No. 3 terminals of the in-car temperature sensor with the specifications shown in the graph; the resistance should be within the specifications for whatever the ambient temperature is.
  1. If the resistance is not as specified, replace the in-car temperature sensor.
    Incorrectly applying power and ground to the in-car temperature sensor will damage it. Follow the instructions carefully.
  1. Connect battery power to the No. 1 terminal of the in-car temperature sensor, and ground the No. 4 terminal; the aspirator fan should run.
  1. If the aspirator fan did not run in Step 4 , replace it.
