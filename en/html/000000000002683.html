A/T Paddle Shifter + (Upshift Switch) Illumination Circuit Troubleshooting
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations and the precautions and procedures in the SRS before doing repair or service.
  1. Remove the steering wheel.
  1. Check for continuity between steering switch harness 20P connector terminals No. 9 and No. 10.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Remove the cable reel.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), and turn the lighting switch ON.
  1. Measure the voltage between cable reel 20P connector terminals No. 16 and No. 17.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Replace the cable reel. ■
NO -
Go to Step 6 .
  1. Measure the voltage between cable reel 20P connector terminal No. 17 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Check for a blown No. 7 (7.5 A) fuse in the passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box. If the fuse is OK, repair open or short in the wire between cable reel 20P connector terminal No. 17 and the passenger's under-dash fuse relay box.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect gauge control module connector A (20P).
  1. Check for continuity between cable reel 20P connector terminal No. 16 and gauge control module connector terminal A2.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Check the illumination control circuit in the gauge control module self-diagnostic function. ■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between cable reel 20P connector terminal No. 16 and gauge control module.■
