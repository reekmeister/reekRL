Rear Differential Center Case Side Oil Seal Replacement
Special Tools Required

Driver B

Attachment, 78 x 90 mm
 07GAD-SD40101
  1. Remove the rear differential.
  1. Remove the right side case (A), the left side case (B), the six 8 x 14 mm dowel pins (C), and the two O-rings (D).
  1. Remove the 60 x 89 x 6.5 mm oil seal (A) from the right side center case (B) with a commercially available tool.
  1. Remove the 60 x 80 x 6.5 mm oil seal (A) from the left side center case (B) with a commercially available tool.
  1. Remove the differential carrier cover (A), the 156 x 3.5 mm O-ring (B), the 8 x 14 mm dowel pin (C), and the 7.7 x 2.3 mm O-ring (D).
  1. Remove the 70 x 100 x 20 mm bearing outer race (A) and 100 mm shim (B) from the differential carrier cover (C) by heating the differential carrier cover to about 100 °C (212 °F) with a heat gun (D). Do not heat the differential carrier cover in excess of 100 °C (212 °F).
    NOTE: Let the differential carrier cover cool to room temperature before installing the bearing outer race.
  1. Remove the 60 x 89 x 6.5 mm oil seal and the 60 x 80 x 6.5 mm oil seal from the differential carrier cover.
  1. Install the new 60 x 80 x 6.5 mm oil seal (A) into the differential carrier cover (B) with the special tools.
  1. Install the new 60 x 89 x 6.5 mm oil seal (A) into the differential carrier cover (B) with the special tools.
  1. Install the 100 mm shim (A) and the 70 x 100 x 20 mm bearing outer race (B) into the differential carrier cover (C) with a press.
  1. Install the 8 x 14 mm dowel pin (A), the new 7.7 x 2.3 mm O-ring (B), the new 156 x 3.5 mm O-ring (C), and the differential carrier cover (D) onto the center case (E).
  1. Install the new 60 x 80 x 6.5 mm oil seal (A) into the right side center case (B) with the special tools.
  1. Install the new 60 x 89 x 6.5 mm oil seal (A) into the left side center case (B) using the special tools.
  1. Remove any dirt and oil from the sealing surface. Apply liquid gasket (P/N 08C70-K0234M) to the sealing surface. Make sure you seal the entire circumference of the bolt holes to prevent oil leakage.
    NOTE:
    • Do not install components if too much time has passed after applying the liquid gasket (for P/N 08C70-K0234M, no more than 4 minutes, for all others, no more than 5 minutes). Instead, remove the old residue and reapply the liquid gasket.
    • Allow it to cure at least 30 minutes after assembly before filling the differential with fluid.
