Brake Booster Replacement
RHD type
  1. Remove the master cylinder.
  1. Remove the battery.
  1. Remove the air cleaner.
  1. Disconnect the vacuum hose (A) from the brake booster (B).
  1. Remove the brake pipes (A) from the clamp (B).
  1. Remove the brake pipe clamp (A).
  1. Remove the water valve mounting bracket (B). Do not disconnect the heater hoses.
  1. Remove the fuel hose stay (A). Do not disconnect the fuel line.
  1. Remove the harness holder (A) mounting bolts from the rear cylinder head cover.
  1. Remove the engine mount control solenoid valve bracket (A) from the rear cylinder head, and disconnect the connectors (B), then move them upward.
  1. Remove the engine wire harness (A) from the harness clamps (B), and move the engine wire harness aside.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard under cover.
  1. Loosen the yoke nut (A), then remove the lock pin (B) and the joint pin (C).
  1. Remove the brake booster mounting nuts (D).
  1. Remove the york (E) from the brake booster rod (F).
  1. Pull the brake booster (A) forward, then move the brake booster to passenger's side with keeping it a gradient.
  1. Remove the brake booster upward from the engine compartment.
  • Be careful not to damage the booster surfaces and threads of the booster stud bolts.
  • Be careful not to bend or damage the brake lines.
  1. Install the brake booster in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Adjust the pushrod length before installing the brake booster.
    • Always use a new lock pin.
    • Install the master cylinder after installing the brake booster.
    • Check the brake pedal height and free play after installing the master cylinder, and adjust it.
    • Set the clock.
    • Bleed the brake system.
