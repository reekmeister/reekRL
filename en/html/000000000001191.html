A/T Interlock System - Park Pin Switch Replacement
NOTE:
  • The park pin switch is available as shift lever bracket assembly (not separately from the shift lever bracket).
  • Refer to Shift Lever Disassembly and Reassembly.
  1. Remove the shift lever assembly.
  1. Remove the shift lever ring, and remove the shift lever knob.
  1. Remove the A/T gear position indicator panel light bulb socket, then remove the bulb from the socket, and remove the A/T gear position indicator panel from the shift lever bracket.
  1. Remove the shift lock release, shift lock solenoid, solenoid plunger, plunger spring, and shift lock stop.
  1. Remove the shift lever bracket from the shift lever bracket base.
  1. Install the new shift lever bracket on the shift lever bracket base.
  1. Apply silicone grease to the joints of the shift lock stop and the shift lever bracket, and install the shift lock stop on the shift lever bracket.
  1. Apply silicone grease to the tip of the shift lock stop, and install the shift lock solenoid assembly by aligning the joint of the shift lock solenoid plunger with the tip of the shift lock stop.
  1. Install the shift lock release.
  1. Install the A/T gear position indicator panel.
  1. Install the bulb in the socket, then install the socket in the A/T gear position indicator panel.
  1. Apply silicone grease to the top of the lock-pin rod, and install the shift lever knob over the rod.
  1. Apply non-hardening thread lock sealant to the screw threads, and secure the shift lever knob with the screws.
  1. Install the shift lever ring over the shift lever knob.
  1. Install the shift lever assembly.
