Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) DTC Troubleshooting: 93-01
EPS (LKAS) Control Unit DTC 93-01:
 A Communication Defect with LKAS Control Unit
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0), then turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Watch the LKAS indicator.
Does the LKAS indicator come on?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
  1. Check for DTC with the HDS.
Is DTC 93-01 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Troubleshoot the indicated DTC(s).■
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect the LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector terminal No. 8 and body ground individually.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Repair short to power in the wire between EPS (LKAS) control unit and LKAS camera/control unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Reconnect the LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector.
  1. Disconnect EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 20 and body ground individually.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Repair short to power in the wire between EPS (LKAS) control unit and LKAS camera/control unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Reconnect EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P).
  1. Disconnect the LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Check for continuity between the ground and LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector terminals No. 6 and No. 8, individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between the LKAS camera/control unit and the EPS (LKAS) control unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 17 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P).
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector terminals No. 6 and No. 8, individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between EPS (LKAS) control unit and LKAS camera/control unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 20 .
  1. Check for continuity between LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector terminal No. 6 and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 20.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 21 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the EPS (LKAS) control unit and the LKAS camera/control unit.■
  1. Check for continuity between LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector terminal No. 8 and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 19.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the EPS (LKAS) control unit and LKAS camera/control unit connectors, and repair if necessary. If all connections are OK, replace the EPS (LKAS) control unit and the LKAS camera/control unit. ■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the EPS (LKAS) control unit and the LKAS camera/control unit.■
