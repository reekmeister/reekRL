Rear Seat Headrest Replacement
NOTE: Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  1. Remove the rear seat-back.
  1. Remove the seat-back cover/pad.
  1. Disconnect the connector (A), and detach it from the seat-back frame. Detach the wire harness clip (B), and remove the harness band (C) (right side headrest only).
  1. Remove the retainers (A) from both headrest guides (B), then remove the headrest (C). Remove both headrest guides.
  1. Install the headrest in the reverse order of removal, and make sure the connector is plugged in properly.
