VSA DTC Troubleshooting: 25
DTC 25:
 Yaw Rate Sensor
NOTE:
  • If DTC 27 or DTC 64 is stored at the same time as DTC 25, troubleshoot DTC 27 or DTC 64 first, then recheck for DTC 25.
  • Information marked with an asterisk 1 (*1) applies to the LHD type.
  • Information marked with an asterisk 2 (*2) applies to the RHD type.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then disconnect the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle around a number of corners.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 25 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
The system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the steering angle sensor 5P connector.
  1. Disconnect the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor 5P connector.
  1. Disconnect the VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between body ground and VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector terminal No. 33, No. 34, and No. 35 individually.
Is there 0.1 V or more?
YES -
Repair short to power in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit, the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor, and the steering angle sensor.■
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector terminal No. 33, No. 34, and No. 35 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit, the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor, and the steering angle sensor.■
NO -
Go to Step 14 .
  1. Check for continuity between VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector terminal No. 35 and yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor 5P connector terminal No. 2.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor.■
  1. Check for continuity between VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector terminal No. 33 and yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor 5P connector terminal No. 4.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 16 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor.■
  1. Check for continuity between VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector terminal No. 34 and yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor 5P connector terminal No. 3.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 17 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor.■
  1. Substitute a known-good yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor.
  1. Reconnect all of the disconnected connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then disconnect the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle around a number of corners.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 25 indicated?
YES -
Check for loose terminals or poor connections at the VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector. If connections are good, replace the VSA modulator-control unit, and recheck.■
NO -
Replace the original yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor. ■
