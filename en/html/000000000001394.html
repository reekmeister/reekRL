PCM Replacement
NOTE: If the troubleshooting leads to replace the PCM after substituting a known-good PCM, reinstall the orignal PCM first, then do this procedure.
  1. Connect the HDS to the data link connector (DLC) (A) located under the driver's side of the dashboard.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Make sure the HDS communicates with the PCM. KD model: If it doesn't, skip steps 4 to 6 and 16, then clean the throttle body and replace the engine oil after this procedure.
  1. KD model: Select the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
  1. KD model: Select the TP POSITION CHECK in the ETCS TEST.
  1. KD model: Select READ DATA in REPLACE PCM with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Remove the panels (A), and pull back the carpet.
  1. Remove the ducts (B).
  1. Disconnect PCM connectors (C).
  1. Remove the bolts (D), then remove the PCM (E).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Open the SCS with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. KD model: Select WRITE DATA in REPLACE PCM with the HDS.
  1. Rewrite the immobilizer code with the PCM replacement procedure in the HDS; it allows you to start the engine.
  1. Reset the PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Do the CKP pattern learn procedure.
