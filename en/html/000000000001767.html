DTC Troubleshooting: B1776
DTC B1776:
 Microphone Input/Output Short to Ground/Open
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1776 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■

  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the No. 9 and No. 10 terminals of the Hands Free Telephone control unit 22P connector individually, then between terminals No. 9 and No. 20.
