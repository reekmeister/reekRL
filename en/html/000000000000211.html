NOTE: Replace the bearing with a new one whenever the outer race is replaced.
  1. Remove the 52 mm bearing outer race (A), the 75 mm outer race (B), and 65 mm outer race (C), from the transfer housing (D).
    NOTE: Some bearing outer races are press-fitted in the housing, remove the races by heating the housing.
  1. Remove the 52 mm, 75 mm, and 65 mm outer races by heating the transfer housing (A) to about 100 °C (212 °F) with a heat gun (B), if the outer races are press-fitted. Do not heat the housing more than 100 °C (212 °F).
  1. Install the 52 mm bearing outer race (A) until it bottoms in the transfer housing with the special tools.
  1. Install the 75 mm bearing outer race (A) and the 65 mm outer race (B) in the transfer housing (C), set the special tool attachments over the races, and install the installer shaft through the attachments.
  1. Tighten the installer shaft nut (07JAF-SJ80120) to install the races (A) (B) into the transfer housing (C) securely.
