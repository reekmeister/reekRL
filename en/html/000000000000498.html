Dashboard/Steering Hanger Beam Removal/Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Have an assistant help you when removing and installing the dashboard.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard/steering hanger beam, body and other related parts.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the audio and the navigation system, then write down the audio presets.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Driver's dashboard undercover
    • Passenger's dashboard undercover
    • Center console
    • Ashtray housing
    • Instrument fascia
    • Driver's dashboard lower cover
    • Glove box
    • Kick panels, both sides
    • Front pillar trim, both sides
    • Steering column
    • Shift lever
  1. Disconnect the hood opener cable.
Driver's side
  1. From under the dash, disconnect the keyless converter unit connector (A) (for some RHD models), brake switch connector (B), left side wire harness connectors (C), engine compartment wire harness connectors (D), and disconnect the dashboard wire harness connectors (E) from the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box (F), then release the wire harness clips.
    NOTE: Lift the white wire harness connector locks (G) before trying to remove the connectors from the fuse box.
LHD
RHD
Middle portion (shift lever portion)
  1. Disconnect and detach the left side wire harness connectors (A, B), release the wire harness clips (C), then remove the rear vent duct A (D). Disconnect the left side wire harness connectors (E) and dashboard wire harness connector (F) from the SRS control unit. Using a T30 Torx bit, remove the ground bolt (G).
Center (passenger's side)
  1. From under the dash, release the clip, and pull the carpet (A) back. Disconnect and detach the PCM connectors (B), engine wire harness connector (C).
LHD
RHD
Passenger's side
  1. From under the dash, disconnect the passenger's door wire harness connector (A), the A/C subwire harness connectors (B), right side wire harness connectors (C) engine compartment wire harness connector (D), and disconnect the dashboard wire harness connectors (E) from the passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box (F). LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Detach all of the harness and connector clips.
  1. Open the passenger's door, and gently pull out along the rear edge to release the hooks (A), then remove the passenger's dashboard side lid (B). LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Open the driver's door, and remove the caps (A), then remove the bolts (B, C), loosen two bolts (D), and lift up on the dashboard (E) to release it from the guide pins (F, G).
    NOTE:
    • Before removing the dashboard/steering hanger beam, make sure all the harnesses have been disconnected.
    • LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Carefully remove the dashboard/steering hanger beam through the front door opening.
    NOTE: Lay the dashboard on its front or back. Do not rest it on the lower console opening or you may damage it.
  1. Remove the bolts, then remove the center bracket (A).
  1. Install the dashboard/steering hanger beam in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Reinstall the center bracket on the center frame, and slightly tighten the mounting bolts. Reinstall the dashboard on the body. After tightening the dashboard mounting bolts and nut, tighten the center bracket mounting bolts and center frame mounting bolts.
    • Make sure the dashboard/steering hanger beam fits onto the guide pins correctly.
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the bolts securing the center bracket and the dashboard before reinstallation.
    • Before tightening the bolts, make sure the wire harnesses are not pinched.
    • Make sure the connectors are plugged in properly.
    • Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
    • Enter the anti-theft codes for the audio and the navigation system, then enter the audio presets.
    • Check for any DTCs that may have been set during repairs, and clear them.
    • If necessary, adjust the shift cable.
    • Do the steering column position memorization.
