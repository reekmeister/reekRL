Navigation System Symptom Troubleshooting - Only rearview camera pictures is not displayed
Only rearview camera pictures is not displayed
  1. Check the No. 7 fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 2 .
NO -
Replace the fuse and recheck.■
  1. Go to the ‘‘Navi System Link''.
  1. Check the indicator of the ‘‘R-Camera''.
Does the indicator become green?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals or repair an open in the wire between the rearview camera and the navigation unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II) and put the vehicle in Reverse.
  1. Disconnect the rearview camera 6P connector.
  1. Measure the voltage between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals of the rearview camera 6P connector.
Is the voltage 7.6−8.4 V?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Disconnect the navigation unit connector D (7P).
  1. Measure the voltage between body ground and navigation unit connector D (7P) terminal No. 1.
Is the voltage 7.6−8.4 V?
YES -
Repair an open in the wire at the VCC line between the rearview camera 6P connector and the navigation unit connector D (7P).■
NO -
Replace the navigation unit.■
  1. Reconnect the rearview camera 6P connector.
  1. Measure the voltage between the No. 3 and No. 4 terminals of the rearview camera 6P connector.
    NOTE: Check the rearview camera is not near the white wall and it is not covered darkly.
Is the voltage about 1 V?
YES -
Repair an open or a short in the wire at the VIDEO line between the rearview camera 6P connector and the navigation unit connector D (7P).■
NO -
Replace the rearview camera.■
