How to Troubleshoot the Adaptive Front Lighting System
Self-diagnostic Function
The self-diagnostic function of the adaptive front lighting control system detects system problems. This information can be retrieved by displaying DTCs with the AFS indicator.
Displaying the DTC (use only if the HDS is unavailable)
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Short the SCS line to body ground.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
    (In this point, the AFS indicator comes on for 2 seconds.)
  1. Within 3 seconds after the AFS indicator turns off, push the AFS OFF switch three times.
  1. Within 6 seconds after the AFS indicator turns off, the AFS indicator starts to indicate the DTCs.
    • If there is a DTC, the AFS indicator starts to blink.
    • If there are no DTCs, the AFS indicator comes on and stays on.
Erasing the DTC
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Short the SCS line to body ground.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
    (at this point, the AFS indicator comes on for 2 seconds and initializing for 1.5 seconds.)
  1. Within 3 seconds after the AFS indicator turns off, push and hold the AFS switch.
  1. Within 3 seconds after the AFS indicator comes on, release the switch.
  1. Within 3 seconds after the AFS indicator turns off, push the switch one time.
  1. Make sure the AFS indicator blinks 2 times. If the AFS indicator does not blink, repeat from Step 1 .
