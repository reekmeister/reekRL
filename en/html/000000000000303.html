Aspirator Fan Circuit Troubleshooting
  1. Check the No. 30 (7.5 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 2 .
NO -
Replace the fuse, and recheck. If the fuse blows again, check for a short in the No. 30 (7.5 A) fuse circuit.■
  1. Disconnect the in-car temperature sensor 4P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between the in-car temperature sensor 4P connector terminal No. 1 and the body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the No. 30 (7.5 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box and the aspirator fan.■
  1. Connect the in-car temperature sensor 4P connector terminal No. 4 to body ground with a jumper wire.
Does the aspirator fan run?
YES -
Check for an open in the wire between the aspirator fan and body ground. If the wire is OK, check for poor ground at G503 for LHD type or G503 for RHD type. ■
NO -
Replace the aspirator fan.■
