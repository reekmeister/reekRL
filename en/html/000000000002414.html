Steering Gearbox Removal and Installation - EPS type
Special Tools Required

Ball joint remover, 32 mm
 07MAC-SL00101
Removal
Note these items during removal:
  • Use solvent and a brush to wash any oil and dirt off the end of the steering gearbox. Avoid any electrical parts. Blow dry with compressed air.
  • Make sure to remove the steering wheel before disconnecting the steering joint to avoid damaging the cable reel.
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is LOCK (0), then do the battery terminal disconnection procedure.
  1. Raise the front of the vehicle, and support it with safety stands in the proper locations.
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Remove the motor from the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard undercover and driver's dashboard lower cover.
  1. Remove the steering wheel.
  1. Remove the steering joint cover (A).
  1. Remove the steering joint bolts (A), then disconnect the steering joint (B) from the pinion shaft and the steering column shaft.
    NOTE: Do not turn the steering column shaft after disconnect the steering joint. If turn the column shaft do the VSA sensor neutral position memorization after install the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the center guide (B) (if equipped), and discard it. The center guide is for factory assembly only.
  1. Remove the lower steering joint cover (C), then remove the pinion shaft grommet A. Be careful not to damage the surface of lower steering joint cover. Replace the cover seal (D) if necessary.
  1. Remove the pinion shaft grommet B from top of the torque sensor.
  1. Apply vinyl tape (A) to the splines on the pinion shaft.
  1. Remove and discard the cotter pin (A) from the tie-rod end ball joint nut (B), and loosen the nut.
  1. Separate the tie-rod ball joint and knuckle using the ball joint remover.
  1. Remove the nuts (A) from the rear engine mount.
  1. Remove the rear engine mount (A) and the engine mount vacuum hose holder mounting bolt (B) on the steering gearbox stiffener.
  1. Disconnect the engine mount vacuum hose (C) from the rear engine mount.
  1. Remove the shift cable mounting bolt (A) on the steering gearbox stiffener (B).
  1. Remove the steering gearbox stiffener (A). Then remove the dynamic damper (B) between the gearbox stiffener and the front suspension subframe.
  1. Remove the steering gearbox mounting bolt (A) and washer (B) on the right gearbox mount.
  1. Remove the steering gearbox mounting bolt (A) and washer (B) on the left gearbox mount.
  1. Remove the front splash shield (A).
    • With pop up hood
    • Without pop up hood
  1. Loosen the propeller shaft mounting nut (A).
  1. Remove the propeller shaft mounting nuts (B).
    NOTE: Do not separate the propeller shaft from the transfer companion flange.
  1. Pull the propeller shaft to make gap between the propeller shaft and the transfer companion flange.
  1. Place a jack (A) under the front tube of the front suspension subframe (B), and support it by raising the jack. Place a transmission jack (C) under the center of the transmission.
  1. Loosen the 14 mm special bolts (A) and 10 mm flange bolts (B) on the front suspension subframe front brackets (C) so they are about 30 mm (1.2 in.) from the mounting surface. Do not loosen the 14 mm special bolts and the 10 mm flange bolts more than necessary.
  1. Loosen the 14 mm special bolts (A) and 12 mm flange bolts (B) on the front suspension subframe rear brackets (C) so they are about 30 mm (1.2 in.) from the mounting surface. Do not loosen the 14 mm special bolts and the 12 mm flange bolts more than necessary.
  1. Lower the transmission jack slowly until the front suspension subframe has dropped about 30 mm (1.2 in.).
  1. Disconnect the stabilizer links (A) from the lower arm on the right and left sides.
  1. Move the steering gearbox to the driver's side, and rotate it so the pinion shaft (A) points toward the front of the vehicle.
  1. Carefully move the steering gearbox (A) as an assembly toward the driver's side of the vehicle until the pinion shaft clears the wheelwell opening. Be careful not to damage the brake lines with the pinion shaft.
  1. Remove the steering gearbox through the wheelwell opening on the driver's side. Be careful not to damage these parts due to interference with the edge of the wheelwell opening when extracting and inserting the steering gearbox.
    • Torque sensor housing
    • Boots
    • Serrations of the pinion shaft
    • Tie-rod end ball joint boots
Installation
NOTE: Do not turn the steering column shaft. If turn the column shaft do the VSA sensor neutral position memorization with the HDS.
  1. Before installing the steering gearbox, make sure that no fluid or grease is on the mating surface of the steering gearbox and the front subframe. To prevent the gearbox mounting bolts from loosening after the installation, remove the fluid or grease from the mount cushions and bolt holes.
  1. Apply vinyl tape (A) to the splines on the pinion shaft.
  1. Apply a mild soap and water solution to both sides of the mount cushion mating surfaces (A).
  1. Slide the steering gearbox (B) between the front suspension subframe and body from the driver's side.
  1. Carefully move the steering gearbox toward the passenger's side until the pinion shaft clears the wheelwell opening on the body.
  1. Rotate the steering gearbox so the pinion shaft (A) points upward.
  1. Continue moving the steering gearbox toward the passenger's side until the steering gearbox is in position.
  1. Connect the stabilizer links (A) to the lower arm on the right and left sides.
  1. Raise the jack supporting front suspension subframe with the transmission jack until the subframe is in position.
  1. Tighten the new 14 mm special bolts (A) and new 12 mm flange bolts (B) on right and left of the vehicle to the specified torque alternately in two or more steps.
  1. Tighten the new 14 mm special bolts (A) and 10 mm flange bolts (B) on right and left of the vehicle to the specified torque alternately in two or more steps.
  1. Lower the transmission jack.
  1. Install the new propeller shaft mounting nuts (A), and tighten them to the specified torque.
  1. Replace the propeller shaft mounting nut (B), and tighten it to the specified torque.
  1. Install the front splash shield (A).
    • With pop up hood
    • Without pop up hood
  1. Loosely install the new steering gearbox mounting bolt (A) and washer (B) on the passenger's side gearbox mount.
  1. Loosely install the new steering gearbox mounting bolt (A) and washer (B) on the driver's side gearbox mount.
  1. Install the steering gearbox stiffener (A) and the dynamic damper (B), then tighten the new mounting bolts (C) to the specified torque.
  1. Loosely install the new steering gearbox mounting bolts (D), then tighten all steering gearbox mounting bolts to the specified torque.
  1. Install the shift cable mounting bolt (A) on the steering gearbox stiffener.
  1. Connect the engine mount vacuum hose (A) in the rear engine mount.
  1. Install the rear engine mount (B), then tighten the new mounting bolts and the engine mount vacuum hose holder mounting bolt (C) on the steering gearbox stiffener to the specified torque.
  1. Install the nuts (A) on the rear engine mount, then tighten them to the specified torque.
  1. Wipe off any grease from the ball joint tapered section and threads. Then reconnect the tie-rod end (A) to the knuckle arms.
  1. Install the tie-rod end ball joint nut, and tighten it to the specified torque. Then install the new cotter pin (B), and bend it as shown (C) or (D).
  1. Remove the vinyl tape from the pinion shaft (A), and install the pinion shaft grommet B.
  1. Install the new cover seal (B) all the way around in the lower steering joint cover (C). Make sure there are no wrinkles in the seal, then install the pinion shaft grommet A.
  1. Install the lower steering joint cover.
  1. Center the steering rack within its stroke.
  1. Insert the upper end of the steering joint onto the steering shaft (A) (line up the bolt hole (B) with the flat portion (C) on the shaft), and loosely install the upper joint bolt (D).
  1. Slip the lower end of the steering joint onto the pinion shaft taking care to align the gap (E) within the angle.
  1. Align the bolt hole (A) on the steering joint with the groove (B) around the pinion shaft then loosely install the lower joint bolt (C).
  1. Pull on the steering joint to make sure that the steering joint is fully seated, then tighten the lower joint bolt to the specified torque.
  1. Tighten the upper joint bolt (D) to the specified torque.
  1. Install the steering joint cover (A).
  1. Install the driver's dashboard undercover and driver's dashboard lower cover.
  1. Install the motor to the steering gearbox.
  1. Install the front wheels, then set the wheels in the straight ahead position.
  1. Install the steering wheel.
  1. Holding the tyres raised off the ground (lifted up), check for the following symptoms by turning the steering wheel fully to the right and left several times.
    Symptom
    Probable cause
    Rubbing sound coming from the lower steering column area.
    Steering column joint is contacting the cover.
    Grating sound from the lower steering column area, or a rough feeling during steering.
    Poor engagement of the pinion shaft serrations.
    Noise from around the steering wheel during steering.
    Poor engagement of the SRS cable reel with the steering wheel, or a damaged cable reel.

  1. Do the battery terminal reconnection procedure, and do the following items:
    • Turn the ignition switch ON (II); the SRS indicator should come on for about 6 seconds and then go off.
    • Verify horn and turn signal switch operation.
    • Verify functions of switches on the steering wheel.
    • Do the steering column position memorization.
    • Do the memorizing the torque sensor neutral position.
  1. After installation, perform the following checks:
    • Start the engine, and let it idle. Turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times. Check that the EPS indicator does not come on.
    • Check the steering wheel spoke angle. If steering spoke angles to the right and left are not equal (steering wheel and rack are not centered), correct the engagement of the joint/pinion shaft serrations, then adjust the front toe by turning the tie-rod ends, if necessary.
    • Check the front toe inspection.
    • Do the steering angle sensor neutral position memorization.
