Roof Console Replacement
  1. Turn the map light switch OFF.
  1. Carefully remove the front map light lens (A).
  1. Remove the two bolts and the roof console (B).
  1. Pull down on the front corners of the roof console to release the two clips.
  1. Push the console forward, then swing the rear edge down.
  1. Disconnect the 28P connector (C) from the roof console.
  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
