Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) DTC Troubleshooting: 91-03
EPS (LKAS) Control Unit DTC 91-03:
 Torque Sensor VS1 and VS2 Lines
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II).
  1. Watch the adaptive cruise control (ACC)/LKAS indicator.
Does the adaptive cruise control (ACC)/LKAS indicator come on?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
  1. Check for DTC with the HDS.
Is DTC 91-03 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Troubleshoot the indicated DTC(s).■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the torque sensor 3P connector and EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P).
  1. Check for continuity between EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminals No. 8 and No. 10.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short between the GRN and WHT wires in the torque sensor circuit between EPS (LKAS) control unit and torque sensor.■
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. On the sensor side, check for resistance between torque sensor coil 3P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 3.
Is the resistance between 24−30 Ω (at 20 °C, 68 °F)?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the EPS (LKAS) control unit connectors, and repair if necessary. If all connections are OK, replace the EPS (LKAS) control unit. ■
NO -
Faulty torque sensor (short or open in the internal circuit), replace the steering gearbox. ■
