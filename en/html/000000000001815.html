DTC Troubleshooting: P0722 (9-3)
DTC P0722:
 Problem in Output Shaft (Countershaft) Speed Sensor Circuit (No Signal Input)
NOTE:
  • Record all freeze data and review General Troubleshooting Information before you troubleshoot.
  • This code is caused by an electrical circuit problem and cannot be caused by a mechanical problem in the transmission.
  1. Record all freeze data, and clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Raise the vehicle, make sure it is securely supported, and allow all four wheels to rotate freely.
  1. Start the engine and turn the VSA off (the light on the VSA OFF switch comes on). Run the engine in the D position at speeds over 2,000 rpm (min −1 ) for more than 10 seconds. Slow down and stop the wheels.
  1. Monitor the OBD status for P0722 in the DTCs/Freeze Data in A/T Mode Menu for a pass/fail.
Does the result indicate FAILED?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at the PCM and the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor connectors. If the HDS indicates NOT COMPLETED, return to Step 3 and recheck.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect PCM connectors A (31P) and B (24P).
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminals A3 and body ground, and between B15 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Repair open in the wires between PCM connector terminals A3, B15, and ground (G101, G102), or repair poor ground (G101, G102), then go to Step 32 .
  1. Connect PCM connectors A (31P) and B (24P).
  1. Disconnect the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor connector terminal No. 1 and body ground.
Is there about 5 V?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Go to Step 26 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect PCM connector B (24P).
  1. Check for continuity between output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor connector terminal No. 2 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between PCM connector terminal B19 and the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor connector, then go to Step 32 .
NO -
Go to Step 16 .
  1. Disconnect PCM connector C (22P).
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminal C4 and output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor connector terminal No. 3.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 18 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor connector and PCM connector terminal C4, then go to Step 32 .
  1. Connect PCM connectors B (24P) and C (22P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor connector terminals No. 2 and No. 3.
Is there about 5 V?
YES -
Go to Step 21 .
NO -
Go to Step 31 .
  1. Connect the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor connector.
  1. Measure voltage between PCM connector terminals B19 and A3 or B15.
Is the voltage 0 V or about 5 V?
YES -
Go to Step 23 .
NO -
Replace the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor, then go to Step 32 .
  1. Raise the vehicle, make sure it is securely supported, and allow all four wheels to rotate freely.
  1. Start the engine, run the vehicle in the D position at engine speeds 2,000 rpm (min −1 ).
  1. Measure voltage between PCM connector terminals B19 and A3 or B15.
Is there 1.5−3.5 V?
YES -
Update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck for DTC P0722. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good PCM, replace the original PCM, then go to Step 32 .
NO -
Replace the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor, then go to Step 32 .
  1. Measure voltage between PCM connector terminals C6 and A3 or B15.
Is there about 5 V?
YES -
Repair open in the wire between PCM connector terminal C6 and the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor, then go to Step 32 .
NO -
Go to Step 27 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect PCM connector C (22P).
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminal C6 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between PCM connector terminal C6 and the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor, then go to Step 32 .
NO -
Check for loose or poor connections at PCM connector terminal C6. If the connection is OK, update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck for DTC P0722. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good PCM, replace the original PCM, then go to Step 32 .
  1. Measure voltage between PCM connector terminals B19 and A3 or B15.
Is there about 5 V?
YES -
Repair open in the wire between PCM connector terminal B19 and the output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor, then go to Step 32 .
NO -
Check for loose or poor connections at PCM connector terminal B19. If the connection is OK, update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck for DTC P0722. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good PCM, replace the original PCM, then go to Step 32 .
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Start the engine and turn the VSA off (the light on the VSA OFF switch comes on). Run the engine in the D position at speeds over 2,000 rpm (min −1 ) for more than 10 seconds. Slow down and stop the wheels.
  1. Monitor the OBD status for P0722 in the DTCs/Freeze Data in A/T Mode Menu for a pass/fail.
Does the result indicate PASSED?
YES -
Troubleshooting is complete.■
NO -
If the PCM was updated and the screen indicates FAILED, substitute a known-good PCM, then return to Step 32 . If the PCM was substituted and the screen indicates FAILED, return to Step 1 and recheck. If the screen indicates NOT COMPLETED, return to Step 33 .
