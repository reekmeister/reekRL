Intermediate Shaft Disassembly
Special Tools Required

Driver
 07749-0010000

Support base attachment
 07LAF-SM40300

Support base
 07965-SD90100

Attachment, 42 x 47 mm
 07746-0010300
  1. Remove the heat shield.
  1. Remove the intermediate shaft outer seal (A) from the bearing support (B).
  1. Remove the set ring (C) and external snap ring (D).
  1. Press the intermediate shaft (A) out of the intermediate shaft bearing (B) using the special tools and a press. Be careful not to damage the metal rings (C) on the intermediate shaft during disassembly.
  1. Remove the internal snap ring (A).
  1. Press the intermediate shaft bearing (A) out of the bearing support (B) using the special tools and a press.
