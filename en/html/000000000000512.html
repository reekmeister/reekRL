Document Tray Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Open the glove box.
  1. Remove the document tray (A).
    -1.
    Open the document tray.
    -2.
    Release the blind fastener (B).
    -3.
    Pull the document tray back to release the hooks (C).
  1. Install the tray in the reverse order of removal.
