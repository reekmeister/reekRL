A/T Shift Cable Adjustment
NOTE: The illustration shows the LHD model; RHD is similar.
  1. Remove the center console cover.
  1. Shift the transmission into the R position.
  1. Remove the nut securing the shift cable end.
  1. Press the holder lock release (A), and pull out the socket holder (B) to remove the shift cable (C) from the shift lever bracket (D). Do not remove the shift cable by pulling the shift cable guide (E) and damper (F).
  1. Push the shift cable (A) until it stops, then release it. Pull the shift cable back one step so that the shift position is in R. Do not hold the shift cable guide (B) and damper (C) to adjust the shift cable.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and verify that the R position indicator comes on.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Place the shift lever in the R position, then insert a 6.0 mm (0.24 in.) pin (A) through the positioning hole (B) on the shift lever bracket, through the positioning hole (C) on the shift lever, and into the positioning hole (D) on the shift lever bracket.
  1. Verify that the shift lever is secured in the R position.
  1. Align the socket holder (A) on the shift cable (B) with the slot in the shift lever bracket (C), then slide the holder into the bracket. Install the shift cable end (D) over the mounting stud (E) by aligning its square hole (F) with the square fitting (G) at the bottom of the stud. Push the holder until it snaps securely in place. Do not install the shift cable by holding the shift cable guide (H) and damper (I).
  1. Verify that the shift cable end (A) is properly installed on the mounting stud (B).
  1. If improperly installed, remove the shift cable from the shift lever bracket, and reinstall the shift cable. Do not install the shift cable end on the mounting stud while the shift cable is on the bracket.
  1. Install and tighten the nut.
  1. Remove the 6.0 mm (0.24 in.) pin that was installed to hold the shift lever.
  1. Move the shift lever to each position, and verify that the A/T gear position indicator follows the transmission range switch.
  1. Allow the all wheels to rotate freely.
  1. Start the engine, and check the shift lever operation in all gears.
  1. Push the shift lock release, and verify that the shift lever releases.
  1. Reinstall the center console cover.
