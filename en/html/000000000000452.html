Power Trunk Control Unit Input Test
NOTE: Before troubleshooting, check for DTCs with the HDS. If any DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTCs troubleshooting first.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the rear seat back.
  1. Disconnected the 20P, 14P, and 5P connectors (A) from the power trunk control unit (B).
  1. Inspect all the connector and socket terminals to be sure they are all making good contact.
    • If the terminals are bent, loose or corroded, repair them as necessary, and recheck the system.
    • If the terminals look OK, go to Step 5 .
  1. With the connectors still disconnected, make these input tests at the connectors.
    • If any test indicates a problem, find and correct the cause, then recheck the system.
    • If the input tests prove OK, go to Step 6 .

    • Faulty beeper
    • An open in the wire
    • Poor ground (G602, G701)

  1. Reconnect all connectors to the power trunk control unit, and do the input test at the appropriate connectors.
    • If the test indicates a problem, find and correct the cause, then recheck the system.
    • If the input tests prove OK, replace the power trunk control unit.
