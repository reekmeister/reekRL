Secondary Fuel Gauge Sending Unit Removal/Installation
Removal
  1. Relieve the fuel pressure.
  1. Remove the rear seat cushion.
  1. Remove the access panel (A) from the right side of the floor.
  1. Disconnect the secondary fuel gauge sending unit 5P connector (B).
  1. Remove the secondary fuel gauge sending unit (A) and base gasket (B).
Installation
  1. Install a new base gasket (A) to the fuel tank (B).
  1. Install the secondary fuel gauge sending unit (C). Be careful not to bend the secondary fuel gauge sending unit.
  1. Connect the secondary fuel gauge sending unit 5P connector (A).
  1. Install the access panel (B).
