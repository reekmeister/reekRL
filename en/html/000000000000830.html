DTC Troubleshooting: B2188
DTC B2188:
 Communication Line (UART Line) Circuit Open
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Open and close the each window by operating the front passenger's, left rear, and right rear window switches.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B2188 indicated, and does the any window fail to open or close?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The UART line is OK at this time.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect these connectors:
    • Driver's MPCS unit connector A (40P)
    • Power window master switch 6P connector
    • Passenger's MPCS unit connector A (28P)
    • Left rear window switch 14P connector
    • Right rear window switch 14P connector
    • Sunroof motor/control unit 10P connector
  1. Connect driver's MPCS unit connector A (40P) terminal No. 8 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between passenger's MPCS unit connector A (28P) terminal No. 4 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire, or replace the faulty passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box (LHD type).■
  1. Check for continuity between right rear window switch 14P connector terminal No. 2 and body ground.

NO -

    • Passenger's MPCS unit connector A (28P)
    • Left rear window switch 14P connector
    • Right rear window switch 14P connector
