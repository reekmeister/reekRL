VSA DTC Troubleshooting: 12, 14, 16, 18
DTC 12, 14, 16, 18:
 Wheel Sensor (Electrical Noise/Intermittent Interruption)
NOTE: If the ABS indicator comes on because of electrical noise, the indicator goes off when you test-drive the vehicle at 30 km/h (19 mph).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then disconnect the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle around a number of corners.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check the appropriate wheel sensor and magnetic encoder for debris or damage.
