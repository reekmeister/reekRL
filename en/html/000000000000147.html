Wheel Sensor Replacement
Front
  1. Disconnect the wheel sensor connector (A).
  1. Remove the clamps (B), the bolt, the O-ring (C) and the wheel sensor (D).
  1. Install the wheel sensor in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Use the new clamps on reassmbly.
    • Install the sensor carefully to avoid twisting the wires.
    • If the wheel sensor comes in contact with the hub bearing, it is faulty.
  1. Start the engine, and check that the ABS and the VSA indicators go off.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle, and check that the ABS and the VSA indicators do not come on.
