Special Tools Required

Mainshaft holder set

Snap ring pliers

  1. Remove the ATF dipstick (A) and the dipstick tube (B).
  1. Remove A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valves A and B (C) (six bolts), ATF feed pipes (D) (three), O-rings (three), and gasket.
  1. Remove the solenoid valve cover (E) (seven bolts), dowel pins (two), and gasket.
  1. Disconnect the solenoid valve connectors, then remove the solenoid harness connector (F).
  1. Remove A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve C (G) (four bolts), ATF feed pipes (H) (three), O-rings (two), and gasket.
  1. Pry the lock tab of the lock washer (I) on the control lever (J), and remove the nut, lock washer, spring washer, and control lever.
  1. Pry the lock tabs of the lock washer (K) on the transmission range switch (L), hold the control shaft (M) with a 6.0 mm wrench, and loosen the locknut (N).
  1. Remove the locknut and lock washer, then remove the transmission range switch (two bolts).
  1. Remove the input shaft (mainshaft) speed sensor (O), output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor (P), and sensor washer (Q).
  1. Remove the ATF temperature sensor connector from the clamp bracket on the ATF cooler line, then remove the ATF temperature sensor (R).
  1. Remove the 6.0 mm bolts (S), line bolts (T), ATF cooler lines (U), and sealing washers (V).
  1. Remove the end cover (W) (three bolts), snap ring cap (X) (two bolts), sealing plug (Y), and washer (Z).
  1. Remove the 3rd clutch transmission fluid pressure switch (AA) and sealing washer.
  1. Remove the 4th clutch transmission fluid pressure switch (BB) and sealing washer.
  1. Remove the 2nd clutch transmission fluid pressure switch (CC) and sealing washer.
