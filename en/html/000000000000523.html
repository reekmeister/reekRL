Interior Trim Removal/Installation - Trunk Area
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  1. Remove the spare tyre lid (A) and spare tyre spacer (B).
  1. With normal tyre: Remove the normal tyre from the trunk compartment.
With spare tyre
With normal tyre
  1. Detach the clips, and release the hooks (A) by pulling the trunk rear trim panel (B) up, then remove it.
With spare tyre
With normal tyre
  1. Release the clips (A, B) and fasteners (C), then remove the trunk front trim panel (D).
  1. Remove the tie down hooks (A) and trunk hooks (B) from both trunk side trim panels.
    -1.
    Pry out the lids (C) with a trim tool, then remove all of the tie down hooks by unscrewing them.
    -2.
    Pry out the caps (D) with a trim tool, then remove both trunk hooks by pulling them out.
  1. Detach the clips, then remove the left trunk side trim panel (A) and the right trunk side trim panel (B) from the trunk compartment.
  1. Remove the trunk light.
  1. Lower the trunk upper trim panel (A) to detach the clips (B), and detach the clips (C) from the navigation unit bracket (D), then remove the panel from the trunk compartment.
  1. Install the trim in the reverse of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • KE model: When installing the right trunk side trim panel (A) and trunk rear trim panel (B), pass the trunk lid opener cable (C) through the inside from the hook (D) of the trunk rear trim panel as shown.
