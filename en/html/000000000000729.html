DTC Troubleshooting: B2988 (COMP solenoid error)
DTC B2988 or COMP Solenoid error is indicated:
 A Problem in the Variable Capacity Control Solenoid Valve Circuit
  1. Clear the DTC by turning the ignition switch OFF, and then ON (II).
  1. Start the engine, and turn on the A/C.
  1. Measure the voltage of the battery.
Is there 11.5 V or more?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Charge the battery or replace it.■
  1. Turn off the A/C, and stop the engine.
  1. Clear the DTC by turning the ignition switch OFF, and then ON (II).
  1. Operate the climate control system in several modes.
  1. Check for DTCs using the self-diagnostic.
Is DTC B2988 or COMP solenoid error indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, check for loose wires or poor connections on the variable capacity control solenoid valve circuit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the climate control unit 40P connector.
  1. Measure the resistance between the climate control unit 40P connector terminals No. 3 and No. 4.
Is there about 5.2 Ω?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Go to Step 14 .
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the climate control unit 40P connector terminals No. 3 and No. 4 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Substitute a known-good climate control unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original climate control unit .■
  1. Disconnect the A/C compressor clutch 3P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the climate control unit 40P connector terminals No. 3 and No. 4 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair any short to body ground in the wires between the climate control unit and the variable capacity control solenoid valve.■
NO -
The variable capacity control solenoid valve is faulty, replace the A/C compressor.■
