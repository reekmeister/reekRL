Rear Seat Headrest Actuator/Wire Harness Replacement
NOTE: Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  1. Remove the rear seat-back.
  1. Detach the hooks (A), and release the hooks (B), then remove the headrest case (C) from the headrest (D).
  1. Remove the actuator wire harness (A).
    -1.
    Detach the hooks (B), then remove the guide (C).
    -2.
    Remove the connector clip (D) from the connector (E).
    -3.
    Remove the headrest pillar trim (F).
    -4.
    Disconnect the actuator connector (G), and detach the harness clip (H) from the headrest base (I).
  1. Carefully remove the foam as needed from the frame. Remove the screws, and release the arm (A) from the hook (B), then remove the actuator (C).
  1. Install the actuator and wire harness in the reverse order of removal.
