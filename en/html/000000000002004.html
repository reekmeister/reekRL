A/T Interlock System - Key Interlock System Circuit Troubleshooting
LHD Model
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations, and the precautions and procedures before performing repair or service.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ACC (I). The shift lever must be in the P position.
  1. Disconnect the steering lock assembly connector.
  1. Check if the ignition switch can be moved to the LOCK (0) position.
Can the ignition switch be moved the LOCK (0) position?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Replace the ignition key cylinder/steering lock assembly.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ACC (I).
  1. Measure voltage between steering lock assembly connector terminal No. 4 and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Check for a blown No. 32 (10 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box. If the fuse is OK, repair open in the wire between the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box and the steering lock assembly connector.■
  1. Disconnect MICU connector P (30P).

  1. Remove the center console.
  1. Disconnect the transmission gear selection switch/park pin switch/A/T gear position indicator panel light connector.
  1. Check for continuity between transmission gear selection switch/park pin switch/A/T gear position indicator panel light connector terminals No. 1 and No. 4 while the shift lever is in the P position, and when the shift lever is out of the P position.
Is there continuity when the shift lever is in any position other than P, and no continuity when the shift lever is in P?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -

  1. Check for continuity between transmission gear selection switch/park pin switch/A/T gear position indicator panel light connector terminal No. 4 and body ground.
