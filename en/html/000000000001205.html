Rear Window Sunshade Disassembly/Reassembly
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the sunshade screen and related parts.
  1. Remove the left arm holder from the screen plate, and remove the left screen holder (NOTE: It is pre-loaded), then slide the screen completely out of the screen base.
  1. Reassemble in the reverse order of disassembly, and note these items:
    • Before attaching the screen holder, rotate the shaft five times to pre-load the screen, then attach the screen holder and the arm holder while maintaining the pre-load.
    • Be sure the ground lead is installed securely.
    • Apply multipurpose grease to each pivoting portion of the sunshade as shown.
