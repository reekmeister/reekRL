POP-UP HOOD System DTC Troubleshooting: 11-8x
DTC 11-8x (11-80 to 11-89, 11-8A to 11-8F):  
Short to Power in Left Side Power Unit Component
Special Tools Required

SRS inflator simulator
 07SAZ-TB4011A

SRS simulator lead E
 07XAZ-S1A0200
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 11-8x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the left side power unit component 2P connector.
  1. Connect the SRS inflator simulator (2Ω connector) and simulator lead E to the engine compartment wire harness 2P connector (A).
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 11-8x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Faulty left power unit component; replace the left power unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector from the POP-UP HOOD unit.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 13 terminal of the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector and body ground. There should be less than 1.0 V.
Is the voltage specified?
YES -
Faulty POP-UP HOOD unit; replace the POP-UP HOOD unit.
NO -
Go to Step 17 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the right side (LHD type) or left side (RHD type) wire harness 16P connector (A) from engine compartment wire harness connector (B).
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
