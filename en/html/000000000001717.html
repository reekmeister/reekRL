Seat Heater Test - With Ventilated Seats
Driver's Seat
  1. Remove the driver's seat.
  1. Disconnect the 4P connector (A) and 2P connector (B) from the seat heater.
  1. Check for continuity between the A4 and B1 terminals of the drivers' seat wire harness side. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, check for an open in the driver's seat wire harness. If the wire harness is OK, replace the seat-back heater.
  1. Check for continuity between the A1 and A4 terminals, the A1 and B1 terminals, and the A2 and A3 terminals. There should be continuity.
  1. If the continuity is not as specified, replace the appropriate seat heater.
Front Passenger's Seat
  1. Remove the passenger's seat.
  1. Disconnect the 4P connector (A) from the seat heater.
  1. Check for continuity between the No. 1 and No. 4 terminals, and No. 3 and No. 2 terminals of the 4P connector. There should be continuity.
  1. If the continuity is not as specified, replace the seat heater.
