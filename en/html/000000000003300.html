F-CAN Circuit Troubleshooting - Part 2
  1. Disconnect these connectors:
    • Gauge control module connector B (28P).
    • VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector.
    • SRS unit A (28P).
    • Navigation unit B (32P).
    • SH-AWD control unit connector A (26P).
    • Steering angle sensor 5P connector.
    • Pop-up hood unit 26P connector.
    • e-pretensioner unit connector B (14P) (if equipped).
    • LKAS camera/control unit 10P connector (if equipped).
    • ACC unit 20P connector (if equipped).
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminal E15 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between the PCM (E15) and the gauge control module, the VSA modulator-control unit, the SRS unit, the SH-AWD control unit, the steering angle sensor, the pop-up hood unit, the navigation unit, the ACC unit, the e-pretensioner unit, the LKAS camera/control unit, or the DLC.■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminal E26 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between the PCM (E26) and the gauge control module, the VSA modulator-control unit, the SRS unit, the SH-AWD control unit, the steering angle sensor, the pop-up hood unit, the navigation unit, the ACC unit, the e-pretensioner unit, the LKAS camera/control unit, or the DLC.■
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC.
  1. Disconnect gauge control module connector B (28P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), and read the HDS.
Does the HDS identify the vehicle?
YES -
Replace the gauge control module. ■
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Reconnect gauge control module connector B (28P).
  1. Disconnect the VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), and read the HDS.
Does the HDS identify the vehicle?
YES -
Replace the VSA control unit. ■
NO -
Go to Step 12 .

  1. Disconnect SH-AWD control unit connector A (26P).
