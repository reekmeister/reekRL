Front Seat Frame Removal/Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to tear the seam or damage the seat covers.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • LHD model is shown; RHD model is similar.
Driver's Seat
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front seat-back cover/pad
    • Front seat cushion cover/pad
    • Seat belt buckle
    • Side airbag
  1. Detach the clip and hooks (A), then remove the upper rail outer cover (B).
  1. Remove the recline inner cover (A) and the center inner cover (B) from the seat frame (C).
  1. Detach the clip, then remove the rear inner cover (A).
  1. Install the new seat frame in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • If the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, replace them with new ones.
Passenger's Seat
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front seat-back cover/pad
    • Front seat cushion cover/pad
    • Seat belt buckle
    • Side airbag
    • OPDS unit
  1. Remove the recline inner cover (A) and the center inner cover (B) from the seat frame (C).
  1. Detach the clip, then remove the rear inner cover (A).
  1. Install the new seat frame in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • If the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, replace them with new ones.
