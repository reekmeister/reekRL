A/T Clutch Pressure Control Solenoid Valve C Replacement
  1. Remove the battery trim and left upper fender cover.
  1. Remove the air intake duct and air cleaner housing.
  1. Disconnect the A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve connector.
  1. Remove the A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve C.
  1. Remove the ATF joint pipes, O-rings, ATF pipe, and gasket.
  1. Clean the mounting surfaces and the fluid passages of the solenoid valve body and the transmission housing.
  1. Install the 8 x 53 mm ATF joint pipe (A) with the filter side into its mounting hole (B).
  1. Check the height (A) of the 8 x 53 mm ATF joint pipe (B) between the top (C) of the pipe and the solenoid valve body mounting surface (D). The height is about 7 mm (0.3 in.) If the height is over 7 mm (0.3 in.), push the pipe until it bottoms in the accumulator body.
  1. Install the new gasket (A) on the transmission housing, and install the 8 x 34.5 mm ATF joint pipe (B) with the filter side into the transmission housing and 8 x 25.2 mm ATF pipe (D).
  1. Install new O-rings (E) over the ATF joint pipes.
  1. Install the A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve C.
  1. Install the air intake duct and air cleaner housing.
  1. Install the left upper fender cover and battery trim.
