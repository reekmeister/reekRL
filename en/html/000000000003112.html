SH-AWD DTC Troubleshooting: 82-2
DTC 82-2:
 Right Search Coil Sensor (Open or Short)
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Make sure the HDS communicates with vehicle and the SH-AWD control unit. If it doesn't, troubleshoot the DLC circuit.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect SH-AWD control unit connector A (26P).
  1. Check for continuity between SH-AWD control unit connector terminals A6 and A19.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Measure resistance between SH-AWD control unit connector terminals A6 and A19.
Is there 2.0 Ω or more?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Go to Step 11 .
  1. Check for continuity between SH-AWD control unit connector terminal A6 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between SH-AWD control unit and body ground.■
NO -
Substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
  1. Disconnect the right search coil 2P connector.
  1. Connect a jumper wire between SH-AWD control unit connector terminals A6 and A19.
  1. Check for continuity between right search coil 2P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 2.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Replace the rear differential side case. ■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between right search coil connector terminals No. 1 and No. 2.■
  1. Disconnect the right search coil 2P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the right search coil 2P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 2.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to ground in the wire between SH-AWD control unit and the right search coil.■
NO -
Replace the rear differential side case. ■
