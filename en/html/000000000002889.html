Front Seat Recline Motor Replacement
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • LHD model is shown; RHD model is similar.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove the seat-back cover and pad.
  1. Disconnect and detach the recline motor connector (A), and detach the harness clip (B).
  1. Release the push nut (A) from the motor side end of the connecting rod (B), then gently tap on the motor side of the connecting rod to remove it from the recline motor (C) and both recline adjusters (D).
  1. Remove the rod cover (E).
  1. Remove the bolt, then remove the recline motor (A). The driver's seat is shown; the passenger's seat is similar.
  1. Install the motor in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Replace the push nut with a new one. Make sure the push nut is installed correctly.
    • Make sure the lumbar support motor connector is plugged in properly, and push the harness clip into place securely.
    • Apply medium strength liquid thread lock to the mounting bolt before reinstallation.
