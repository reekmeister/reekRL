DTC Troubleshooting: B1172
DTC B1172:
 Gauge Control Module Lost Communication with LKAS Control Unit (LKAS Message)
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Check for the DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1172 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The F-CAN communication line is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections between the gauge control module connector B (28P) and LKAS control unit 26P connector. If the connections are good, check the battery condition and the charging system.■
  1. Check for the DTCs in the PCM with the HDS.
Are any DTCs indicated?
YES -
Troubleshoot the indicated PCM DTCs.■
NO -
Go to Step 5 .
  1. Do the Gauge Control Module Input Test.
Are all inputs OK?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Repair the faulty input, then recheck the DTCs.■
  1. Substitute a known-good gauge control module.
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Check for the DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1172 indicated?
YES -
Replace the LKAS control unit .■
NO -
The original gauge control module is faulty; replace it. ■
