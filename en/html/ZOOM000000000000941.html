POP-UP HOOD Pedestrian Crash Sensor Calibration
When you replace the pedestrian crash sensor, bumper beam, yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor, and/or POP-UP HOOD unit, calibrating the pedestrian crash sensor. Calibrating the pedestrian crash sensor incomplete, DTC 57-71 is indicated. If another DTC is indicated, do the indicated DTC troubleshooting.
Before calibrating
  • Make sure the battery is sufficiently fully charged, it is necessary to check the condition of the battery, cables, and the charging system.
  • Park the vehicle on horizontal.
  • Make sure all the ture sizes are correct and that their pressure is correct.
  • Remove all baggage from the vehicle except the spare ture and the tool kit.
  • Mechanic gets on driver's seat.
  • Closing all doors.
  • Shift transmission lever into P, and apply the parking brake.
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II). Do not start the engine.
  1. Select POP-UP HOOD, MISCELLANEOUS FUNCTION, then VEHICLE LEVEL POSITION CALIBRATION on the HDS.
  1. Confirm whether CL-00 indicated on the HDS display. If another No. indicated, troubleshoot the appropriate items in the table.
