DTC Troubleshooting: B1884
DTC B1884:
 Trunk Lid Closer Unit Half Latch Switch Circuit Malfunction
  1. Make sure the trunk lid closed fully.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Press and hold the trunk lid outer handle switch, and open the trunk lid fully. Wait for a second.
  1. Close the trunk lid slowly and manually.
  1. Press and hold the trunk lid outer handle switch, and open the trunk lid again.
  1. Check for the DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1882 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The power trunk system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at power trunk control unit connector and at trunk lid closer unit connector.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the power trunk control unit connector C (14P).
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector C (14P) No. 1 and No. 11 terminals.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Go to Step 11 .
  1. Disconnect the 10P connector from the trunk lid closer unit.
  1. At the trunk lid closer unit side, check for continuity between the trunk lid closer unit 10P connector No. 1 and No. 6 terminals.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair an open in the wire (HALF line or SEG3 line) between the trunk lid closer unit and the power trunk control unit.■
NO -
Faulty half latch switch, replace the trunk lid closer unit.■
