DTC Troubleshooting: B2991 (AUDIO communication error)
DTC B2991 or Audio communication error is indicated:
 Climate Control Unit Lost Communication with Audio Unit
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then ON (II).
  1. Do the self-diagnostic with the HDS or climate control unit.
  1. Check for DTCs.
Is DTC B2991 or Audio communication error indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, check for loose wires or poor connections on the audio unit circuit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect audio unit connector B (32P).
  1. Disconnect the climate control unit 40P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the following terminals of the climate control unit 40P connector and audio unit connector B (32P).
    40P:
    32P:
    No. 29
    No. 26
    No. 30
    No. 10

Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Repair any open in the wires between the climate control unit and the audio unit.■
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the climate control unit 40P connector terminals No. 29 and No. 30 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair any short to body ground in the wires between the climate control unit and the audio unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Check for continuity between the climate control unit 40P connector terminals No. 29 and No. 30.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wires between the climate control unit and the audio unit.■
NO -
Check for loose wires or poor connections at the climate control unit 40P connector and at audio unit connector B (32P). If the connections are good, substitute a known-good climate control unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original climate control unit . If the symptom/indication continues, substitute a known-good audio unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original audio unit.■
