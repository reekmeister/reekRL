Starter Removal and Installation
Removal
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the radio and the navigation system, then write down the customer's radio presets. Make sure the ignition switch is OFF.
  1. Remove the battery trim.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery first, then disconnect the positive cable.
  1. Remove the engine cover.
  1. Remove the vacuum hose (A) and transmission dipstick (B).
  1. Remove the harness clamp (A).
  1. Disconnect the starter cable (B) from the B terminal, then disconnect the BLK/WHT wire (C) from the S terminal.
  1. Remove the two bolts holding the starter, then remove the starter.
Installation
  1. Install the starter (A) using a new gasket (B), then install the harness clamp (C), and connect the B terminal (D) and BLK/WHT wire (E). Make sure the crimped side of the B terminal is facing out.
  1. Install the vacuum hose (A) and transmission dipstick (B).
  1. Install the intake manifold cover.
  1. Connect the positive cable to the battery first, then connect the negative cable.
  1. Install the battery trim.
  1. Start the engine to make sure the starter works properly.
  1. Enter the anti-theft codes for the radio and the navigation system, then enter the customer's audio presets.
  1. Do the power window control unit reset procedure.
  1. Do the steering column position memorization procedure.
  1. Set the clock.
