Steering Gearbox Removal - HPS type
Special Tools Required

Ball joint remover, 32 mm
 07MAC-SL00101
Note these items during removal:
  • Using solvent and a brush, wash any oil and dirt off the valve body unit, its lines, and the end of the steering gearbox. Blow dry with compressed air.
  • Make sure to remove the steering wheel before disconnecting the steering joint. Damage to the cable reel can occur.
  • The illustration shows LHD model, RHD model is symmetrical.
  1. Remove the right and left front fender trim (A).
  1. Drain the power steering fluid.
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is LOCK (0), then do the battery terminal disconnection procedure.
  1. Remove the relay box (A), then release the wire harness clips.
  1. Remove the front tower bar.
  1. Raise the front of vehicle, and support it with safety stands in the proper locations.
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Remove the steering wheel.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard undercover and driver's dashboard lower cover.
  1. Remove the steering joint cover (A).
  1. Remove the steering joint bolts (A), then disconnect the steering joint (B) from the pinion shaft and the steering column shaft.
    NOTE: Do not turn the steering column shaft after disconnect the steering joint. If turn the column shaft do the VSA sensor neutral position memorization after install the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the center guide (A).
  1. Remove the lower steering joint cover (B). Be careful not to damage the mating surface on the lower steering joint cover and the pinion shaft grommet. Replace the cover seal (C) if necessary.
  1. Remove the pinion shaft grommet B from top of the valve body unit.
  1. Apply vinyl tape (A) to the splines on the pinion shaft.
  1. Remove and discard the cotter pin (A) from the tie-rod end ball joint nut (B), and loosen the nut.
  1. Separate the tie-rod ball joint and knuckle using the ball joint remover.
  1. Remove the nuts (A) from the rear engine mount.
  1. Remove the rear engine mount (A) and the engine mount vacuum hose holder mounting bolt (B) on the steering gearbox stiffener.
  1. Disconnect the engine mount vacuum hose (C) from the rear engine mount.
  1. Remove the shift cable mounting bolt (A) on the steering gearbox stiffener.
  1. LHD model: Remove the feed line holder mounting bolt (B), then disconnect the return hose from the clamp (C).
  1. Remove the feed line holder mounting bolt (A) and return line holder mounting bolt (B) on the steering gearbox.
  1. Disconnect the stepping valve motor connector (A).
  1. LHD model: Place several shop towels under the line connections, and cover the gearbox mounting part to protect it from the power steering fluid. Loosen the adjustable hose clamp (A), and disconnect the return hose (B).
  1. LHD model: Loosen the 14 mm flare nut (C), and disconnect the feed line (D).
  1. LHD model: Remove the return line joint (A) from the steering gearbox.
  1. RHD model: Place several shop towels under the line connections, and cover the gearbox mounting part to protect it from the power steering fluid. Loosen the flare nuts (A), then disconnect the feed line (B) and return line (C).
  1. Remove the P/S heat baffle plate (A).
  1. Remove the steering gearbox stiffener (A) and the dynamic damper (B) between the steering gearbox stiffener and front suspension subframe.
  1. Remove the steering gearbox mounting bolt (A) and washer (B) on the passenger's side gearbox mount.
  1. Remove the steering gearbox mounting bolt (A) and washer (B) on the driver's side gearbox mount.
  1. LHD model: Remove the 4-way brake line joint mounting bolts (A).
  1. Move the steering gearbox to the passenger's side, and rotate it so the pinion shaft points toward the front of the vehicle.
  1. Carefully move the steering gearbox as an assembly toward the driver's side until the pinion shaft clears the wheelwell opening. Be careful not to damage the brake lines with the pinion shaft.
  1. Lift the passenger's side of the gearbox, and remove the steering gearbox through the wheelwell opening on the driver's side as shown by the arrow.
  1. After removing the steering gearbox, make sure that no power steering fluid gets on the gearbox mount cushions, gearbox housing, surface of the front suspension subframe, and stiffener. Wipe off any spilled fluid at once.
