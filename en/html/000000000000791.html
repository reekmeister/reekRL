DTC Troubleshooting: B1005
DTC B1005:
 MICU Lost Communication with Relay Control Module
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Wait for 6 seconds.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1005 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the MICU and the relay control module are OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections shown:
LHD type
  • The relay control module connector J (10P)
  • The driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connector D (17P)
RHD type
  • The relay control module connector J (10P)
  • The driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connector X (39P)
  • The passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box connector B (10P)
  • The passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box connector E (14P)
If the connections are good, check the battery condition and the charging system.■
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1158 also indicated?
YES -
Go to Relay Control Module Input Test. ■
NO -
Check for an open in the communication circuit between the MICU and the relay control module. If the circuit is OK, go to the MICU input test. If the circuit is bad, repair the open.■
