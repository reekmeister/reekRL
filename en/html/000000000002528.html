TWC (Warm Up) Removal/Installation
FRONT
  1. Remove the engine cover.
  1. Remove the condenser fan shroud.
  1. Remove the No. 2 ignition coil and the ignition coil heat insulator.
  1. Remove the front A/F sensor (Sensor 1) and the front secondary HO2S (Sensor 2).
  1. Remove the exhaust pipe A mounting nuts (front WU-TWC side).
  1. Remove the EGR pipe.
  1. Carefully remove the front WU-TWC (A).
  1. Carefully install the front WU-TWC with a new gasket and new self-locking nuts. Tighten the nuts in a crisscross pattern in two or three steps.
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
