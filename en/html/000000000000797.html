DTC Troubleshooting: B1029
DTC B1029:
 Ultrasonic Communication Line (SENSOR COM Line) Circuit Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1029 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The ultrasonic communication line is OK at this time.■
  1. Check the No. 7 (10 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Replace the blown fuse and recheck the system.■
  1. Disconnect the alarm control siren 4P connector.
  1. Measure voltage between the No. 4 terminal of the alarm control siren 4P connector and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Repair an open in the WHT wire.■
  1. Check for continuity between the No. 1 terminal of the alarm control siren 4P connector and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Repair an open in the BLK wire or poor ground (G602) [G653].■
[ ]: RHD type
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connector P (30P).
  1. Connect the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connector P (30P) No. 9 [No. 12] terminal and body ground with a jumper wire.
    [ ]: RHD type
  1. Check for continuity between alarm control siren 4P connector No. 3 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Repair an open in the RED wire.■
  1. Check for continuity between the No. 9 [No. 12] terminal of the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connector P (30P) and body ground.
    [ ]: RHD type
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire.■
NO -
Substitute a known-good alarm control siren, and recheck. If the DTC goes away, replace the original alarm control siren. If the DTC is still indicated, go to the MICU input test. ■
