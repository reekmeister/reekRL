A/T Shift Solenoid Valve Test and Replacement
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC (A).
  1. Choose Shift Solenoid A, B, C, and D in Miscellaneous Test Menu on the HDS.
  1. Check that shift solenoid valves A, B, C, and D operate with the HDS. A clicking sound should be heard.
    • If a clicking sound is heard, the valves are OK.
    • If no clicking sound is heard, go to Step 4 .
  1. The shift solenoid test has finished if the tests are OK. If no sound is heard, remove the shift solenoid valve, and test the solenoid valve.
  1. Get the customer's radio and navigation anti-theft codes, and write down the radio presets.
  1. Remove the battery trim and left upper fender cover.
  1. Disconnect the negative terminal from the battery, then disconnect the positive terminal.
  1. Remove the battery hold-down bracket, and remove the battery and battery tray.
  1. Remove the air intake duct and air cleaner housing.
  1. Remove the battery base.
  1. Remove the nuts securing the shift cable bracket (A).
  1. Remove the spring clip/washer (B) and control pin (C), then separate the shift cable end (D) from the control lever (E). Check synthetic resin bushing (F) in the shift cable end for a proper fit and wear. If the bushing is fitted loose or worn, replace the shift cable.
  1. Disconnect the connectors from the A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve A, B, solenoid harness, transmission range switch, ATF temperature sensor, output shaft (countershaft) speed sensor, input shaft (mainshaft) speed sensor, 3rd clutch transmission fluid pressure switch, and 4th clutch transmission fluid pressure switch.
  1. Remove the bolt securing the harness cover (A).
  1. Remove the A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve A and B, ATF pipes (C), and gasket (D).
  1. Replace the gasket and O-rings (E) with new ones when installing the A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve A and B.
  1. Remove the solenoid valve cover (A), dowel pins (B), and gasket (C).
  1. Replace the gasket with a new one when installing the solenoid cover.
  1. Disconnect each solenoid valve connector.
  1. Measure the resistance between each connector terminal of the shift solenoid valve and body ground.
    Standard:
    12−25 Ω

    • Replace the solenoid valve if the resistance is out of standard.
    • If the resistance is within the standard, go to Step 21 .
  1. Connect the negative battery terminal to body ground, and connect the positive battery terminal to each solenoid terminal individually.
    • If a clicking sound is heard, go to Step 22 and replace the solenoid harness.
    • If no clicking sound is heard, go to Step 23 and replace the shift solenoid valve.
  1. Remove the solenoid harness, and install a new O-ring (A) on the solenoid harness connector (B), and install the connector in the transmission housing.
  1. Remove the mounting bolts, then remove the solenoid valves.
  1. Install new O-rings (E) on each solenoid valves.
    NOTE: A new solenoid valve comes with new O-rings. If you install a new solenoid valve, use the O-rings provided on it.
  1. Install shift solenoid valve D (black connector) by holding the shift solenoid valve body; make sure the mounting bracket contacts the accumulator body.
    NOTE: Do not hold the solenoid valve connector to install the solenoid valve. Be sure to hold the solenoid valve body.
  1. Install shift solenoid valve A (black connector) by holding the shift solenoid valve body; make sure the mounting bracket contacts the accumulator body.
  1. Install shift solenoid valve C (brown connector) by holding the shift solenoid valve body; make sure the mounting bracket contacts the bracket of shift solenoid valve A.
    NOTE: Do not install shift solenoid valve C before installing shift solenoid valve A. If shift solenoid valve C is installed before installing shift solenoid valve A, it may damage the hydraulic control system.
  1. Install shift solenoid valve B (brown connector) by holding the shift solenoid valve body; make sure the mounting bracket contacts the accumulator body.
  1. Connect the YEL harness terminal to shift solenoid valve A, the GRN harness terminal to shift solenoid valve C, the RED harness terminal to shift solenoid valve B, and the ORN harness terminal to shift valve D.
  1. Install the shift solenoid valve cover, dowel pins, and a new gasket.
  1. Install the new solenoid valve body gasket on the solenoid valve cover, and install the ATF pipes with the filter end in the transmission housing. Install new O-rings over the ATF pipes.
  1. Install A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve A and B.
  1. Secure the harness cover with the bolt.
  1. Check the connectors for rust, dirt, or oil, clean or repair if necessary, then connect the connectors securely.
  1. Apply molybdenum grease to the bore hole of synthetic resin bushing in the shift cable end, and attach the shift cable end to the control lever, then insert the control pin into the control lever hole through the shift cable end, and secure the control pin with the spring clip/washer.
  1. Secure the shift cable bracket with the nuts.
  1. Install the battery base.
  1. Install the air intake duct and air cleaner housing.
  1. Install the battery tray, battery, and battery hold-down bracket, then connect the battery terminals.
  1. Install the left upper fender cover and battery trim.
  1. Enter the radio and navigation anti-theft codes, then enter the radio presets, and set the clock.
  1. Do the steering column position memorization procedures.
