DTC Troubleshooting: P0973 (7-3)
DTC P0973:
 Short in Shift Solenoid Valve A Circuit
NOTE:
  • Record all freeze data and review General Troubleshooting Information before you troubleshoot.
  • This code is caused by an electrical circuit problem and cannot be caused by a mechanical problem in the transmission.
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle for several minutes in the D position through all five gears.
  1. Check that DTC P0973 recurs.
Is DTC P0973 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Choose Shift Solenoid A in Miscellaneous Test Menu, and test the shift solenoid valve A with the HDS.
Is the system normal?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Test-drive the vehicle for several minutes in the D position through all five gears.
  1. Monitor the OBD status for P0973 in the DTCs/Freeze Data in A/T Mode Menu for a pass/fail.
Does the result indicate FAILED?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check the SH A (BLU/YEL) wire for an intermittent short between shift solenoid valve A and the PCM. If the HDS indicates NOT COMPLETED, return to Step 4 and recheck.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect PCM connectors A (31P), B (24P), and C (22P).
  1. Measure resistance between PCM connector terminals C1 and A3 or B15.
Is there less than 12 Ω?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck for DTC P0973. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good PCM, replace the original PCM, then go to Step 17 .
  1. Disconnect the solenoid harness connector at the transmission housing.
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminals C1 and A3 or B15.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between PCM connector terminal C1 and the solenoid harness connector, then go to Step 17 .
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Inspect shift solenoid valve A.
Is shift solenoid valve A OK?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Replace shift solenoid valve A, then go to Step 17 .
  1. Disconnect PCM connector D (17P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between PCM connector terminals D12 and A3 or B15.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Check for loose or poor connections at PCM connector terminal D12. If the connection is OK, update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck for DTC P0973. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good PCM, replace the original PCM, then go to Step 17 .
NO -
Check for a blown No. 29 fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box. If the fuse is OK, repair open in the wire between PCM connector terminal D12 and the under-dash fuse/relay box, then go to Step 17 .
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle for several minutes in the D position through all five gears.
  1. Monitor the OBD status for P0973 in the DTCs/Freeze Data in A/T Mode Menu for a pass/fail.
Does the result indicate PASSED?
YES -
Troubleshooting is complete.■
NO -
If the PCM was updated and the screen indicates FAILED, substitute a known-good PCM, then return to Step 17 . If the PCM was substituted and the screen indicates FAILED, return to Step 1 and recheck. If the screen indicates NOT COMPLETED, return to Step 18 .
