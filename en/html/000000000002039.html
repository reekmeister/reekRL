Fuel Tank Replacement
Except KD model
  1. Drain the fuel tank.
  1. Reinstall the fuel tank unit.
  1. Remove the propeller shaft.
  1. Remove the rear differential.
  1. Jack up the vehicle, and support it with jackstands.
  1. Remove the fuel tank covers (A) and parking brake cable bolts (B).
  1. Disconnect the hoses (C). Slide back the clamps, then twist the hoses as you pull to avoid damaging them.
  1. Place a jack, or other support, under the tank (D).
  1. Remove the strap bolts, and let the straps (E) fall free.
  1. Remove the fuel tank. If it sticks to the undercoat on its mount, carefully pry it off the mount.
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
