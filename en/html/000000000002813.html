Front Bumper Removal/Installation
POP-UP HOOD system components are located in this area. Review the POP-UP HOOD system component locations and the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
With POP-UP HOOD
NOTE:
  • Have an assistant help you when removing and installing the front bumper.
  • Take care not to scratch the front bumper and the body.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front air spoiler
    • Upper grille cover
  1. Remove the bolts (A, D), the screws (B), and the clips (C) securing the front bumper (E).
  1. Pull on the front bumper (A) at the wheel arch areas to release it from the hooks (B) on the side spacers (C).
  1. With the help of an assistant, pull the wheel arch portion away from the side spacer (A) while pulling the front bumper (B) to release the bumper from the hooks (C) on the corner upper beam (D).
  1. Disconnect the front fog light connectors (A), outside temperature sensor connector (B), and headlight washer tube (C), then remove the front bumper (D).
    NOTE: Take care not to deform the safety plate (E) by pressing or hitting it. If the safety plate is deformed, the pedestrian crash sensor mount angle will be shifted resulting in improper POP-UP HOOD system activation.
  1. If the POP-UP HOOD has been activated, replace the front bumper.
  1. Install the bumper in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Make sure the front fog light connectors and outside temperature sensor connector are plugged in properly, and the headlight washer tube is connected properly.
    • Make sure the front bumper engages the hooks (of both corner upper beams and side spacers) on each side securely.
    • If the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips and hooks into place securely.
Without POP-UP HOOD
NOTE:
  • Have an assistant help you when removing and installing the front bumper.
  • Take care not to scratch the front bumper and the body.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Front air spoiler
    • Upper grille cover
  1. Remove the bolts (A, D), screws (B), and clips (C) securing the front bumper (E).
  1. Pull on the front bumper (A) at the wheel arch areas to release it from the hooks (B) on the side spacers (C).
  1. With the help of an assistant, pull the wheel arch portion away from the side spacer (A) while pulling the front bumper (B) to release the bumper from the hooks (C) on the corner upper beam (D).
  1. Disconnect the front fog light connectors (A), outside temperature sensor connector (B), and headlight washer tube (C), then remove the front bumper (D).
  1. Release the hooks, then remove the front bumper absorber (A).
  1. Install the bumper in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Make sure the front fog light connectors and outside temperature sensor connector are plugged in properly, and the headlight washer tube is connected properly.
    • Make sure the front bumper engages the hooks (of both corner upper beams and side spacers) on each side securely.
    • If the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips and hooks into place securely.
