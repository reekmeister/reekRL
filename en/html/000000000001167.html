SRS Symptom Troubleshooting - SRS indicator stays on, but no DTCs are stored
SRS indicator stays on, but no DTCs are stored (except KD model)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and see if the PCM-FI indicator come on.
Does the PGM-FI indicator come on?
YES -
Go to Step 2 .
NO -
Go to the PGM-FI System troubleshooting.
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. In Body Electrical on the HDS, check for DTCs.
Is DTC B1187 indicated?
YES -
Go to the troubleshooting DTC B1187.
NO -
Faulty gauge control module; replace the gauge control module. ■
