Dashboard Driver's Switch Panel Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Gently pull out the driver's switch panel (A) to release the hooks (B) and detach the clips. Disconnect the power mirror switch connector (C) then remove the panel.
  1. Install the panel in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Replace any damaged clips.
    • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly.
    • Push the clips and hooks into place securely.
