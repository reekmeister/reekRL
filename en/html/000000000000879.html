POP-UP HOOD System DTC Troubleshooting: 41-2x, 41-3x, 41-7x, 41-8x, 41-9x
DTC 41-2x (41-20 to 41-29, 41-2A to 41-2F):
 Wrong Sensor is Connected to Left Side Pedestrian Crash Sensor
DTC 41-3x (41-30 to 41-39, 41-3A to 41-3F), 41-7x (41-70 to 41-79, 41-7A to 41-7F), 41-8x (41-80 to 41-89, 41-8A to 41-8F), 41-9x (41-90 to 41-99, 41-9A to 41-9F):
Faulty Left Side Pedestrian Crash Sensor
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 41-2x, 41-3x, 41-7x, 41-8x, or 41-9x indicated?
YES -
Replace the left side pedestrian crash sensor and then calibrate the left side pedestrian crash sensor. ■
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
