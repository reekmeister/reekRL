DTC Troubleshooting: B1890
DTC B1890:
 Power Trunk Closer Function Error
  1. Make sure the trunk lid closed fully.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. With the trunk lid closed fully, pull the trunk lid outer handle and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Check the closer motor operating sound.
Can you hear the sound?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Go to Step 17 .
  1. Check for the DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1890 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The power trunk system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at power trunk control unit connectors and at power trunk closer unit connector.■
  1. Check for the DTCs with the HDS.
Are DTC B1880, B1881, B1882, B1883, and/or B1884 also indicated?
YES -
Go to the indicated DTCs troubleshooting.■
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Close the trunk lid fully.
  1. Measure voltage between the power trunk control unit connector C (14P) No. 3 and No. 11 terminals while the trunk lid is opening with the keyless transmitter or the trunk lid outer handle switch.
Does it alternate between about 0 V and 5 V (Digital voltmeter will read about 2.5 V)?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
  • If there is no voltage, go to Step 13 .
  • If there is about 5 V, go to Step 15 .
  1. Close the trunk lid again.
  1. Measure voltage between the power trunk control unit connector C (14P) No. 4 and No. 11 terminals while the trunk lid is opening with the keyless transmitter or the trunk lid outer handle switch.
Does it alternate between about 0 V and 5 V (Digital voltmeter will read about 2.5 V)?
YES -
Replace the power trunk control unit .■
NO -
  • If there is no voltage, go to Step 13 .
  • If there is about 5 V, go to Step 15 .
  1. Disconnect the 10P connector from the trunk lid closer unit.
  1. Check for continuity between the No. 3 terminal and body ground, and No. 4 terminal and body ground individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short to ground in the wire (CLCL line or CLOP line) between the trunk lid closer unit and the power trunk control unit.■
NO -
Faulty close switch or open switch, replace the trunk lid closer unit.■
  1. Disconnect the 10P connector from the trunk lid closer unit.
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector C (14P) No. 3 and No. 4 terminals and the trunk lid closer unit 10P connector No. 8 and No. 9 terminals individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Faulty close switch or open switch, replace the trunk lid closer unit.■
NO -
Repair an open in the wire (CLCL line or CLOP line) between the trunk lid closer unit and the power trunk control unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Measure voltage between the power trunk control unit connector B (20P) No. 12 terminal and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 19 .
NO -
Check for No. 15 (20 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box. If the fuse is OK, check for an open in the wire between the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box and the power trunk control unit.■
  1. Disconnect the power trunk control unit connector A (5P).
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector A (5P) No. 4 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 21 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire or poor ground (G701).■
  1. Open the trunk lid.
  1. Disconnect the trunk lid closer unit 10P connector.
  1. Disconnect the power trunk control unit connector B (20P).
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector B (20P) No. 1 and No. 2 terminals and the trunk lid closer motor 10P connector No. 3 and No. 4 terminals.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Replace the trunk lid closer unit.■
NO -
Repair an open in the wire between the trunk lid closer unit and the power trunk control unit.■
