SRS DTC Troubleshooting: 42-1x
DTC 42-1x (42-10 to 42-19, 42-1A to 42-1F):
 No Signal from the Right Front Impact Sensor
Special Tools Required

SRS inflator simulator
 07SAZ-TB4011A

SRS simulator lead H
 07YAZ-S3A0100
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and check that the SRS indicator comes on for about 6 seconds and then goes off.
Does the SRS indicator stay on, and is DTC 42-1x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF. Check the connections between SRS unit connector A (28P) and the SRS unit, between the engine compartment wire harness 2P connector and the right front impact sensor, and at engine compartment wire harness 4P connector.
Are the connections OK?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Repair the poor connections and retest. If DTC 42-1x is still present, go to Step 4 .
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect the engine compartment wire harness 2P connector (A) from the right front impact sensor.
  1. Disconnect SRS unit connector A (28P) from the SRS unit.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 16 and No. 28 terminals of SRS unit connector A (28P). There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Short in the engine compartment wire harness or dashboard wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Check resistance between the No. 16 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and body ground, and between the No. 28 terminal and body ground. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Short to ground in dashboard wire harness or engine compartment wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 16 terminal of SRS unit connector A (28P) and body ground, and between the No. 28 terminal and body ground. There should be less than 1 V.
Is the voltage as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Short to power in the engine compartment wire harness or dashboard wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Connect the SRS inflator simulator (jumper connector) and simulator lead L [H] to the engine compartment wire harness 2P connector (A).
