Dashboard Center Panel Trim Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Console panel
    • Console front cover, both sides
  1. Remove the bolts securing the front of the center console (A), then pull out both sides as needed.
  1. Gently pull out along the bottom to release the clips and hooks (A), then remove the center trim (B).
  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clip portions into place securely.
