Seat Heater Test - Rear Seat
Rear Seat
  1. Remove the rear seat cushion, and the rear seat-back.
  1. Disconnect the 3P [4P] connector from the left [right] seat cushion heater, and 2P connectors from the seat-back heaters.
    [ ]: Right seat
Left Rear Seat Cushion Heater Test
  1. Check for continuity between the seat cushion heater 3P connector No. 1 and No. 2 terminals, and No. 2 and No. 3 terminals. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, replace the seat cushion heater.
Left Rear Seat-back Heater Test
  1. Check for continuity between the seat-back heater 2P connector No. 1 and No. 2 terminals. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, replace the seat-back heater.
  1. Reconnect the 3P and 2P connectors to the seat heaters, and check for continuity between the seat cushion heater 3P connector No. 1 terminal and the seat-back heater 2P connector No. 2 terminal. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, repair an open in the wire between the seat cushion heater and the seat-back heater.
Right Rear Seat Cushion Heater Test
  1. Check for continuity between the seat cushion heater 4P connector No. 2 and No. 3 terminals, and No. 3 and No. 4 terminals. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, replace the seat cushion heater.
Right Rear Seat-back Heater Test
  1. Check for continuity between the seat-back heater 2P connector No. 1 and No. 2 terminals. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, replace the seat-back heater.
  1. Reconnect the 3P and 2P connectors to the seat heaters, and check for continuity between the seat cushion heater 3P connector No. 2 terminal and the seat-back heater 2P connector No. 2 terminal. There should be continuity. If there is no continuity, repair an open in the wire between the seat cushion heater and the seat-back heater.
