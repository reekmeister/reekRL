DTC Troubleshooting: B1881
DTC B1881:
 Trunk Lid Closer Unit Close Switch Circuit Malfunction
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (ll).
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Open the trunk lid with the trunk lid outside handle.
  1. Manually close the trunk lid slowly until about half-latched position, then open it with the trunk lid outer handle switch.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1881 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The power trunk system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at power trunk control unit connectors and at trunk lid closer unit connector.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 10P connector from the trunk lid closer unit.
  1. At the trunk lid closer unit side, check for continuity between the trunk lid closer unit 10P connector No. 1 and No. 8 terminals.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Faulty close switch, replace the trunk lid closer unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Reconnect the 10P connector to the trunk lid closer unit.
  1. Disconnect the 14P connector from the power trunk control unit.
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector C (14P) No. 3 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Go to Step 14 .
  1. Disconnect the 10P connector from the trunk lid closer unit.
  1. At the trunk lid closer unit side, check for continuity between the trunk lid closer unit 10P connector No. 8 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Replace the trunk lid closer unit.■
NO -
Repair a short in the wire between the trunk lid closer unit and power trunk control unit.■
  1. Turn the trunk lid latch switch to the fully-closed position using a screwdriver.
  1. Disconnect the 20P connector from the power trunk control unit.
  1. Using jumper wires, connect battery power to the power trunk control unit connector B (20P) No. 2 terminal and ground the No. 1 terminal momentarily. (The motor should run in the close direction.)
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector C (14P) No. 3 and No. 11 terminals.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Replace the power trunk control unit .■
NO -
Go to Step 18 .
  1. Disconnect the 10P connector from the trunk lid closer unit.
  1. At the trunk lid closer unit side, check for continuity between the trunk lid closer unit 10P connector No. 1 and No. 8 terminals.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair an open in the wire between the trunk lid closer unit and power trunk control unit.■
NO -
Replace the trunk lid closer unit.■
