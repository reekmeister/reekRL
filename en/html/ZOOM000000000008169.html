DTC Troubleshooting: P0480 (101)
DTC P0480:
 RFC System Malfunction
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS, and wait 3 minutes or more.
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P0480 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Select RADIATOR FAN in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P0480 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the RFC unit, the RFC unit relay, the radiator fan motor, the condenser fan motor, and the PCM.■
  1. Check for these conditions at the radiator fan and the condenser fan:
    • Interference with fan shroud
    • Debris in the fan
    • Ice on the fan blades
Are both fans OK?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Repair or replace the damaged parts, then go to Step 52 .
  1. Select RADIATOR FAN in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
  1. Check the radiator fan and the condenser fan operation.
Do both fans operate?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Replace the motor that is not operating, then go to Step 52 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Let the engine cool until the coolant temperature is low.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), and wait 3 minutes or more.
  1. Confirm that the A/C is not switched on.
  1. Check the radiator fan and the condenser fan operation.
Do both fans keep running?
YES -
Go to Step 25 .
NO -
Go to Step 14 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Check the No. 23 IGP (7.5 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 16 .
NO -
Repair short in the wire the RFC unit relay and the No. 23 IGP (7.5 A) fuse. Also replace the No. 23 IGP (7.5 A) fuse, then go to Step 52 .
  1. Check the No. 34 (KD model), No. 35 (Except KD model) +B RAD FAN (50 A) fuse in the battery terminal fuse box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 17 .
NO -
Go to Step 31 .
  1. Disconnect the RFC unit 1P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), and wait 3 minutes or more.
  1. Measure the voltage between the RFC unit 1P connector terminal and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 20 .
NO -
Go to Step 39 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
