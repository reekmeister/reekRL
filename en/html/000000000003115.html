SH-AWD DTC Troubleshooting: 44-12
DTC 44-12:
 SH-AWD Relay Stuck ON
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Make sure the HDS communicates with vehicle and the SH-AWD control unit. If it doesn't, troubleshoot the DLC circuit.
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 44-12 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the SH-AWD relay, then test the SH-AWD relay. ■
Is the SH-AWD relay OK?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Replace the SH-AWD relay. ■
  1. Disconnect SH-AWD control unit connector A (26P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between SH-AWD control unit connector terminal A3 and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Repair short to power in the wire between the SH-AWD control unit and the SH-AWD relay (FSR line).■
NO -
Go to Step 11 .
  1. Disconnect SH-AWD control unit connector B (8P).
  1. Measure voltage between SH-AWD control unit connector terminals B1 and B2.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Repair short to power in the wire between the SH-AWD control unit and the SH-AWD relay (PWR line).■
NO -
Substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
