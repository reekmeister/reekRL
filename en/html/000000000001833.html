DTC Troubleshooting: P0815 (24-5)
DTC P0815:
 Short in Transmission Gear Selection Switch Upshift Switch Circuit, or Transmission Gear Selection Switch Upshift Switch Stuck ON
NOTE:
  • Record all freeze data and review General Troubleshooting Information before you troubleshoot.
  • This code is caused by an electrical circuit problem and cannot be caused by a mechanical problem in the transmission.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Shift to the P position.
  1. Verify the transmission gear selection switch upshift switch signal inputs with the HDS in the A/T data list.
Is UPSHIFT SW ON?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check the S-UP (BLU) wire for an intermittent short to ground between the transmission gear selection switch and PCM.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the transmission gear selection switch/park pin switch/A/T gear position indicator panel light connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Verify the transmission gear selection switch upshift switch signal inputs with the HDS in the A/T data list.
Is UPSHIFT SW ON?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Replace the transmission gear selection switch, then go to Step 12 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect PCM connector E (31P).
  1. Check for continuity between transmission gear selection switch connector terminal No. 7 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between PCM connector terminal E19 and the transmission gear selection switch, then go to Step 12 .
NO -
Check for loose or poor connections at PCM connector terminal E19. If the connection is OK, update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM, then recheck for DTC P0815. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good PCM, replace the original PCM, then go to Step 12 .
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Start the engine in the P position, wait for more than 10 seconds, then shift to the M position.
  1. Push the shift lever toward shiftup position (+) slowly. and return to neutral position; repeat this test 10 times or more.
  1. Pull the shift lever toward shiftdown position (−) slowly, and return to neutral position; repeat this test 10 times or more.
  1. Monitor the OBD status for P0815 in the DTCs/Freeze Data in A/T Mode Menu for a pass/fail.
Does the result indicate pass?
YES -
The problem has been corrected.■
NO -
If the PCM was updated and the screen indicates FAILED, substitute a known-good PCM, then return to Step 12 . If the PCM was substituted and the screen indicates FAILED, return to Step 1 and recheck. If the screen indicates NOT COMPLETED, return to Step 13 .
