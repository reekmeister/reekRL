Blower Unit Removal and Installation
  1. Remove the glove box housing.
  1. Remove the kick panel.
  1. Cut the plastic cross brace (A) in the glove box opening with diagonal cutters in the area shown. Retain plastic cross bace to be reinstalled later.
    NOTE: Use the grommets (B) and the self-tapping screws by installing plastic crosss brace.
  1. Remove the wire harness clips (A) and the connector clips (B). Remove the bolts and the glove box frame (C).
  1. Disconnect the connectors (A) from the blower motor, throttle actuator control module subharness, and dashboard wire harnesses. Remove the connector clip (B) and the wire harness clips (C).
  1. Disconnect the connector (A) from the recirculation control motor. Remove the mounting nuts, bolts, and the blower unit (B).
  1. Install the unit in the reverse order of removal. Make sure that there is no air leakage.
