Radiator and A/C Condenser Fan, Motor, and Shroud Removal and Installation
Removal
  1. Remove the right side fender trim.
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Remove the harness connectors (A), the ground cable (B), and the harness clamps (C).
  1. Loosen the A/C condenser fan shroud mounting bolts (D).
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Remove the air intake duct cover (A) and the coolant reservoir (B).
  1. Disconnect the radiator fan control (RFC) connectors (A) and the A/C condenser fan motor connector (B), then remove the harness clamps (C).
  1. Remove the radiator fan control (RFC) unit.
  1. Remove the condenser fan shroud assembly (A).
  1. Remove the radiator fan shroud assembly (B) from right side of the engine compartment.
  1. Disassemble the fan shrouds.
Installation
  1. Assemble the fan shrouds.
  1. Install the radiator fan shroud assembly (A).
  1. Install the condenser fan shroud assembly (B).
  1. Install the radiator fan control (RFC) unit.
  1. Connect the RFC connectors (A) and the A/C condenser fan motor connector (B), then install the harness clamps (C).
  1. Install the coolant reservoir (A) and the air intake duct cover (B).
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Tighten the A/C condenser fan shroud mounting bolts (A).
  1. Install the harness connectors (B), ground cable (C), and harness clamps (D).
  1. Install the splash shield.
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Install the right side fender trim.
