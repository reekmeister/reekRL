DTC 11, 13, 15, 17:

NOTE:
  • Information marked with an asterisk 1 ( *1 ) applies to the LHD type.
  • Information marked with an asterisk 2 ( *2 ) applies to the RHD type.

Does the ABS indicator come on?
YES -
Check for loose terminals or poor connections at the VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector. If connections are good, replace the VSA modulator-control unit, and recheck.■
NO -
Replace the original wheel sensor. ■
