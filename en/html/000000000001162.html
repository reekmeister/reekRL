SRS DTC Troubleshooting: 61-2x
DTC 61-2x (61-20 to 61-29, 61-2A to 61-2F):
 Short in Left Side Seat Belt Buckle Switch (KD model)
NOTE: This code may inadvertently be set if power from fuse 21 (METER IG) is lost. If this is the case, clear the code and confirm that the SRS indicator does not come back on before proceeding.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), then buckle and unbuckle the left side seat belt several times.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 61-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the Troubleshooting Index.
  1. Check the No. 21 (10 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Short to ground in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box No. 21 (10 A) fuse circuit. After No. 21 (10 A) fuse is replaced, erase the DTC memory.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the left side wire harness or driver's seat wire harness 3P connector from the left side seat belt buckle switch 3P connector (A).
  1. Buckle the left side seat belt.
    • Check resistance between the No. 1 and No. 3 terminals of the left side seat belt buckle switch 3P connector. There should be 0−1 Ω.
    • Check resistance between the No. 2 and No. 3 terminals of the same connector. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Are the resistances as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Replace the left side seat belt buckle assembly, then clear the DTC.■
  1. Unbuckle the left side seat belt.
    • Check resistance between the No. 2 and No. 3 terminals of the left side seat belt buckle switch 3P connector. There should be 0−1 Ω.
    • Check resistance between the No. 1 and No. 3 terminals of the same connector. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Are the resistances as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Replace the left side seat belt buckle assembly, then clear the DTC.■
  1. Check resistance between the No. 1 terminal of the left side seat belt buckle switch 3P connector and body ground, and between the No. 2 terminal and body ground. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Replace the left side seat belt buckle assembly. ■
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect both seat belt tensioner 4P connectors from the left side wire harness and right side wire harness.
  1. Disconnect SRS unit connector B (28P) from the SRS unit.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 2 terminal of the driver's seat wire harness 3P connector and body ground. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
    NOTE: Some resistance will be read by some meters since the MICU also monitors the seat belt buckle switch.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Short to ground in the driver's seat wire harness, left side wire harness, or multiplex control unit; replace the faulty harness or part.■
  1. Disconnect the driver's seat belt tension reducer solenoid 2P connector. Check resistance between the No. 1 terminal of the driver's seat wire harness 3P connector and body ground. There should be an open circuit or at least 1 MΩ.
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Faulty SRS unit; replace the SRS unit. ■
NO -
Short to ground in the driver's seat wire harness or left side wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
