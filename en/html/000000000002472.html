Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) DTC Troubleshooting: 63
DTC 63:
 Millimeter Wave Radar Aiming Error
NOTE: DTC 63 cannot be cleared with the HDS. To clear the DTC 63, do the millimeter radar aiming. If the millimeter wave radar aiming is complete, the DTC 63 is cleared.
  1. Do the millimeter radar aiming procedure.
  1. Test-drive the vehicle at 40 km/h (25 mph) or more, and turn the adaptive cruise control (ACC) switch ON.
  1. With the ACC activated, test-drive vehicle on a straight road at 40 km/h (25 mph) or more for 5 minutes or more.
  1. Watch the ACC indicator and CMBS indicator.
Do both indicators come on?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 63 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Troubleshoot the indicated DTC(s). If there are no DTCs, the system is OK at this time.■
  1. Remove the upper grille.
  1. Check the installation of the millimeter wave radar and the main bracket.
Is the installation OK?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Reinstall or replace the part that has a problem, then go to Step 8 .

  1. With the ACC activated, test-drive the vehicle on a straight road at 40 km/h (25 mph) or more for 5 minutes or more.
