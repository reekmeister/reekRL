DTC Troubleshooting: B1879
DTC B1879:
 Leftt Pinch Sensor Circuit Malfunction
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Manually open the trunk.
  1. Close the trunk with the keyless transmitter or the inside trunk lid switch.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1879 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The power trunk system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at power trunk control unit connectors and left pinch sensor connector.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector from the left pinch sensor.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between the harness side of the left pinch sensor 2P connector No. 2 (+) and No. 1 (−) terminals.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Replace the left pinch sensor. ■
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 20P connector from the power trunk control unit.
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector B (20P) No. 16 terminal and the left pinch sensor 2P connector No. 2 terminal.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire.■
