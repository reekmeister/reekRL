POP-UP HOOD System Precautions and Procedures
General Precautions
Please read the following precautions carefully before performing the POP-UP HOOD system service. Observe the instructions described in this manual, or the airbags could accidentally deploy and cause damage or injuries.
  • Except when performing electrical inspections, always turn the ignition switch OFF, ground the SCS line with the HDS to take the PCM out of active status, disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
    NOTE: The POP-UP HOOD system memory is not erased even if the ignition switch is turned OFF or the battery cables are disconnected from the battery.
  • Use replacement parts which are manufactured to the same standards and quality as the original parts. Do not install used POP-UP HOOD system parts. Use only new parts when making POP-UP HOOD system repairs.
  • Carefully inspect any POP-UP HOOD system part before you install it. Do not install any part that shows signs of being dropped or improperly handled, such as dents, cracks or deformation.
  • Before removing any of the POP-UP HOOD system parts (including the disconnection of the connectors), always disconnect the POP-UP HOOD system connector.
  • Use only a digital multimeter to check the system. If it is not a Honda multimeter, make sure its output is 10 mA (0.01 A) or less when switched to the lowest value in the ohmmeter range. A tester with a higher output could cause accidental deployment and possible injury.
  • Do not put objects on the front passenger's airbag.
  • The original audio and navigation system have a coded theft protection circuit. Be sure to get the customer's anti-theft codes and write down the audio presets before disconnecting the negative cable from the battery.
  • Before returning the vehicle to the customer, enter the audio code, the navigation code, then enter the audio presets; set the clock.
Safety Plate Precautions
  • Bumper beam with built-in safety plate is installed in the front bumper inside to relax a bent shock of a pedestrian when a vehicle collided with a pedestrian.
  • Because it is set for a purpose to let you relax a shock by the strength that it is easy to transform safety plate, be careful to the handling at the time of replacement.
Power Unit Component Precautions
As for the power unit components, it is attached the bracket which limited actuation for the case that malfunctioned by any chance. As for the pack, it is applied exclusive box. By doing this, conveyance of a product and safety of a pack condition are conserved.Please detach the bracket when you use power unit components.
In a recovery of a power unit components of non-deployment, please assemble the bracket which disengaged from new article power unit components to return product and send it back by exclusive box.
Power Unit Component Handling and Storage
Do not disassemble a power unit component. It has no serviceable parts. Once a power unit component has been deployed, it cannot be repaired or reused. For temporary storage of the power unit component during service, please observe the following precautions.
  • Store the removed power unit component with the deployment surface up. Never put anything on the removed power unit component.
  • To prevent damage to the power unit component, keep it away from any oil, grease, detergent, or water.
  • Store the removed power unit component on a secure, flat surface away from any high heat source (exceeding 90 °C/200 °F).
  • Never perform electrical inspections to the power unit component, such as measuring resistance.
  • Do not turn a face and a body to the side that a rod leaves.
  • For proper disposal of a damaged power unit component, refer to power unit component disposal.
Wiring Precautions
Observe the following instructions.
  • Never attempt to modify, splice, or repair POP-UP HOOD system wiring. If there is an open or damage in POP-UP HOOD system wiring or terminals, replace the harness.
  • Be sure to install the harness wires so they do not get pinched or interfere with other parts.
  • Make sure all POP-UP HOOD system ground locations are clean and securely fastened for optimum metal-to-metal contact. Poor grounding can cause intermittent problems that are difficult to diagnose.
Precautions for Electrical Inspections
POP-UP HOOD unit connector
When using electrical test equipment, insert the probe of the tester into the check tool. Do not insert the probe of the tester into the terminal, and do not tamper with the connector. Use a shaped probe.
Power unit component and pedestrian crash sensor connectors
  • Connect the simulator lead E to the power unit component, and probe is contacted to simulator lead.
  • Connect the simulator lead H to the pedestrian crash sensor, and probe is contacted to simulator lead.
Other connectors
  • When using electrical test equipment, insert the probe of the tester into the wire side of the connector. Do not insert the probe of the tester into the terminal side of the connector, and do not tamper with the connector.
  • Use an U-shaped probe. Do not insert the probe forcibly.
  • Use specified service connectors in troubleshooting.
    Using improper tools could cause an error in inspection due to poor metal-to-metal contact.
Spring-loaded Lock Connector
Some POP-UP HOOD system connectors have a spring-loaded lock.

Pedestrian Crash Sensor Connectors

Disconnecting
To release the lock, pull the spring-loaded sleeve (A) and the slider (B) while holding the opposite half of the connector. Then pull the connector halves apart. Be sure to pull on the sleeve and not on the connector half.
Connecting
Hold both connector halves, and press them firmly together until the projection (C) of the sleeve-side connector clicks.
