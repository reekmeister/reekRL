Glove Box Removal/Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Remove the passenger's undercover.
  1. Open the glove box (A) and document tray (B). Remove the lid (C), then remove the screws (D) and bolt (E).
  1. Close the glove box (A), then remove the bolts.
  1. Remove the glove box (A).
    -1.
    Pull out the glove box, then remove the clips.
    -2.
    Remove the harness clip (B) and the ambient light bulb socket (C).
    -3.
    Disconnect the trunk lid opener main switch connector (D).
  1. Install the box in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly, and the bulb socket is connected securely.
    • Take care not to scratch the passenger's front console cover.
