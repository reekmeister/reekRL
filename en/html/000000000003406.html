Hands Free Telephone System Symptom Troubleshooting - No sound when Bluetooth audio communicates
No sound when Bluetooth audio communicates
NOTE:
  • Check the vehicle battery condition first.
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  • Set the fader and balance positions to the center.
  • Check if the audio device functions normally.
  • Check if the new registration of bluetooth device has been completed.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Turn on the audio unit, and check for sound in each mode (AM, FM, and CD).
Is the sound is normal?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Go to no sound is heard from speakers. ■
  1. Press the DISC/AUX button, and check if it changes to Bluetooth Audio source.
Does it change to Bluetooth Audio source?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Substitute a known-good audio unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original audio unit.
  1. Check if it is connected when switching to Bluetooth Audio screen by pressing the DISC/AUX button.

YES -

  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Check if the Bluetooth audio device can be played.
Does the Bluetooth audio play?
YES -
Internal malfunction in unit where a connector for the USB adapter unit is connected.
NO -
Go to Step 19 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect the Hands Free Telephone control unit 28P connector.
  1. Reconnect USB adapter unit connector A (14P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Check the operation of the reconnected unit.
Is the system normal?
YES -
Substitute a known-good Hands Free Telephone control unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original Hands Free Telephone control unit. ■
NO -
Substitute a known-good audio unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original audio unit. ■
