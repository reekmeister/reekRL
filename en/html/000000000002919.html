Under-dash Fuse/Relay Box (Passenger's) Removal/Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations, (Except KD model), (KD model) and precautions and procedures before performing repairs or servicing.
Removal
  1. Do the battery terminal disconnection procedure.
  1. Remove the right front door sill trim and right kick panel.
  1. Disconnect the connectors from the passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box (A).
  1. Remove the mounting bolts and the passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Carefully remove the relays by prying under the base of the relay.
    NOTE: Do not use pliers. Pliers will damage the relays, which could cause the engine to stall or not start.
Installation
  1. Install the passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Install the relays and connectors to the passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Install the removed parts in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Do the battery terminal reconnection procedure.
  1. Confirm that all systems work properly.
