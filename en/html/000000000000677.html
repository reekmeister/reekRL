Resetting the Power Window Control Unit
Resetting the power windows are required when any of the following have occurred:
  • Power window regulator replacement or repair
  • Power window motor replacement or repair
  • Window run channel replacement or repair
    • -
    Driver's MPCS unit replacement or repair
    -
    Passenger's MPCS unit replacement or repair
    -
    Rear window switch replacement
  • Door glass replacement or repair
  • Power is removed from a power window control unit while the power window timer is ON.

Using the HDS
  1. Connect the Honda Diagnostic System (HDS) to the vehicle's DLC.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), then enter the vehicle's VIN and mileage at the prompts.
  1. Select ‘‘BODY ELECTRICAL'' from the ‘‘SYSTEM SELECTION'' menu.
  1. From the ‘‘BODY ELECTRICAL SYSTEM SELECT'' menu, select ‘‘POWER WINDOWS''.
  1. From the ‘‘MODE'' menu, select ‘‘ADJUSTMENTS''.
  1. From the ‘‘ADJUSTMENT'' menu, select ‘‘WINDOW P RESET'' for driver's side (passenger's side) window.
  1. Follow the prompts on the screen.
  1. Confirm that the power window control unit is reset by using the window AUTO UP and AUTO DOWN function.
Without the HDS
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Move the window all the way down by using the window DOWN switch.
  1. Open the driver's door.
    NOTE: Step 4 −7 must be done within 5 seconds of each other.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Push and hold the window DOWN switch.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Release the window DOWN switch.
  1. Repeat Step 4 −7 three more times.
  1. Wait 1 second.
  1. Confirm that AUTO UP and AUTO DOWN do not work. If AUTO UP and DOWN work, go back to Step 1 .
  1. Move the window all the way down by using the window DOWN switch.
  1. Pull up and hold the window UP switch until the window reaches the fully closed position, then continue to hold the switch for 1 second.
  1. Confirm that the power window control unit is reset by using the window AUTO UP and AUTO DOWN function.
    If the window still does not work in AUTO, repeat the procedure several times, paying close attention to the 5 second time limit between steps.
