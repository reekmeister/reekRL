SRS DTC Troubleshooting: 21-1x, 21-2x
DTC 21-1x (21-10 to 21-19, 21-1A to 21-1F):
 Open or Increased Resistance in Left Side Seat Belt Tensioner
DTC 21-2x (21-20 to 21-29, 21-2A to 21-2F):
 Increased Resistance in Left Side Seat Belt Tensioner
Special Tools Required

SRS inflator simulator
 07SAZ-TB4011A

SRS simulator lead F
 07XAZ-SZ30100
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and check that the SRS indicator comes on for about 6 seconds and then goes off.
Does the SRS indicator stay on, and is DTC 21-1x or 21-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect the left side wire harness 4P connector (A) from the left side seat belt tensioner.
  1. Connect the SRS inflator simulator (2 Ω connectors) and simulator lead F to the left side wire harness.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 21-1x or 21-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Open or increased resistance in the left side seat belt tensioner; replace the left side seat belt. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 3 minutes.
  1. Disconnect the right side seat belt tensioner connector.
  1. Disconnect SRS unit connector B (28P) from the SRS unit.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 1 and the No. 2 terminals of SRS unit connector B (28P). There should be 2.0−3.0 Ω.
Is resistance as specified?
YES -
Faulty SRS unit or poor connection at SRS unit connector B (28P) and the SRS unit. Check the connection; if the connection is OK, replace the SRS unit. ■
NO -
Go to Step 13
  1. Disconnect right side wire harness 16P connector (A) from left side wire harness connector (B).
  1. Check resistance between the No. 4 [No. 2]and No. 5 [No. 3] terminals of the left side wire harness 16P connector. There should be 2.0−3.0 Ω. []: RHD type
Is the resistance as specified?
YES -
Open or increased resistance in the right side wire harness; replace the right side wire harness.■
NO -
Open or increased resistance in the left side wire harness; replace the left side wire harness.■
