Front Bumper Safety Plate Replacement - With POP-UP HOOD System
POP-UP HOOD system components are located in this area. Review the POP-UP HOOD system component locations and the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
With POP-UP HOOD
NOTE:
  • Have an assistant help you when removing and installing the front bumper safety plate.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the audio and the navigation system, then write down the audio presets.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Remove the front bumper.
  1. Disconnect the pedestrian crash sensor subharness connector (A).
  1. Remove the bolts, then remove the front bumper beam/safety plate assembly (A).
    NOTE: Take care not to deform the safety plate by pressing or hitting it. If the safety plate is deformed, the pedestrian crash sensor mount angle will be shifted resulting in improper POP-UP HOOD system activation.
  1. If the POP-UP HOOD has been activated, replace the front bumper beam/safety plate assembly.
  1. Install the front bumper beam/safety plate assembly in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Special corrosion-resistant bolts are used in this area. The use of any other type of hardware may result in accelerated corrosion.
    • Make sure the pedestrian crash sensor subharness connector is plugged in properly.
    • Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
    • Calibrate the pedestrian crash sensor.
    • Enter the anti-theft codes for the audio and the navigation system, then enter the audio presets.
    • Check for any DTCs that may have been set during repairs, and clear them.
    • Do the steering column position memorization.
