Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) DTC Troubleshooting: 4, 5
LKAS Camera DTC 4:
 Lost Communication with LKAS Control Unit
LKAS Camera DTC 5:
 Reception Error with LKAS Control Unit
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then turn it ON (II).
  1. Watch the adaptive cruise control (ACC)/LKAS indicator.
Does the adaptive cruise control (ACC)/LKAS indicator come on?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 4 or 5 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Troubleshoot the indicated DTC.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the LKAS camera 10P connector and LKAS control unit 26P connector.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between body ground and the LKAS camera 10P connector terminal No. 3 and No. 4 individually.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Repair short to power in the wire between the LKAS camera and LKAS control unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and the LKAS camera 10P connector terminal No. 3 and No. 4 individually.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short to body ground in the wire between the LKAS camera and LKAS control unit.■
NO -
Go to Step 11 .
  1. Check for continuity between LKAS camera 10P connector terminal No. 4 and LKAS contorl unit 26P connector terminal No. 11.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the LKAS camera and LKAS control unit.■
  1. Check for continuity between LKAS camera 10P connector terminal No. 3 and LKAS control unit 26P connector terminal No. 24.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Check for loose terminals in the LKAS camera 10P connector and LKAS control unit 26P connector. If all connections are OK, replace the LKAS camera and LKAS control unit as a set.
NO -
