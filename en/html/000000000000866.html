DTC Troubleshooting: B1888
DTC B1888:
 Power Trunk Motor Circuit Malfunction
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Open the trunk lid fully.
  1. Close the trunk lid automatically with the keyless transmitter or the inside trunk lid switch.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1888 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The power trunk system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at power trunk control unit connectors and at drive unit connectors.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the 2P connector from the drive unit.
  1. Disconnect the 5P connector from the power trunk control unit.
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector A (5P) No. 3 and No. 5 terminals and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair a short in the wire.■
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Using jumper wires, connect battery power to the power trunk control unit connector A (5P) No. 3 (or No. 5) terminal and ground the No. 5 or (No. 3) terminal.
  1. Check the motor operation.
Does the motor run normally?
YES -
Go to Step 12 .
NO -
Faulty power trunk motor, replace the drive unit.■
  1. Measure voltage between the power trunk control unit connector A (5P) No. 2 terminal and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Check the No. 35 (30 A) fuse in the battery terminal fuse box. If the fuse is OK, check for an open in the wire between the battery terminal fuse box and the power trunk control unit.■
  1. Check for continuity between the power trunk control unit connector A (5P) No. 1 terminal and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Repair an open in the wire or poor ground (G653).■
  1. Disconnect the 20P connector from the power trunk control unit.
  1. Measure voltage between the power trunk control unit connector B (20P) No. 10 and No. 19 terminals.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Replace the power trunk control unit.■
NO -
Check the No. 7 (10 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box. If the fuse is OK, check for an open in the wire between the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box and the power trunk control unit.■
