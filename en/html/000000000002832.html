Center Console Removal/Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the front seat, dashboard, and related parts.
  1. Detach the clips by pulling the center console panel (A) up. Disconnect the seat heater switch connectors or seat ventilation switch connectors (B), cigarette lighter connector (C), and cigarette lighter light connector (D).
  1. Remove the center console trim (A).
    -1.
    Open the console armrest.
    -2.
    Open the beverage holder lid (B), pull the front portion of the trim up by hand to detach the clips and the hooks (C), then remove it.
  1. Pull the beverage holder (A) up by hand to detach the clips (B, C), then remove it.
  1. Remove the accessory trim (A).
    -1.
    Remove the console box mat (B).
    -2.
    Remove the screws and release the hooks (C), then remove the trim.
    -3.
    Disconnect the rear accessory power socket connector (D), ambient light connector (E), and USB jack connector (F).
  1. Detach the clips and the hook (A) by pulling the driver's front console cover (B) and passenger's front console cover (C) out, and pull them backward to release their pins (D) from the heater unit, then remove them.
LHD model
RHD model
  1. Remove the center console (A).
    -1.
    Disconnect the subharness connector (B), and detach it from the vent duct.
    -2.
    Remove the bolts (C, D).
    -3.
    Pull out the console backward, then remove it.
    NOTE: Take care not to deform the rear vent duct (E) while removing and storing the console.
  1. Install the console in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • If the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, replace them with new ones.
    • Make sure the connectors are plugged in properly.
    • When installing the console mounting bolts, first install the front bolts in the bottom of the center console trim.
    • After installing the front console covers, make sure the front pins of the covers are installed securely by moving the front edge of the panels to the outside.
