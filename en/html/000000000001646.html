Upper Grille Replacement
With POP-UP HOOD: POP-UP HOOD system components are located in this area. Review the POP-UP HOOD system component locations and the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the bumper and grille.
  • With adaptive cruise control: The millimeter wave rader should be kept from any damage while removing or installing the upper grille.
  1. Remove the upper grille cover.
  1. Pull out the upper grille (A) and detach the clips, then remove the upper grille. Take care not to drop the clips inside the front bumper (B).
  1. With POP-UP HOOD: If the POP-UP HOOD has been activated, replace the upper grille.
  1. If necessary, remove the screws, then remove the emblem base (without adaptive cruise control) or the front ‘‘H'' emblem (with adaptive cruise control) (A), both rader covers (B) (LEGEND), and upper grille molding (C) from the upper grille base (D).
LEGEND
RL
  1. LEGEND: If necessary, remove the screws, then remove both upper grille upper moldings.
  1. Install the grille in the reverse order of removal. Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
