Rear Seat Belt Replacement
Rear Seat Belt
NOTE: Check the rear seat belts for damage, and replace them if necessary.
  1. Remove the rear seat cushion.
  1. Remove the lower anchor bolt (A).
  1. Remove the rear seat-back.
  1. Remove the rear pillar trim from both sides.
  1. Remove the rear shelf.
  1. Remove the retractor mounting bolt (A) and the retractor bolt (B), then remove the rear seat belt (C) and retractor (D).
  1. Install the seat belt in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the anchor bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Check that the retractor locking mechanism functions as described.
    • Before installing the anchor bolt, make sure there are no twists or kinks in the seat belt.
Center Seat Belt and Seat Belt Buckles
NOTE: Check the rear seat belts for damage, and replace them if necessary.
  1. Remove the rear seat cushion.
  1. Except KD model: Disconnect the right seat belt buckle switch connector (A), and detach the connector (B) from the rear seat-back frame (C).
  1. Remove the center anchor bolts (A), then remove the seat belt buckles (B) and center seat belt buckle (C). Take care not to damage the left seat belt buckle and center seat belt buckle switch harness (D) (except KD model).
  1. Remove the rear seat-back. Take care not to damage the rear seat belts, center seat belt, and seat belt buckles.
  1. Excpt KD model: Disconnect the left seat belt buckle and center seat belt buckle switch connector (A).
  1. Release the lock washer (A), then remove the anchor bolt (B), spring washer (C), center seat belt (D), and right seat belt buckle (E).
  1. Remove the rear pillar trim from both sides.
  1. Remove the rear shelf.
  1. Remove the retractor mounting ET screw (A) and the retractor bolt (B), then remove the rear seat belt (C) and retractor (D).
  1. Remove the rear center seat belt protector (A).
  1. Install the seat belt and buckles in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the anchor bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Check that the retractor locking mechanism functions as described.
    • Assemble the washers on the retractor bolt as shown.
    • Before installing the center anchor bolt, make sure there are no twists or kinks in the center belt.
    • Make sure the rear center ELR (emergency locking retractor) is pointing straight forward.
    • Except KD model: Before attaching the rear seat-back, make sure the rear seat belt buckle switch wire harnesses are not pinched.
    • Except KD model: Make sure the buckle switch connectors are plugged in properly.
