DTC Troubleshooting: P0139 (161), P0159 (162)
DTC P0139:
 Rear Secondary HO2S (Bank 1, Sensor 2) Circuit Slow Response
DTC P0159:
 Front Secondary HO2S (Bank 2, Sensor 2) Circuit Slow Response
NOTE:
  • Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  • Information marked with an asterisk ( * ) applies to the front bank (Bank 2).
  • Information marked with an asterisk ( *1 ) applies to KD model.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 3,000 rpm (min −1 ) without load (in P or N) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.
  1. Test-drive under these conditions:
    • Engine coolant temperature (ECT SENSOR (ETC SENSOR 1) *1 ) above 70 °C (158 °F)
    • Transmission in D
    • Vehicle speed between 56−88 km/h (35−55 mph)
    • Drive 5 minutes or more
  1. Monitor the OBD STATUS for DTC P0139 and/or P0159 * in the DTCs MENU with the HDS.
Does the screen indicate FAILED?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
If the screen indicates PASSED, intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) and the PCM. If the screen indicates EXECUTING, keep driving until a result comes on. If the screen indicates OUT OF CONDITION, go to Step 3 and recheck.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Replace the secondary HO2S (Sensor 2).
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Reset the PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 3,000 rpm (min −1 ) without load (in P or N) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.
  1. Test-drive under these conditions:
    • Engine coolant temperature (ECT SENSOR (ECT SENSOR 1) *1 ) above 70 °C (158 °F)
    • Transmission in D
    • Vehicle speed between 56−88 km/h (35−55 mph)
    • Drive 5 minutes or more
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P0139 and/or P0159 * indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) and the PCM, then go to Step 1 .
NO -
Go to Step 14 .
  1. Monitor the OBD STATUS for DTC P0139 and/or P0159 * in the DTCs MENU with the HDS.
Does the screen indicate PASSED?
YES -
Troubleshooting is complete. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs were indicated in Step 13 , go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
NO -
If the screen indicates FAILED, check for poor connections or loose terminals at the secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) and the PCM, then go to Step 1 . If the screen indicates EXECUTING, keep driving until a result comes on. If the screen indicates OUT OF CONDITION, go to Step 11 .
