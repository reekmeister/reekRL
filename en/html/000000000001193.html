A/T Interlock System - Shift Lock Solenoid Replacement
  1. Remove the shift lever assembly.
  1. Remove the A/T gear position indicator panel light socket (A), and remove the indicator panel from the shift lever bracket.
  1. Remove the shift lock release (B).
  1. Release the lock tab (C) retaining the shift lock solenoid to slide the shift lock solenoid out.
  1. Install the shift lock solenoid plunger (D) and plunger spring (E) in the new shift lock solenoid (F).
  1. Apply silicon grease to the tip of the shift lock stop (G), if necessary.
  1. Install the new shift lock solenoid assembly by aligning the joint of the shift lock solenoid plunger with the tip of the shift lock stop.
  1. Install the shift lock release.
  1. Install the A/T gear position indicator panel on the shift lever bracket, and install the indicator panel light socket in the indicator panel.
  1. Install the shift lever assembly.
