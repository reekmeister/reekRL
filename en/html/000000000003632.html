Keyless/Power Door Locks/Security System (MICU, Driver's and Passenger's MPCS Unit, Left and Right Rear Window Switches, Keyless Converter Unit, and Keyless Receiver Unit) Input Test
NOTE: Before testing, make sure the hazard warning lights work properly.
MICU
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the driver's side kick panel.
  1. Disconnect driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connectors E, F, G, K, N, P, and Q.
    NOTE: All connector views are wire side of female terminals.
  1. Inspect the connector and socket terminals to be sure they are all making good contact.
    • If the terminals are bent, loose or corroded, repair them as necessary and recheck the system.
    • If the terminals look OK, go to Step 5 .
  1. With the connectors still disconnected, make these input tests at the connectors.
    • If any test indicates a problem, find and correct the cause, then recheck the system.
    • If all the input tests prove OK, go to Step 6 .
    Cavity
    Wire
    Test condition
    Test: Desired result
    Possible cause if result is not obtained
