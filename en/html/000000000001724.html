Front Seat Ventilation Blower Motor/Duct Replacement
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
Seat-back Blower Motor/Duct (For Some Models)
NOTE:
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove the seat-back.
  1. Remove the seat-back cover and pad.
  1. Disconnect the connector (A), then remove the blower motor/duct (B) from the seat-back frame.
  1. Remove the clips, then separate the duct (A, B) and blower motor (C).
  1. Install the blower motor/duct in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly.
Seat Cushion Rear Blower Motor/Duct (For Some Models)
NOTE:
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove the front seat cushion cover.
  1. Disconnect the blower motor connector (A), then remove the connector from the bracket.
  1. Release the wire bands (A) and clips (B) securing the blower duct (C) to the seat cushion springs (D).
  1. Remove the screws, and release the hooks (A) from cushion frame wire, then remove the blower motor/duct (B).
  1. Remove the clips, then separate the duct (A) and blower motor (B).
  1. Install the blower motor/duct in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips securing the duct and motor, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Replace the wire bands and clips you removed with new ones. Install the clips with commercially available upholstery ring pliers.
    • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly.
Seat Cushion Front Blower Motor (For Some Models)
NOTE:
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove the front seat cushion cover.
  1. Disconnect the blower motor connector (A) and remove the screws, then remove the blower motor (B) from the duct.
  1. Install the blower motor in the reverse order of removal, and make sure the connector is plugged in properly.
Seat Cushion Front Duct (For Some Models)
NOTE:
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove the seat-back.
  1. Remove the front seat cushion cover/pad.
  1. Remove the screws, then remove the duct (A).
  1. Install the duct in the reverse order of removal.
