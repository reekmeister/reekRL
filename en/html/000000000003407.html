Hands Free Telephone System Symptom Troubleshooting - Bluetooth audio cannot communicate
Bluetooth audio cannot communicate
NOTE:
  • Check the vehicle battery condition first.
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  • Check if the audio device functions normally.
  • Make sure the audio device is registered before troubleshooting. If the audio device can not be registered, see Bluetooth audio device can not be registered troubleshooting.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Turn on the audio unit, and press AUX button to select Bluetooth audio source.
Does it change to Bluetooth Audio source?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Substitute a known-good audio unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original audio unit.
  1. Check if the Bluetooth audio device can be played.
Does the Bluetooth audio play?
YES -
Intermittent failure. The audio unit is OK at this time.■
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Check if there is abnormality in the registered Bluetooth phone or if it is a compatible model.
  1. Register and check Bluetooth audio device function on a known-good vehicle equipped with Bluetooth audio.
Is the Bluetooth phone normal?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Defective Bluetooth phone.
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC. Check for HFL DTCs.
Are there any DTCs indicated?
YES -
Go to HFL DTC troubleshooting. ■
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Go into the Diagnostic mode, and use the System Links diagnosis.
Is the Display icon or Audio icon red?
YES -
Check for error code to the Error History. ■
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Disconnect the navigation display unit 20P connector and Hands Free Telephone control unit 28P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the navigation display unit 20P connector and the Hands Free Telephone control unit 28P connector according to the table.
    Hands Free Telephone control unit connector
    Navigation display unit connector
    Wire color
    No. 4
    No. 5
    GRN
    No. 5
    No. 15
    RED

Is there continuity?
YES -
Substitute a known-good Hands Free Telephone control unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original Hands Free Telephone control unit. ■
NO -
Repair short in the wire(s) between the Hands Free Telephone control unit 28P connector and the navigation display unit. Replace the affected shield harness.■
