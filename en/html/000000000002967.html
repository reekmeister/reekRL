DTC Troubleshooting: P0404 (12)
DTC P0404:
 EGR Control Circuit Range/Performance Problem
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 3,000 rpm (min −1 ) without load (in P or N) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.
  1. Do the EGR TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
Is the result OK?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Clean any carbon build-up on the EGR valve with throttle plate and induction cleaner.■
NO -
Go to Step 5 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect the EGR valve 5P connector.
  1. At the EGR valve side, measure the resistance between EGR valve 5P connector terminals No. 2 and No. 3.
Is there 100 kΩ or more?
YES -
Go to Step 25 .
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. At the EGR valve side, measure the resistance between EGR valve 5P connector terminals No. 2 and No. 4.
Is there 100 kΩ or more?
YES -
Go to Step 25 .
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Check for continuity between EGR valve 5P connector terminal No. 5 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the EGR valve and G101, then go to Step 26 .
  1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS.
  1. Disconnect PCM connector B (24P).
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminal B5 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between the PCM (B5) and the EGR valve, then go to Step 27 .
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Connect EGR valve 5P connector terminal No. 1 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between PCM connector terminal B5 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 15 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the PCM (B5) and the EGR valve, then go to Step 27 .
  1. Remove the EGR valve.
  1. Clean any carbon build-up on the EGR valve with Honda genuine throttle plate and induction cleaner.
  1. Install the EGR valve.
  1. Reconnect the EGR valve 5P connector.
  1. Reconnect PCM connector B (24P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Reset the PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 3,000 rpm (min −1 ) without load (in P or N) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.
  1. Do the EGR TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
Is the result OK?
YES -
Go to Step 33 .
NO -
Go to Step 25 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Replace the EGR valve.
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Reset the PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 3,000 rpm (min −1 ) without load (in P or N) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.
  1. Do the EGR TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P0404 indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the EGR valve and the PCM, then go to Step 1 . If the connections and terminals are OK, go to Step 34 .
NO -
Troubleshooting is complete. If any other Temporary DTCs or are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
