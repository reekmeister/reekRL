EVAP Canister Replacement
KD model
  1. Remove the propeller shaft.
  1. Remove the rear differential.
  1. Disconnect the connectors (A) and the hoses (B).
  1. Remove the EVAP canister (A).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
Except KD model
  1. Remove the propeller shaft.
  1. Remove the rear differential.
  1. Disconnect the hoses (A).
  1. Remove the EVAP canister (B).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
