Front Seat Removal/Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the body or tear the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • DTC codes will set if the ignition is turned ON (II) with the seats removed.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the audio and the navigation system, then write down the audio presets.
  1. Tilt the steering wheel all the way up, and telescope it all the way in.
  1. KD model: Slide the front seat forward fully, and remove the anchor cover (A).
  1. KD model: Remove the lower anchor bolt (A).
  1. Raise the seat fully, and slide it halfway to the rear so you can access the seat mounting bolts.
  1. Slide the seat all the way forward, and remove the seat track end covers (A), then remove the bolts.
  1. Slide the seat all the way back, and remove the bolts securing the front seat (A).
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before removing the seat.
  1. Lift up the front seat, then detach the harness clip (A), and disconnect the power seat harness connector (B) and the side airbag connector (C). Disconnect the OPDS unit connector (D) and POP-UP HOOD unit connector (E) (for some models) on the passenger's seat. LHD is shown; RHD is similar.
  1. With the help of an assistant, carefully remove the front seat through the front door opening.

Passenger's
  1. Install the seat in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the seat mounting bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the seat mounting bolts to the specified torque in the sequence shown. Slide the seat all the way back and tighten (1) and (2), then slide it forward and tighten (3) and (4). The LHD driver's seat is shown; the LHD passenger's seat is symmetrical, and RHD is similar.
    • Make sure each connector is plugged in properly.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten them to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
    • Enter the anti-theft codes for the audio and navigation system, then enter the audio presets.
    • Do the steering column position memorization.
