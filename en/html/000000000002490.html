Alternator Removal and Installation
Removal
  1. Do the battery terminal disconnection procedure.
  1. Remove the grille cover.
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Remove the ground cable (A), harness clamps (B) and connector (C) from the A/C condenser fan shroud.
  1. Loosen the two bolts (D) securing the A/C condenser fan shroud.
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Remove the radiator fan control (RFC) unit.
  1. Remove the coolant reservoir (A).
  1. Remove the two bolts (B), then remove the A/C condenser fan shroud.
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Disconnect the alternator connector (A) and BLK wire (B) from the alternator.
  1. Remove the bolt (C) securing the harness holder.
  1. Remove the mounting bolt (A) and alternator bracket mounting bolt (B), then remove the alternator.
Installation
  1. Install the alternator.
  1. Connect the alternator connector (A) and the BLK wire (B) to the alternator.
  1. Install the bolt (C) securing the harness holder.
  1. Install the drive belt.
  1. Install the A/C condenser fan shroud (A), and tighten the bolts.
  1. Install the coolant reservoir (B).
  1. Install the RFC unit.
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Tighten the two bolts (A) securing the A/C condenser fan shroud.
  1. Install the ground cable (B), harness clamps (C) and connector (D) to the A/C condenser fan shroud.
  1. Install the splash shield.
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Install the grill cover.
  1. Do the battery terminal reconnection procedure.
