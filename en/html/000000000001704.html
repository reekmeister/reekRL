Power Seat Motor (Driver's) Test
Slide motor, Front up-down motor, Rear up-down motor
  1. Remove the driver's seat.
  1. Disconnect the connector from each power seat motor.
    NOTE: All connectors are terminal side of male terminals.
LHD type
RHD type
  1. Test each motor by connecting power and ground according to the table. When the motor stops running, disconnect battery power immediately.
  1. If the motor does not run or fails to run smoothly, replace it:
    • Slide motor
    • Front up-down motor
    • Rear up-down motor
Recline motor
  1. Remove the driver's seat.
  1. Disconnect the power seat wire harness 10P (or 12P) * connector from the power seat subharness.
  1. Test motor by connecting power and ground according to the table. When the motor stops running, disconnect battery power immediately.
  1. If the motor does not run or fails to run smoothly, check for an open in the wire between the 10P (or 12P) * connector and recline motor. If the harness is OK, replace the recline motor.
