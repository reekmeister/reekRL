DTC Troubleshooting: B1077
DTC B1077:
 Windshield Wiper (As) Signal Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Turn the wiper switch to LOW, then HIGH, for at least 15 seconds in each position.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1077 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure. The windshield wiper system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Test the wiper motor.
Does the wiper motor operate correctly?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Replace the windshield wiper motor and recheck.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Turn the wiper switch to LOW, then HIGH, and check wiper operation.
Do the wipers operate normally?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Disconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box connector K (5P) and windshield wiper motor 5P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between the No. 4 [No. 5] terminal of the windshield wiper motor 5P connector and the No. 5 terminal of the under-hood fuse/relay box connector K (5P).
    [ ]: RHD type
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Repair an open in the WHT wire.■
  1. Check for continuity between the No. 5 terminal of the under-hood fuse/relay box connector K (5P) and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -

YES -
Go to Step 13 .
