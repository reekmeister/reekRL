Crankshaft Pulley Removal and Installation
Special Tools Required

Handle, 6-25-660L
 07JAB-001020B

Crankshaft pulley holder 50 offset 65
  070AB-RJA0100

Socket wrench, 19 x 90L
 07JAA-001020A
Removal
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Remove the right front wheel.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Hold the pulley with the handle (A) and the crankshaft pulley holder (B).
  1. Remove the bolt with a heavy duty 19 x 90L socket wrench (C) and a breaker bar, then remove the crankshaft pulley.
Installation
  1. Remove any oil and clean the pulleys (A), the crankshaft (B), the bolt (C), and the washer (D). Lubricate with new engine oil as shown.
  1. Install the crankshaft pulley, and torque the bolt. Do not use an impact wrench.
    -1.
    Hold the pulley with the handle (A) and the crankshaft pulley holder (B). Tighten the bolt to 64 N·m (6.5 kgf·m, 47 lbf·ft) with a torque wrench and a 19 x 90L socket wrench (C).
    -2.
    Mark the bolt head (D) and the crankshaft pulley (E) as shown, then tighten the bolt an additional 60 ° (The mark on the bolt head lines up with the mark on the crankshaft pulley).
  1. Install the drive belt.
  1. Install the splash shield.
  1. Install the right front wheel.
