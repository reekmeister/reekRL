Front Seat Removal/Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to tear the seam or damage the seat covers.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • DTC codes will set if ignition is turned ON (II) with the seats removed.
  1. Tilt the steering wheel all the way up, and move it all the way in.
  1. KD model: Slide the front seat all the way forward. Carefully pry up on the bottom of the anchor cover (A) to release the hooks (B) and the tab (C), then remove the cover by pulling it upward.
Driver's seat
Passenger's seat
  1. KD model: Remove the lower anchor bolts (A).
  1. Slide the front seat all the way forward, remove the seat track outer end covers (A) and the seat track inner end covers (B) from the back of both seat tracks.
  1. Remove the rear bolts.
  1. Slide the front seat all the way back, and remove the front bolts.
  1. Do the battery terminal disconnection procedure, then wait at least 3 minutes before removing the seat.
  1. Lift up the front seat, then detach the harness clips (A), and disconnect the seat wire harness connector (B), the side airbag connector (C), seat position sensor connector (D), OPDS unit connector (E) and POP-UP HOOD connector (F) (for some models) on the passenger's seat LHD model is shown; RHD model is similar.
Driver's seat
Passenger's seat
  1. Install the seat in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Apply medium strength liquid thread lock to the seat mounting bolts before reinstallation.
    • Tighten the seat mounting bolts to the specified torque in the sequence shown. Slide the seat all the way back and tighten (1) and (2), then slide it forward and tighten (3) and (4). The driver's seat is shown; the passenger's seat is symmetrical.
    • Tighten the bolts by hand first, then tighten them to specification with a torque wrench.
    • Do the battery terminal reconnection procedure.
    • Check for any DTCs that may have been set during repairs, and clear them.
