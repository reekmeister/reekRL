Roof Molding Replacement
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the body.
  1. Apply protective tape (A) to the body. Using a trim tool, pry up on the roof molding (B).
  1. Pull up and slide the roof molding to release the front bracket (C) from the pin (D).
  1. Pull up the front portion of the roof molding (A).
  1. Pull up and release the rear bracket (A) from the pin (B), then remove the roof molding (C).
  1. Install the molding in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Take care not to damage the windshield molding.
    • Make sure the roof molding is installed securely.
