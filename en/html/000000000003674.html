DLC Circuit Troubleshooting
NOTE: Make sure the HDS and the DLC cable of the HDS are not defective.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC.
    NOTE: Make sure the HDS is properly connected to the DLC.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), and read the HDS.
Does the HDS identify the vehicle?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Go to Step 21 .

  • If the HDS does not communicate with the IMMOBI (immobilizer) system, go to Step 11 .
  • If the HDS does not communicate with the BODY ELECTRICAL system, go to Step 13 .

NO -

  1. Continue to check for continuity between body ground and DLC terminals No. 1 and No. 7, while disconnecting these connectors, one at a time:
    • SRS unit connector A (28P)
    • VSA modulator-control unit 47P connector
    • SH-AWD control unit connector A (26P)
    • Pop-up hood unit 26P connector
    • Immobilizer-keyless control unit 7P connector
    • Driver's under-dash fuse/relay box (P) (30P) connector
Does continuity go away when one of the above connectors is disconnected?
YES -
Replace the part that caused an open when it was disconnected.■
NO -
Repair short in the wire between the DLC No. 7 (K-line) and the VSA modulator-control unit, the SRS unit, the immobilizer-keyless control unit, the SH-AWD control unit, the pop-up hood unit, or the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box or DLC No. 1 (DIAG-H line) and the immobilizer control unit.■
  1. Connect DLC terminals No. 1 and No. 7 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between body ground and these connector terminals:
    Connector
    Terminal

    No. 3 (GRY)

    No. 6 (RED)
