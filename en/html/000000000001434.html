Fuel Tank Draining
  1. Remove the secondary fuel gauge sending unit.
  1. Using a hand pump, a hose, and a container suitable for fuel, draw the fuel from the secondary fuel gauge sending unit side of the tank.
  1. Remove the fuel tank unit.
  1. Using a hand pump, a hose, and a container suitable for fuel, draw the fuel from the fuel tank unit side of the tank.
  1. Install the secondary fuel gauge sending unit and fuel tank unit.
