Direct Electromagnetic Clutch Test (with the brake tester)
NOTE:
  • No DTC was detected.
  • Engine is start.
  • The SH-AWD control unit had been initialized.
  • The tyre pressures are at the specified value.
  • Tyre wear is the same between the left and right tyres.
  1. Set the vehicle on the brake tester in the opposite direction.
  1. Set the rope and fix the vehicle.
  1. Put the weight of 60 kg on the trunk.
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC (A).
  1. Move the Shift lever in the P.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Do the direct electromagnetic clutch test with the HDS and follow the screen prompts.
    • If the results are OK, the right and left direct electromagnetic clutch is normal.
    • If the results are NG, go to Step 8 .
  1. Replace the differential oil, then recheck the direct electromagnetic clutch with the HDS and follow the screen prompts.
    • If the results are OK, the right and left direct electromagnetic clutch is normal.
    • If the results are NG, replace the right and left clutch assemblies.
Direct Electromagnetic Clutch Test (with the torque wrench)
NOTE:
  • No DTC was detected.
  • The shift lever is in N.
  • The SH-AWD control unit had been initialized.
  • The tyre pressures are at the specified value.
  • Tyre size and wear are the same between the left and right tyres.
  1. Check that the differential oil temperature is over 20 °C (68 °F) with the HDS. If it is below 20 °C (68 °F), raise the temperature by driving the vehicle in circles or by constantly turning.
  1. Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.
  1. Remove the rear wheels.
  1. Install the torque wrench adapter on the rear wheel hub.
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC.
  1. Do the direct electromagnetic clutch test with the HDS.
  1. Install a torque wrench on the special tool as shown on the HDS. Write down the torque value while slowly rotating the rear hub with the torque wrench as shown. (Turn the hub no more than 90 degrees in 7 to 10 seconds).
    NOTE: Use a torque wrench capable of measuring up to 490 N·m (50.0 kgf·m, 360 lbf·ft).
  1. Enter the torque value into the HDS, and wait for the test to finish.
    • If the result shows the clutch driving force is normal, the clutch assembly is OK.
    • If the result shows the clutch driving force is abnormal, replace the differential fluid, and retest. If the result still shows the clutch driving force is abnormal, replace the right and left clutch assemblies.
Acceleration Device Test
NOTE:
  • No DTC is detected.
  • The SH-AWD control unit has been initialized.
  • The shift lever is in P.
  1. Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC (A).
  1. Do the acceleration device test with the HDS.
  1. Look at the results displayed on the HDS.
    • If the acceleration ratio is lower than the normal value when decelerating is displayed, replace the rear differential assembly.
    • If the acceleration ratio is higher than the normal value when decelerating is displayed, go to Step 5 .
    • If the acceleration ratio is lower than the normal value when accelerating is displayed, go to Step 10 .
    • If the acceleration ratio is higher than the normal value when accelerating is displayed, replace the SH-AWD control unit.
  1. Remove the 8 mm bolt (A) and the sealing washer (B) from the differential (C) as shown.
  1. Connect the oil pressure gauge (D), the oil pressure hose (E) and the oil pressure hose adapter (F), then install the assembly into the differential. Route the hose under the vehicle toward the front door.
  1. Do the acceleration device test. When you get to the ‘‘Keep the speed constant between 41 km/h (22 mph) and 52 km/h (28 mph)'' screen in the test, write down the pressure indicated on the pressure gauge. Complete the test.
    • If the hydraulic pressure was over 8.0 kgf/cm 2 (113 psi), go to Step 8 .
    • If the hydraulic pressure was below 8.0 kgf/cm 2 (113 psi), replace the rear differential assembly.
  1. Replace the shift solenoid.
  1. Do the acceleration device test.
    • If Normal is displayed, the problem has been fixed.
    • If the acceleration ratio is higher than the normal value when accelerating is displayed, replace the rear differential assembly.
  1. Check the differential oil level. If the oil level was low, add oil as needed, then go to Step 12 . If the oil level was OK, go to Step 11 .
  1. Do the acceleration device test. After the shift solenoid test (a portion of the acceleration device test) is complete, continue the acceleration device test. When you get to the ‘‘Keep the speed constant between 41 km/h (22 mph) and 52 km/h (28 mph)'' screen, write down the pressure indicated on the pressure gauge. Complete the test.
    • If the hydraulic pressure was less than 8.0 kgf/cm 2 (113 psi), go to Step 12 .
    • If the hydraulic pressure was over 8.0 kgf/cm 2 (113 psi), replace the rear differential assembly.
  1. Do the acceleration device test. Look at the results displayed on the HDS.
    • If Normal is displayed, the problem has been fixed.
    • If ‘‘Acceleration device is abnormal'' is displayed, go to Step 13 .
  1. Replace the shift solenoid, then do the acceleration device test.
    • If Normal is displayed, the problem has been fixed.
    • If ‘‘Acceleration device is abnormal'' is displayed, replace the rear differential assembly.
