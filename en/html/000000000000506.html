Dashboard Passenger's Vent Panel Removal/Installation
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is symmetrical.
  1. Remove these items.
    • Center trim, both sides
    • Ashtray housing
    • Instrument fascia
    • Audio unit
  1. Remove the passenger's vent panel (A).
    -1.
    Open the glove box.
    -2.
    Gently pull out the panel to release the clips (B, C).
    -3.
    Disconnect the hazard warning switch connector (D).
  1. Install the panel in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly.
