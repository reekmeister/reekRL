DTC Troubleshooting: B1075
DTC B1075:
 Headlight Signal Error
  1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.
  1. Turn the combination switch ON (headlight LOW position) from the OFF position the ON position.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1075 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the headlight circuit is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections at under-hood fuse/relay box connector J (10P), passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box *1 and at the combination switch connector.■
*1: RHD type except KE model
  1. With the headlight switch OFF, select LIGHTING SYSTEM from the HDS, and enter the DATA LIST.
  1. Check the ON/OFF information of the Headlight Switch (BACK-UP) in the DATA LIST.
Is the information indicator OFF?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Turn the headlight switch ON.
  1. Check the ON/OFF information of the Headlight Switch (BACK-UP) in the DATA LIST.
Is the information indicator ON?
YES -
Faulty relay control module; replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.■
NO -
Go to Step 8 .
  1. Disconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box connector J (10P) and the wiper/washer switch *1 (combination light switch) *2 8P connector.
    *1: LHD type and KE model
    *2: RHD type except KE model
  1. Check for continuity between the No. 6 terminal of the under-hood fuse/relay box connector J (10P) and the No. 1 terminal of the wiper/washer switch (combination switch control unit) 8P connector.
Is there continuity?
YES -
  • LHD type and KE model: Go to Step 10 .
  • RHD type except KE model: Faulty combination switch control unit or relay control module; substitute a known-good combination light switch and retest. If the DTC is no longer present, replace the original combination light switch. If the DTC is still present, the relay control module is faulty, replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.■
NO -
Faulty passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box (RHD type) or an open in the WHT or GRY wire.■
  1. Perform the combination light switch test.
