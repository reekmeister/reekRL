Windshield Wiper Arm/Nozzle Adjustment
Wiper arms stop position
  1. When the wiper arms stop at automatic stop position, confirm the wiper arms are at the standard position.
    a:
    Position at about 115 mm from the top of cowl cover (A) (KG and KE models) (Summer position)
    a:
    Position at about 237 mm from the top of cowl cover (A) (KG and KE models) (Winter position)
    a:
    Position at about 80 mm from the top of cowl cover (A) (Except KG and KE models) (Summer position)
    a:
    Position at about 237 mm from the top of cowl cover (A) (Except KG and KE models) (Winter position)
    b:
    Position at about 28 mm from the top of cowl cover (A) (Summer position)
    b:
    Position at about 186.7 mm from the top of cowl cover (A) (Winter position)

Washer nozzle position
  1. When you jet the washer fluid, hit to confirm 50 % or more of the jet fluid splashes down within the slash area. If there are not standard positions, adjust the nozzles.
