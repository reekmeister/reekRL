Ignition Coil Removal/Installation
  1. Remove the engine cover (A), right upper fender trim (B), battery trim (C), left upper fender trim (D), then remove the grille cover (E).
  1. Disconnect the ignition coil connectors (A), then remove the front bank ignition coils (B).
  1. Disconnect the ignition coil connectors (A), then remove the rear bank ignition coils (B).
  1. Install the ignition coils in the reverse order of removal.
