EPS Motor Removal and Installation
Removal
NOTE: Do not allow dust, dirt, or other foreign materials to enter the steering gearbox.
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes for the radio and the navigation system, then write down the audio presets.
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is OFF, then disconnect the negative cable from the battery.
  1. Remove the right and left front fender trim (A), then remove the intake manifold cover (B).
  1. Remove the relay box (A), then remove the wire harness clips.
  1. Remove the front strut brace.
  1. Remove the P/S heat baffle plate (A).
  1. Remove the wire harness stay A from the steering gearbox.
  1. Disconnect the torque sensor 3P connector (A) from the steering gearbox.
  1. Remove the harness clip (A), then remove the harness stay B and harness stay C.
  1. Disconnect the motor 1P connector (A), motor 2P connector (B), EPS gearbox 12P connector (C), and remove the connectors (D) from the connector bracket (E). Wrap the connectors with vinyl tape to avoid the contamination from grease or water.
  1. Raise the front of the vehicle, and support it with safety stands in the proper locations.
  1. Remove the driver's side front wheel.
  1. Remove the motor (A) from the steering gearbox.
    NOTE: Remove the bottom mounting bolt (B) on the motor from the wheelwell opening.
Installation
  1. Remove the motor bushing (A), and wipe the grease off it.
  1. Clean the mating surface of the motor (B) and the plate (C).
  1. Remove the O-ring (D), and discard it.
  1. Apply a thin coat of silicone grease to the new O-ring, and carefully fit it on the motor.
  1. Apply silicone grease to the motor bushing.
  1. Install the motor bushing on the motor.
  1. Clean the mating surface of the steering gearbox (A).
  1. Install the motor on the steering gearbox by engaging the motor bushing and the lug portion (B). Note the motor installation position (direction of motor wire).
  1. Before tightening the bolts, turn the motor two or three times to the right and left about 45 degrees. Make sure the motor is evenly seated on the steering gearbox, and that the O-ring is not pinched between the mating surfaces.
  1. Install the flange bolts (A), and tighten to the specified torque alternately in two or more steps.
  1. Install the removed parts in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Make sure the motor 1P connector, the motor 2P connector, the EPS gearbox 12P connector, and the torque sensor 3P connector are properly connected.
    • Make sure the motor and the EPS wires are not caught or pinched by any parts.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery and do the following items:
    • Enter the anti-theft codes for the radio and the navigation system, then enter the audio presets.
    • Do the memorizing the torque sensor neutral position.
    • Do the steering column position memorization.
  1. After installation, start the engine, and let it idle. Turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times. Check that the EPS indicator does not come on.
