Fuel Gauge Sending Unit Test
NOTE: For the fuel gauge system circuit diagram, refer to the Gauges Circuit Diagram.
  1. Check for body electrical system DTCs.
    • If no problem is found, go to Step 2 .
    • If DTC B1175 is indicated, go to the DTC B1175 troubleshooting.
  1. Check the No. 21 METER (10 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
  1. Do the gauge drive circuit check.
    • If the fuel gauge needle sweeps from the minimum to maximum position and then returns to minimum, the gauge is OK. Go to Step 4 .
    • If the fuel gauge needle does not sweep correctly, replace the gauge control module and retest.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Remove the rear seat cushion.
  1. Remove the access panel (A) from the left side of the floor.
  1. Disconnect the fuel pump 5P connector (B).
  1. Remove the fuel tank unit from the fuel tank.
    NOTE: If the fuel tank is full, drain the fuel.
  1. Measure resistance between the No. 1 and No. 2 terminals of the fuel pump 5P connector with the float at E (EMPTY), LOW (LOW FUEL INDICATOR), and F (FULL) positions.
    • If you do not get the following readings, replace the fuel gauge sending unit.
    • If the fuel gauge sending unit test is OK, do the secondary fuel gauge sending unit test.
    Float Position
    F 1.1 in. (28.9 mm)
    LOW 4.7 in. (118.1 mm)
    E 5.7 in. (144.7 mm)
    Resistance (Ω)
    9 to11
    217.3 to243.1
    284.1 to294.1

    NOTE: Remove the No. 15 BACK UP (40 A) fuse from the under-hood fuse/relay box for at least 30 seconds after completing troubleshooting, otherwise it may take up to 20 minutes for the fuel gauge to indicate the correct fuel level.
