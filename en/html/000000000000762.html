EPS DTC Troubleshooting: 71-01, 71-02, 71-03
DTC 71-01:
 Motor Angle Sensor (SIN/COS Signals) (Steering diagnosis)
DTC 71-02:
 Motor Angle Sensor (Neutral Position Learning of SIN/COS) (Initial diagnosis)
DTC 71-03:
 Motor Angle Sensor (SIN/COS Signals) (Steering diagnosis)
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Turn the steering wheel to the right or left, and wait 10 seconds or more.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC 71-01, DTC 71-02, or DTC 71-03 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the motor angle sensor and the EPS (LKAS) control unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P).
  1. Measure resistance between EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminals No. 13 and No. 27.
Is the resistance between 15−24 Ω?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Go to Step 10 .
  1. Disconnect the EPS gearbox 12P connector.
  1. Check for continuity between EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminal No. 13 and No. 27.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between the EPS (LKAS) control unit terminals D13 (R1 line) and D27 (R2 line).■
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. On the sensor side, measure resistance between EPS gearbox 12P connector terminals No. 5 and No. 11.
Is the resistance between 15−24 Ω?
YES -
Repair open in the wire between the motor angle sensor and the EPS (LKAS) control unit.■
NO -
Replace the motor. The fault is an open or short in the motor angle sensor wire harness, or the motor angle sensor internal circuit.■
  1. Measure resistance between EPS (LKAS) control unit connector D (28P) terminals No. 12 and No. 26.
