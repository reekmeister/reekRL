VSA System Symptom Troubleshooting - Brake system indicator does not come on (Check bulb operation with parking brake)
Brake system indicator does not come on (check bulb operation with parking brake)
  1. With the parking brake applied, turn the ignition switch ON (II), and watch the brake system indicator.
Does the brake system indicator come on?
YES -
The system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures. ■
NO -
Go to Step 2 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, then ON (II) again.
Does the ABS indicator come on for several seconds?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Repair open in the gauge control module indicator power source circuit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Release the parking brake.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
Does the brake system indicator come on for several seconds?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Do the troubleshooting for the gauge control module. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Do the parking brake switch test.
Is the switch OK?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Replace the parking brake switch. ■
  1. Remove the gauge control module. Do not disconnect the gauge control module connectors.
  1. Connect gauge control module connector B (28P) terminal No. 14 and body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
Does the brake system indicator come on and stay on?
YES -
Repair open in the wire between the gauge control module and the parking brake switch.■
NO -
Replace the gauge control module. ■
