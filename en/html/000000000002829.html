Side Sill Panel Replacement
  1. Remove the side sill panel (A).
    -1.
    On the front of the rear wheel arch, remove the clips (B).
    -2.
    Remove the expansion clips (C).
    -3.
    Slide the side sill panel rearward and remove it. The side clips (D) will stay in the body.
    -4.
    Remove the side clips (D) by turning the clip 45 ° from the body.
    -5.
    On the back of the front wheel arch, remove two screws (E) and clip (B), and if equipped, remove the front splash guard (F).
    -6.
    Turn the side sill inner trim (G) 45 ° to release the side clip (D), then remove the trim.
    -7.
    If necessary, remove the screws (H), then remove the moldings (I).
  1. If the clips are damaged or stress-whitened, replace them with new ones.

  1. Hold the panel up, and fit all the side clips into the holes in the body, then push on the panel until the clips snap into place.
  1. Install all the expansion clips (D).

  1. Without splash guard: install the screws (F).
