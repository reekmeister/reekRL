DTC Troubleshooting: B1080
DTC B1080:
 Power Supply Circuit (IG1 Line) Input Error for Relay Control Module and MICU
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and then back ON (II).
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1080 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the MICU and the relay control module are OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections shown:
LHD type
  • The relay control module connector J (10P)
  • The driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connector D (17P)
RHD type
  • The relay control module connector J (10P)
  • The driver's under-dash fuse/relay box connector X (39P)
  • The passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box connector B (10P)
  • The passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box connector E (14P)
If the connections are good, check the battery condition and the charging system.■
  1. Check the MICU IG1 power supply No. 21 (10 A) fuse in the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box.
Is the fuse OK?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Replace the fuse, and recheck for DTCs.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box connector J (10P), check for loose or poor connection. If the terminals are bent, loose or corroded, repair them as necessary and recheck the system.
  1. Reconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box connector J (10P).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Measure voltage between the No. 8 terminal of the under-hood fuse/relay box connector J (10P) and body ground.
Is there battery voltage?
YES -
Go to Step 10 .
NO -
Open, or loose connection, in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box, passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box * , and the driver's under-dash fuse/relay box. Repair the wire, and recheck for DTCs.■
*: RHD type
  1. Check for voltage between the No. 10 terminal of the under-hood fuse/relay box connector J (10P) and body ground.
Is there less than 1 V?
YES -
Faulty relay control module; replace the under-hood fuse/relay box. ■
NO -
An open in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box and G301. If the wire is OK, inspect the G301.■
