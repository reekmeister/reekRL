POP-UP HOOD Safety Plate Inspection
NOTE: Do not replace the pedestrian crash sensors and PUH unit before do the following procedure.
  1. Park the vehicle on flat horizontal surface.
  1. Ensure the tyre pressures are set at the correct value.
  1. Ensure all luggage are removed from the vehicle.
  1. Sit the driver's seat and ensure all of the doors are closed and nobody is touching the vehicle.
  1. The shift lever in the Park position, then apply the parking brake.
  1. Connect the HDS to the data link connector (DLC).
  1. Select POP-UP HOOD, then DATA LIST on the HDS.
  1. Confirm the Right, Center, and Left Safety Plate Angle (L, C, and R PCS detection).
    If the all of the angles are less than +/−10.0 degrees, the safety plate can be reused.
    If the one or more angles are more than +/−10.0 degrees, the safety plate must be replacement.
    NOTE: Refer to Component Replacement/Inspection After Deployment regarding parts that must be replaced.
