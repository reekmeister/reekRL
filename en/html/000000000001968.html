SH-AWD DTC Troubleshooting: 81-2
DTC 81-2:
 Left Search Coil Sensor (Open or Short)
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect SH-AWD control unit connector A (26P).
  1. Check for continuity between SH-AWD control unit connector terminals A4 and A17.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Go to Step 6 .
  1. Measure resistance between SH-AWD control unit connector terminals A4 and A17.
Is there 2.0 Ω or more?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. Check for continuity between SH-AWD control unit connector terminal A4 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between SH-AWD control unit connector terminal A4 and body ground.■
NO -
Check for loose connector terminals at the SH-AWD control unit and repair if necessary. If the connections are OK, substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. ■
  1. Connect a jumper wire between SH-AWD control unit connector terminals A4 and A17. Disconnect the left search coil 2P connector, then check for continuity between left search coil 2P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 2.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Replace the rear differential left side case. ■
NO -
Repair open in the wire between left search coil 2P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 2.■
  1. Disconnect the left search coil 2P connector, then check for continuity between left search coil 2P connector terminals No. 1 and No. 2.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between SH-AWD control unit connector A and the left search coil connector.■
NO -
Replace the rear differential left side case. ■
