Front Seat Disassembly/Reassembly
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
Seat-back/Seat Cushion
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • DTC codes will set it the ignition is turned ON (II) with the seats removed.
  1. Remove the front seat.
  1. Remove the screw and release the hooks (A), tab (B), and pin (C), then remove the front cover (D).
  1. Remove the recline cover (A).
    -1.
    Remove the screw and detach the tabs (B).
    -2.
    Pull up the cover, then detach the hooks (C).
    -3.
    Disconnect the recline switch and up-down switch connector (D), lumbar support switch connector (E).
    -4.
    Remove the wire harness (F) from the hooks (G).
  1. Remove the center cover (A).
    -1.
    Release the tabs (B).
    -2.
    Pull up the cover, then detach the hook (C).
  1. From under the seat cushion, disconnect the seat harness connector (A). Detach the side airbag connector clip (B), the OPDS unit connector clip (C) on the passenger's seat. Release the hook springs (D) and seat cushion cover (E), pull the cover back, and remove the wire ties (F). LHD is shown; RHD is similar.

Passenger's
  1. Remove the seat-back (A).
    -1.
    Remove the headrest (B).
    -2.
    Remove the seat cushion trim cover (C) from hook (D).
    -3.
    Remove the bolts.
    -4.
    Lift the seat-back, then pull the side airbag harness (E), seat harness (F) and OPDS harness on passenger's seat, out through the hole in the seat cushion trim cover.
  1. Assemble the seat-back and seat cushion in the reverse order of disassembly, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
    • Push the clips into place securely.
    • Apply multipurpose grease to the moving parts of the seat track.
    • To prevent wrinkles in the seat cushion cover, stretch the material evenly over the pad.
Seat-back
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Replace the push nuts with new ones. Make sure the push nuts are installed correctly.
  • Apply medium strength type liquid thread lock to the motor mounting bolt before reinstallation.

Seat Cushion
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Apply oil to the pivot portions of the slide lock.
  • Apply multipurpose grease to the sliding parts and pivot portions of the seat tracks.
  • The driver's seat is shown; the passenger's seat is similar.
