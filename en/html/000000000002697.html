A/T Clutch Disassembly
Special Tools Required

Clutch spring compressor set
 07LAE-PX40000

Clutch spring compressor attachment
 07LAE-PX40100

Clutch spring compressor attachment
 07HAE-PL50100

Clutch spring compressor bolt assembly
  07GAE-PG40200

Snap ring pliers
 07LGC-0010100
  1. Remove the snap ring (A) with a screwdriver (B).
  1. Remove the 1st clutch end-plate (A), 1st clutch discs (B) (5), 1st clutch wave-plates (C) (5), disc spring (D), 1st-hold clutch plate B (E), 1st-hold clutch discs (F) (3), 1st-hold clutch wave-plates (G) (2), and flat-plate (H) from the 1st/1st-hold clutch drum (I).
  1. Make reference marks on the clutch wave-plate.
  1. Remove the clutch end-plate (A), clutch discs (B) (7), clutch wave-plates (C) (3), and clutch flat-plates (D) (4) from the 2nd clutch drum (E).
  1. Make reference marks on the clutch wave-plates.
  1. Remove the clutch end-plate (A), clutch discs (B) (6), clutch wave-plates (C) (3), and clutch flat-plates (D) (3) from the 3rd clutch drum (E).
  1. Make reference marks on the clutch wave-plates.
  1. Remove the clutch end-plate (A), clutch discs (B) (5), clutch wave-plates (C) (2), clutch flat-plates (D) (3), and the disc spring (E) from the 4th clutch drum (F).
  1. Make reference marks on the clutch wave-plates.
  1. Remove the clutch end-plate (A), clutch discs (B) (5), clutch wave-plates (C) (2), clutch flat-plates (D) (3), and the disc spring (E) from the 5th clutch drum (F).
  1. Make reference marks on the clutch wave-plates.
  1. Install the special tools.
  1. Be sure the special tool (A) is adjusted to have full contact with the spring retainer (B) on the 1st, 2nd, and 4th clutches.
  1. Check the placement of the clutch spring compressor attachment. If either end of the special tool is set over an area of the spring retainer that is unsupported by the return spring, reset the clutch spring compressor attachment over the proper position.
  1. Set the special tool (A) on the spring retainer (B) of the 3rd and 5th clutches so the special tool works on the clutch return spring (C).
  1. Check the placement of the clutch spring compressor attachment. If either end of the special tool is not set over the clutch return spring end (A), reset the clutch spring compressor attachment over the return spring end.
  1. Compress the return spring until the snap ring can be removed.
  1. Remove the snap ring with snap ring pliers.
  1. Remove the special tools.
  1. Remove the snap ring (A), spring retainer (B), and return spring (C).
  1. Wrap a shop rag around the clutch drum, and apply air pressure to the fluid passage to remove the piston. Place a finger tip on the other passage while applying air pressure.
  1. Remove the 1st/1st-hold clutch piston (A) from the clutch drum (B), and remove the O-rings from the 1st clutch piston.
  1. Remove the 1st-hold clutch piston (A) from the 1st clutch piston (B), and remove the O-rings from the 1st-hold clutch piston.
  1. Remove the clutch piston, and remove the O-rings from the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th clutch pistons.
