DTC Troubleshooting: P0125 (86)
DTC P0125:
 ECT Sensor 1 Malfunction/Slow Response
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Start the engine, and let it idle for 5 minutes or more.
  1. Check ECT SENSOR 1 in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is about −12 °C (10 °F) or less indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at ECT sensor 1, ECT sensor 2, and the PCM. If they are OK, replace ECT sensor 1. ■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Allow the engine to cool to 40 °C (104 °F) or less.
  1. Make sure the temperature difference of ECT SENSOR 1 and ECT SENSOR 2 is between about 10 °C (50 °F) in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
  1. Start the engine, and let it idle until ECT sensor 1 goes up aruond 70 °C (158 °F).
Does the ECT sensor 2 also show about 70 °C (158 °F)?
YES -
Go to Step 7 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at ECT sensor 1, ECT sensor 2, and the PCM.■
  1. Check the thermostat.
Is the thermostat OK?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at ECT sensor 1, ECT sensor 2, and the PCM. If they are OK, replace ECT sensor 1, then go to Step 8 .
NO -
Replace the thermostat, then go to Step 8 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Allow the engine to cool to 40 °C (104 °F) or less.
  1. Check the temperature difference of ECT SENSOR 1 and ECT SENSOR 2 is between about 10 °C (50 °F) in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
  1. Start the engine, and let it idle until ECT sensor 1 goes up aruond 70 °C (158 °F).
Does the ECT sensor 2 also show about 70 °C (158 °F)?
YES -
Go to Step 1 and recheck.
NO -
Troubleshooting is complete.■
