HFT-Voice Control Switch Test/Replacement
  1. Remove the driver's airbag.
  1. Disconnect the 6P connector (A).
  1. Remove the two screws and HFT-voice control switch (B).
  1. Measure the resistance between the No. 5 and No. 2 terminals in each switch position according to the table.

    HFT-Voice Control Switch

    Position
    Resistance
    OFF
    About 1 kΩ
    HFT Talk button pressed
    About 185 kΩ
    HFT Back button pressed
    About 45 kΩ

  1. Check for continuity between the No. 4 (+) and No. 3 (−) terminals.
    There should be comtinuity.
  1. If the resistance is not as specified and/or there is no continuity between the No. 4 (+) and No. 3 (−) terminals, replace the switch.
