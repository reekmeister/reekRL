Yaw Rate-Lateral Acceleration Sensor Replacement
NOTE:
  • Do not damage or drop the sensor as it is sensitive.
  • Do not use air or electric impact tools.
  1. Remove the center console.
  1. Remove the select lever fixing bolts.
  1. Remove the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor bracket fixing bolts, and then disconnect the sensor connector (A).
  1. Pull out the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor bracket (B) with the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor.
  1. Remove the sensor fixing bolts, and then separate the sensor (C) from the bracket.
  1. Install the sensor in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Do the VSA sensor neutral position memorization.
  1. Do the SH-AWD yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor neutral position memorization procedure.
