POP-UP HOOD Unit Replacement
NOTE: Review the seat replacement procedure before doing repair s or service.
Removal
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Disconnect the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector.
  1. Remove the passenger's seat assembly.
  1. Remove the bolt (A) and the POP-UP HOOD unit (B).
Installation
  1. Install the new POP-UP HOOD unit (A) with the bolt (B).
  1. Install the seat assembly, then connect right side wire (LHD type) or left side wire (RHD type) harness 26P connector.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Set the seat-back in the normal position, and make sure there is nothing on the front passenger's seat.
  1. Calibrating the pedestrian crash sensor.
