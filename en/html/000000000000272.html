ECPS Indicator Circuit Troubleshooting - ECPS indicator come on, but there is no power assist
ECPS indicator comes on, but there is no power assist
  • Hard steering
  • Excessively light steering
  • Uneven or rough steering
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
Is there clicking noise from the stepping valve motor for 2 to 3 seconds?
YES -
The ECPS system is OK at this time. Go to Symptom Troubleshooting.■
NO -
Faulty ECPS control unit or the stepping valve motor, replace the ECPS control unit or the valve housing and stepping valve motor. ■
