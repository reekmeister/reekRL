Windshield Wiper Blade Replacement
Removal
  1. Pull the wiper arm up to the wiper position .
  1. Remove the shank cover (A) and screw (B).
  1. Pull the wiper rubber (A) from wiper blade holder.
Install
  1. Install the wiper rubber rein forcement (A) into the slots along of the new wiper rubber (B).
  1. Install the new wiper rubber into the wiper blade holder (A) untill tabs lock (B).
  1. Install the wiper blade onto the windshield wiper arms in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Test by turning on the wipers. If the blades slip, turn off the wipers and seat the atttachaments more firmly.
