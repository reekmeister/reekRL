Cylinder Head Cover Removal
Front
  1. Remove the intake manifold.
  1. Remove the three ignition coils from the front cylinder head.
  1. Remove the harness holder mounting bolt (A) and the harness clamp (B), then remove the harness holder (C) from the bracket.
  1. Remove the front cylinder head cover.
Rear
  1. Remove the intake manifold.
  1. Remove the three ignition coils from the rear cylinder head.
  1. Remove the power steering hose clamp mounting bolt (A).
  1. Remove the harness holder mounting bolts (B), the harness cramp (C), and the breather hose (D).
  1. Remove the wire harness (E) from the rear upper cover.
  1. Remove the cylinder head cover.
