Maintenance Schedule for Normal and Severe Conditions - Except EU, Australia, New Zealand, and Korea
40,000km(2year)
This maintenance schedule outlines the minimum required maintenance that you should perform to ensure the trouble-free operation of the vehicle. Due to regional and climatic differences, some additional servicing may be required. Please consult the warranty booklet for a more detailed description.
The following items must be used for ‘‘Severe Conditions'' indicated on schedule.
  • Engine oil and engine oil filter: A/B/C/D/E
  • Transmission fluid: B/D
  • Transfer fluid: B/D
  • Rear differential fluid: B/D/E
A: Driving less than 8 km (5 miles) per trip or, in freezing temperatures, driving less than 16 km (10 miles) per trip.
B: Driving in extremely hot over 35 °C (95 °F) conditions.
C: Extensive idling or long periods of stop-and-go driving.
D: Towing trailer, driving with a car-top carrier, or driving in mountainous conditions.
E: Driving on muddy, dusty, or de-iced roads.
*: Only severe schedule is required in some countries; refer to local warranty booklet.
