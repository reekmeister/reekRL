DTC Troubleshooting: P0400 (80)
DTC P0400:
 EGR System Leak Detected
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Check for a loose or damaged EGR pipe.
Is the EGR pipe OK?
YES -
Go to Step 2 .
NO -
Reconnect or replace the EGR pipe, then go to Step 7 .
  1. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 3,000 rpm (min −1 ) without load (in P or N) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.
  1. Test-drive under these conditions:
    • Engine coolant temperature (ECT SENSOR 1) above 70 °C (158 °F)
    • Transmission in D
    • Vehicle speed at 40 km/h (25 mph) for 5 minutes or more
    • Maintain the vehicle speed at 50 km/h (32 mph) for 5 minutes or more with cruise control set
  1. Monitor the OBD STATUS for DTC P0400 in the DTCs MENU with the HDS.
Does the screen indicate FAILED?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
If the screen indicates PASSED, intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for exhaust leakage between EGR pipe and EGR valve. If the screen indicates NOT COMPLETED, go to Step 3 and recheck.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Replace the EGR valve.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Reset the PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Test-drive under these conditions:
    • Engine coolant temperature (ECT SENSOR 1) above 70 °C (158 °F)
    • Transmission in D
    • Vehicle speed at 40 km/h (25 mph) for 5 minutes or more
    • Maintain the vehicle speed at 50 km/h (32 mph) for 5 minutes or more with cruise control set
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P0400 indicated?
YES -
Check for exhaust leakage between EGR pipe and EGR valve, then go to Step 1 .
NO -
Go to Step 12 .
  1. Monitor the OBD STATUS for DTC P0400 in the DTCs MENU with the HDS.
Does the screen indicate PASSED?
YES -
Troubleshooting is complete. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs were indicated in Step 11 , go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
NO -
If the screen indicates FAILED, check for exhaust leakage between EGR pipe and EGR valve, then go to Step 1 . If the screen indicates NOT COMPLETED, go to Step 10 .
