Front Seat-back Cover Replacement
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations for the appropriate model:
  • Except KD model
  • KD model
Also review the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.
NOTE:
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to tear the seams of damage the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • LHD is shown; RHD is similar.
  1. Remove the seat-back.
  1. Remove the headrest.
  1. Detach the clips and hooks (A) by pulling the bottom of the back panel (B) back, then gently pull down the panel to release the hooks (C) from the seat-back frame, and remove the panel.
    NOTE: Detach the clips and hooks in the numbered sequence as shown.
  1. Release the hooks (A), then loosen the seat-back cover (B).
  1. Pull the recline motor harness (A), seat-back heater harness (B), side airbag harness (C) and the OPDS harness (D) (passenger's seat) out through the holes (E) in the seat-back cover.
Driver's seat-back
Passenger's seat-back
  1. Passenger's: Release the hooks (A) then turn over the seat-back cover (B). Detach the hooks (C), then remove the OPDS unit cover (D). Disconnect the OPDS unit sensor connectors (E), then release the harness band (F).
  1. With ventilation blower duct: Pull out the duct (A) from seat-back pad (B).
  1. Release the front hook (A) and rear hook (B) of the reinforcing cloth (C) from seat-back frame (D).
  1. Pull out the headrest guides (A) while pinching the tabs on the end of the guides, and remove them.
  1. Remove the seat-back cover (A) and seat-back pad (B) from the seat-back frame (C).
  1. Pass both lower retainers (A) through the slots in the seat-back pad.
  1. Pull back the edge of the seat-back cover all the way around, and release the clips (A), then remove the seat-back cover.
  1. Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Reinitialize the OPDS control unit.
    • To prevent wrinkles when installing a seat-back cover, make sure the material is stretched evenly over the pad before securing the clips, hooks, and hook strips.
    • Replace any clips you removed with new ones (A). Install them with commercially available upholstery ring pliers (B).
    • Reinstall the front hook (C) and rear hook (D) of the reinforcing cloth (E) securely.
    • Use only original Honda/Acura replacement seat-back covers.
    • Make sure the recline motor harness, side airbag harness, seat-back heater harness, and OPDS unit harness (passenger's seat) are routed properly.
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened back panel clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
