Engine Block and Piston Inspection
  1. Remove the piston from the engine block.
  1. Check the piston for distortion or cracks.
  1. Measure the piston diameter at a point 16.0 mm (0.63 in.) from the bottom of the skirt.

    Piston Diameter

    Standard (New):
    89.983−89.996 mm (3.5426−3.5431 in.)

  1. Measure wear and taper in direction X and Y at three levels in each cylinder as shown.

    Cylinder Bore Size

    Standard (New):
    90.000−90.015 mm (3.5433−3.5439 in.)

  1. Check the top of the engine block for warpage. Measure along the edges and across the center as shown.

    Engine Block Warpage

    Standard (New):
    0.07 mm (0.003 in.) max.

  1. Calculate the difference between cylinder bore diameter and piston diameter. If the clearance is near or exceeds the service limit, inspect the piston and cylinder bore for excessive wear.

    Piston-to-Cylinder Bore Clearance

    Standard (New):
    0.004−0.032 mm (0.0002−0.0013 in.)
    Service Limit:
    0.08 mm (0.003 in.)
