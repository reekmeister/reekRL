Is the park pin switch signal OFF while the shift lever is in P, and ON when the shift lever is shifted out of P?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Go to Step 9 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the center console.
  1. Disconnect the park pin switch/A/T gear position indicator panel light connector, and remove it from the shift lever bracket.
  1. Check for continuity between park pin switch/A/T gear position indicator panel light connector terminal No. 4 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Repair short in the wire between park pin switch/A/T gear position indicator panel light connector terminal No. 4 and the MICU.■
NO -
Replace the park pin switch. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect the 30P connector from the MICU.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Measure the voltage between MICU P (30P) connector terminal and body ground:
    • LHD model: No. 16 terminal of the MICU P (30P) connector and body ground.
    • RHD model: No. 29 terminal of MICU P (30P) connector and body ground.
Is there voltage?
YES -
Substitute a known-good MICU and recheck.■
NO -
Repair open or short in the ATP P wire between MICU P (30P) connector and the transmission range switch.■
