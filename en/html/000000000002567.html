Injector Replacement
  1. Relieve the fuel pressure.
  1. Remove the intake manifold.
  1. Disconnect the quick-connect fitting (A).
  1. Remove the fuel joint hose mounting bolt (B).
  1. Disconnect the connectors (C) from the injectors.
  1. Remove the fuel rail mounting bolts (D) from the fuel rails (E).
  1. Remove the fuel rails and the injectors from the injector base.
  1. Remove the injector clips (F) form the fuel rails.
  1. Remove the injectors from the fuel rails.
  1. Relieve the fuel pressure.

  1. Remove the fuel joint hose mounting bolt (B).
  1. Disconnect the connectors (C) from the injectors.
  1. Remove the fuel rail mounting bolts (D) from the fuel rails (E).
  1. Remove the fuel rails and the injectors from the injector base.
  1. Remove the injector clips (F) form the fuel rails.
  1. Remove the injectors from the fuel rails.
  1. Coat the new O-rings (black) (A) with clean engine oil, and insert the injectors (B) into the fuel rails (C).
  1. Install the injector clips (D).
  1. Coat the new injector O-rings (green) (E) with clean engine oil.
  1. Install the fuel rails and injectors in the injector base (F).
  1. Install the fuel rail mounting bolts, and connect the connectors on the injectors.
  1. Install the fuel joint hose mounting bolt.
  1. Connect the quick-connect fitting.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), but do not operate the starter. After the fuel pump runs for about 2 seconds, the fuel rail will be pressurized. Repeat this two or three times, then check the fuel leakage.
  1. Install the intake manifold with a new gasket.
