Hands Free Telephone System Symptom Troubleshooting - Bluetooth audio device cannot be registered
Bluetooth audio device cannot be registered
NOTE:
  • Check the vehicle battery condition first.
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  • When six devices are already registered, delete a device before registering another one.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Press the HFT TALK button.
Does beep sound?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Go to Step 5 .
  1. Check if there is no abnormality in the registered device or if it is a compatible model.
  1. Check either by using a known-good connected device or by using a known-good vehicle equipped with Bluetooth audio.
Is the Bluetooth device normal?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Defective Bluetooth device.
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC. Check for HFT DTCs.
Is there any DTCs indicate?
YES -
Go to HFT DTC troubleshooting.■
NO -
Go to symptom troubleshooting Voice control does not work/respond or HFT switch test. ■
