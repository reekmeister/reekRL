Front Bumper Duct Trim Replacement
NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the front bumper.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove the front bumper:
    • With POP-UP HOOD
    • Without POP-UP HOOD
  1. Remove the front bumper duct trim (A).
    -1.
    Release the headlight washer tube (B) from the hooks (C) on the front bumper duct trim.
    -2.
    Remove the screws, then remove the front bumper duct trim.
  1. If necessary, remove the screws (A, B), then remove the molding (C).
  1. Install the front bumper duct trim in the reverse order of removal.
