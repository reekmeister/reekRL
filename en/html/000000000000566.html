Resetting the Sunroof Control Unit
Resetting the sunroof is required when any of the following have occurred.
  • The sunroof was moved manually with the battery dead or disconnected.
  • The sunroof motor was replaced with a new one.
  • Any of components related to the sunroof were replaced.
    • -
    Wind deflector
    -
    Sunroof glass
    -
    Sunroof seal
    -
    Sunroof glass bracket
    -
    Sunroof cables, etc.
To reset the sunroof control unit, do these steps
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Press and hold the tilt switch, and turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Release the tilt switch, and turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Repeat steps 2 and 3 four times.
  1. Press and hold the sunroof open switch until 3 seconds after the sunroof is fully opened.
  1. Press and hold the sunroof close switch until 3 seconds after the sunroof is fully closed (tilted).
  1. Confirm that the sunroof control unit is reset by using the sunroof AUTO OPEN and AUTO CLOSE function.
