POP-UP HOOD System Troubleshooting Procedure
If mult-information display shows a warning, perform the following procedure.
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is OFF.
  1. Connect the HDS to the DLC (A).
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Select the POP-UP HOOD system in the HDS, and check the TEST MODE MENU is indicated.
Is TEST MODE MENUE indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
  • Open in the VA line and VB line or blowout of No. 10 (7.5 A) fuse or No. 31 (7.5 A) fuse by short to ground in the No. 10 (7.5 A) fuse circuit.
  • Open in the ground line.
  • Open or short to ground in the circuit of a No. 4 terminal of POP-UP HOOD unit connector.
  1. Check the POWER SUPPLY VOLTAGE (VA) and POWER SUPPLY VOLTAGE (VB) on the HDS. There should be more than 10 V.
Is the voltage as specified?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Charge battery or replace the battery.
  1. In Body Electrical on the HDS, check for DTCs.
Is DTC indicated?
YES -
Go to the Troubleshooting Index of Body Electrical.
NO -
Go to Step 7 .
  1. From the POP-UP HOOD system on the HDS, select WARNING DISPLAY FORCE ACTIVATION. In the WARNING DISPLAY FORCE ACTIVATION, indicate warning OFF, and check the multi-information display.
Does the warning indicated?
YES -
Faulty gauge control module.
NO -
Faulty POP-UP HOOD unit.
