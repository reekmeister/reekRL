Roof Drip Molding Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • Take care not to scratch the trim and panels.
  • After removing the roof drip molding, be sure to replace it with a new one.
  1. Detach the hooks (A), then remove the rear pillar trim cover (B).
  1. Remove the roof drip molding (C).
    -1.
    Remove the screw (D).
    -2.
    Detach the clips (E, F, G, H, I, J, K) in alphabetical order.
  1. Remove the remaining clips from the body, and attach them to the molding.
  1. Align the molding with the body, and starting with the middle clip (K) and rear quarter clip (G), snap the clips into place in reverse alphabetical order.
  1. Reinstall the rear pillar trim cover.
