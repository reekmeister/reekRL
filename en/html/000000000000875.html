POP-UP HOOD System DTC Troubleshooting: 12-3x
DTC 12-3x (12-30 to 12-39, 12-3A to 12-3F):  
Short to Another Wire or Decreased Resistance in Right Side Power Unit Component
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 12-3x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the both power unit component 2P connectors.
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 12-1x or 12-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 9 .
NO -
Faulty right side power unit component; replace the right side power unit.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the right side (LHD type) or left side (RHD type) wire harness 16P connector (A) from engine compartment wire harness connector (B).
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 12-1x or 12-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 14 .
NO -
Short in the engine compartment wire harness; replace the engine compartment wire harness.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector from the POP-UP HOOD unit.
LHD type
RHD type
  1. Check for continuity between the No. 10 terminal and No. 11 terminal of the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Short in the right side (LHD type) or left side (RHD type) wire harness or engine compartment wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
NO -
Faulty POP-UP HOOD unit or poor connection at POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector and the POP-UP HOOD unit. Check the connection between the connector and the POP-UP HOOD unit. If the connection is OK, replace the POP-UP HOOD unit. ■
