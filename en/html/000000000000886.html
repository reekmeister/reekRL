POP-UP HOOD System DTC Troubleshooting: 56-1x, 56-2x
DTC 56-1x (56-10 to 56-19, 56-1A to 56-1F):
 Open or Short in CAN Line between POP-UP HOOD Unit and A/T
DTC 56-2x (56-20 to 56-29, 56-2A to 56-2F):
 Open or Short in CAN Line between POP-UP HOOD Unit and VSA
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and check that the PGM-FI indicator, A/T indicator, or VSA indicator comes on for about 6 seconds and then goes off.
Dose the PGM-FI indicator, A/T indicator, or VSA indicator stay on?
YES -
Go to Step 2 .
NO -
Go to the Trouble Shooting of faulty system.
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 56-1x or 56-2x indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Disconnect the POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector from the POP-UP HOOD unit.
  1. Install a jumper wire between the No. 3 and No. 16 terminal of POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector.
  1. Check resistance between the No. 15 and No. 26 terminal of PCM connector E (31P). There should be less than 1.0 Ω.
Is the resistances specified?
YES -
Faulty POP-UP HOOD unit or poor connection at POP-UP HOOD unit 26P connector and the POP-UP HOOD unit. Check the connection between the connector and the POP-UP HOOD unit. If the connecting is OK, replace the POP-UP HOOD unit. ■
NO -
Open in the right side wire harness (LHD type), left side wire harness (RHD type), or dashboard wire harness; replace the faulty harness.■
