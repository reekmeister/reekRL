DTC Troubleshooting: P0123 (7)
DTC P0123:
 TP Sensor A Circuit High Voltage
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Check TP SENSOR A in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
Is there about 4.8 V or more?
YES -
Go to Step 4 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the throttle body and the throttle actuator control module.■
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Are DTC P0123 and P0223 indicated at the same time?
YES -
Go to Step 13 .
NO -
Go to Step 5 .
  1. Measure voltage between throttle body 6P connector terminal No. 1 and body ground.
Is there about 5.0 V?
YES -
Go to Step 20 .
NO -
Go to Step 6 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the throttle body 6P connector.
  1. Disconnect the 13P connector between the PCM subharness and the throttle actuator control module subharness.
  1. Connect throttle body 6P connector terminal No. 1 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between 13P connector terminal No. 4 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the throttle body and the 13P connector (TPSA line), then go to Step 22 .
  1. Disconnect the throttle actuator control module 16P connector, LHD model, RHD model.
  1. Check for continuity between throttle actuator control module 16P connector terminal No. 10 and 13P connector terminal No. 4.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Substitute a known-good throttle actuator control module, LHD model, RHD model, then go to Step 22 and recheck. If DTC P0123 is not indicated, replace the original throttle actuator control module, LHD model, RHD model, then go to Step 22 . If DTC P0123 is indicated, go to Step 20 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the throttle actuator control module and the 13P connector (TPSA line), then go to Step 22 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Disconnect the throttle body 6P connector.
  1. Disconnect the 13P connector between the PCM subharness and the throttle actuator control module subharness.
  1. Connect throttle body 6P connector terminal No. 4 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between 13P connector terminal No. 5 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 18 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the throttle body and the 13P connector (SG line), then go to Step 22 .
  1. Disconnect the throttle actuator control module 16P connector, LHD model, RHD model.
  1. Check for continuity between throttle actuator control module 16P connector terminal No. 9 and 13P connector terminal No. 5.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Substitute a known-good throttle actuator control module, LHD model, RHD model, then go to Step 22 and recheck. If DTC P0123 is not indicated, replace the original throttle actuator control module, LHD model, RHD model, then go to Step 22 . If DTC P0123 is indicated, go to Step 20 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the throttle actuator control module (SG line) and the 13P connector, then go to Step 22 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  1. Replace the throttle body.
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
  1. Reset the PCM with the HDS.
  1. Do the PCM idle learn procedure.
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P0123 indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the throttle body and the throttle actuator control module, then go to Step 1 .
NO -
Troubleshooting is complete. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
