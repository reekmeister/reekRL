Blower Power Transistor Test
  1. Remove the passenger's dashboard undercover.
  1. Disconnect the 5P connector from the power transistor.
  1. Measure the resistance between the No. 1 and No. 4 terminals of the power transistor. It should be about 1.5 kΩ.
    • If the resistance is within the specifications, go to Step 4 .
    • If the resistance is not within the specifications, replace the power transistor.
  1. Carefully release the lock tab on the No. 3 terminal (RED/BLK) (A) in the 5P connector, then remove the terminal and insulate it from body ground.
  1. Reconnect the 5P connector to the power transistor.
  1. Make sure the RED/BLK wire is completely isolated, then supply 12 V to the No. 3 cavity with a jumper wire.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and check that the blower motor runs.
    • If the blower motor does not run, replace the power transistor.
    • If the blower motor runs, the power transistor is OK.
