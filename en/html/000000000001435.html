Fuel Tank Unit Removal/Installation
Removal
  1. Relieve the fuel pressure.
  1. Remove the rear seat cushion.
  1. Remove the access panel (A) from the left side of the floor.
  1. Disconnect the fuel pump 5P connector (B).
  1. Disconnect the quick-connect fitting (C) from the fuel tank unit.
  1. Remove the fuel tank unit (A), and disconnect the transfer tube (B).
    NOTE: If the fuel tank is full, drain the fuel.
Installation
  1. Install a new base gasket (A) to the fuel tank (B).
  1. Install the fuel tank unit (C), and connect the transfer tube (D). Be careful not to bend the fuel gauge sending unit.
  1. Connect the quick-connect fitting (A).
  1. Connect the fuel pump 5P connector (B).
  1. Install the access panel (C).
