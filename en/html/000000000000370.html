Side Impact Sensor (Second) Replacement
Removal
  1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, and wait at least 3 minutes before beginning work.
  1. Disconnect the appropriate side curtain airbag 2P connector.
  1. Disconnect the side impact sensor (second) 4P [2P] connector (A) from the left side or right side wire harness.
    [ ]: KD model
  1. Using a TORX T30 bit, remove the TORX bolt (B), then remove the side impact sensor (second) (C).
Installation
  1. Install the new side impact sensor (second) (A) with the TORX bolt (B), then connect the left side or right side wire harness 4P [2P] connector (C) to the side impact sensor (second).
    [ ]: KD model
  1. Reconnect the negative cable to the battery.
  1. Reinstall all removed parts.
  1. After installing the side impact sensor (second), confirm proper system operation: Turn the ignition switch ON (II); the SRS indicator should come on for about 6 seconds and then go off.
