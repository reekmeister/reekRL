SH-AWD DTC Troubleshooting: 16-3
DTC 16-3:
 Steering Angle Sensor Neutral Position Incorrect
NOTE:
  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information.
  • Check the wheel alignment.
  • Check for installation of the steering angle sensor.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Make sure the HDS communicates with vehicle and the SH-AWD control unit. If it doesn't, troubleshoot the DLC circuit.
  1. Check for DTCs in the VSA system with the HDS.
Are any steering angle sensor DTCs indicated?
YES -
Go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
NO -
Go to Step 4 .
  1. Start the engine.
  1. Check the STEERING ANGLE SENSOR in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
When the steering wheels is turned slowly, does the value change?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
  1. Test-drive the vehicle in a straight line at 30 km/h (19 mph) or more.
  1. Park the vehicle on a level surface, and center the steering wheel.
  1. Check the STEERING ANGLE SENSOR in the DATA LIST with the HDS.
It is within ±5 deg of center?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. If the DTC returns, substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
NO -
Do the steering angle sensor neutral position memorization. If the DTC returns, substitute a known-good SH-AWD control unit and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with the known-good SH-AWD control unit, replace the original SH-AWD control unit. ■
