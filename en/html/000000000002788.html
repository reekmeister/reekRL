Power Steering Pump Pressure Test
Special Tools Required

P/S joint adapter (pump)
 07RAK-S040111

P/S joint adapter (hose)
 07RAK-S040122

P/S pressure gauge
 07406-0010001
Check the fluid pressure as follows to determine whether the trouble is in the pump or gearbox.
  1. Remove the right front fender trim (A).
  1. Check the power steering fluid level.
  1. Disconnect the pump outlet hose (A) from the pump outlet with care so as not to spill the power steering fluid on the frame and other parts, then install the P/S joint adapter (pump) on the pump outlet (B).
  1. Connect the P/S joint adapter (hose) to the P/S pressure gauge, then connect the pump outlet hose to the P/S joint adapter (hose).
  1. Install the P/S pressure gauge to the P/S joint adapter (pump).
  1. Fully open the shut-off valve (A).
  1. Fully open the pressure control valve (B).
  1. Start the engine and let it idle.
  1. Turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times to warm the fluid to operating temperature at 70 °C (158 °F).
  1. Measure steady-state fluid pressure while the engine is idling. If the pump is in good condition, the gauge should read no more than 1,470 kPa (15 kgf/cm 2 , 213 psi).
    If it reads high, check for:
    • Clogged or deformed pump outlet or return line between the pump and gearbox.
    • Clogged valve body unit.
  1. Close the shut-off valve, then close the pressure control valve gradually until the pressure gauge needle is stable. Read the pressure.
    Do not keep the shut-off valve closed more than 5 seconds or the pump could be damaged by over-heating.
  1. Immediately open the shut-off valve fully. If the pump is in good condition, the gauge should read at least 9,400−9,800 kPa (96−100 kgf/cm 2 , 1,365−1,422 psi). A low reading means pump output is too low for full assist. Repair or replace the pump.
