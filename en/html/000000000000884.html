POP-UP HOOD System DTC Troubleshooting: 43-2x, 43-3x, 43-7x, 43-8x, 43-9x
DTC 43-2x (43-20 to 43-29, 43-2A to 43-2F):
 Wrong Sensor is Connected to Right Side Pedestrian Crash Sensor
DTC 43-3x (43-30 to 43-39, 43-3A to 43-3F), 43-7x (43-70 to 43-79, 43-7A to 43-7F), 43-8x (43-80 to 43-89, 43-8A to 43-8F), 43-9x (43-90 to 43-99, 43-9A to 43-9F):
 Faulty Right Side Pedestrian Crash Sensor
  1. Erase the DTC memory.
  1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Read the DTC.
Is DTC 43-2x, 43-3x, 43-7x, 43-8x, or 43-9x indicated?
YES -
Replace the right side pedestrian crash sensor and then calibrate the right side pedestrian crash sensor. ■
NO -
Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. If another DTC is indicated, go to the DTC Troubleshooting Index.
