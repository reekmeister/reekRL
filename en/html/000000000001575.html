Steering Column Removal and Installation
SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations: except KD model, KD model, and the precautions and procedures before performing repairs or service.
Removal
  1. Make sure you have the anti-theft codes (if equipped) for the radio and the navigation system, then write down the audio presets.
  1. Adjust the steering column to full tilt down position, and to the full telescopic out position.
  1. Make sure the ignition switch is OFF, then disconnect the negative cable from the battery.
  1. Remove the driver's airbag assembly and the steering wheel.
  1. Remove the driver's dashboard undercover and driver's dashboard lower cover.
  1. Remove the steering joint cover (A).
  1. Remove the upper column cover (A), and disconnect the connectors from the tilt/telescopic switch (B) on the lower column cover (C).
  1. Disconnect the wire harness connectors from the combination switch assembly (D).
  1. Remove the combination switch assembly from the steering column shaft by removing the screws (E).
  1. Disconnect the connectors from the ignition switch, and release the wire harness clips from the steering column.
  1. Disconnect the connectors from the tilt/telescopic motors (F).
  1. Disconnect the connectors from the tilt/telescopic control unit (G).
  1. Remove the steering joint bolts (H), then disconnect the steering joint (I) from the pinion shaft and the steering column shaft.
  1. Remove the steering column (J) by removing the attaching nuts and bolts.
  1. Remove the upper column cover (A), and disconnect the connectors from the tilt/telescopic switch (B) on the lower column cover (C).
  1. Disconnect the wire harness connectors from the combination switch assembly (D).
  1. Remove the combination switch assembly from the steering column shaft by removing the screws (E).
  1. Disconnect the connectors from the ignition switch, and release the wire harness clips from the steering column.
  1. Disconnect the connectors from the tilt/telescopic motors (F).
  1. Disconnect the connectors from the tilt/telescopic control unit (G).
  1. Remove the steering joint bolts (H), then disconnect the steering joint (I) from the pinion shaft and the steering column shaft.
  1. Remove the steering column (J) by removing the attaching nuts and bolts.
Installation
  1. Install the steering column in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Make sure the wires are not caught or pinched by any parts.
    • Take care not to let the sliding capsules fall out of position during column installation.
  1. Center the steering rack within its stroke.
  1. Install the steering joint (A) aligning the slit (B) of the steering joint with the tab (C) of the center guide. Position the angle of the center guide as shown if necessary.
  1. With the rack in the straight ahead driving position, slip the lower end of the steering joint on to the pinion shaft (D).
  1. Align the bolt hole (A) on the steering joint with the groove (B) around the pinion shaft, and loosely install the joint bolts (C). Be sure that the joint bolt is securely in the groove in the pinion shaft. Pull on the steering joint to make sure that the steering joint is fully seated. Tighten the steering joint bolt to the specified torque.
  1. Install the driver's dashboard undercover and driver's dashboard lower cover.
  1. Finish the installation, and note these items:
    • Turn the ignition switch ON (II); the SRS indicator should come on for about 6 seconds and then go off.
    • Make sure the connectors are properly connected.
    • Reinstall the steering wheel.
    • Reconnect the battery.
    • Enter the anti-theft codes (if equipped) for the radio and the navigation system, then enter the audio presets.
    • Verify horn and turn signal switch operation.
    • Verify functions of switches on the steering wheel.
    • Do the steering column position memorization.
    • Make sure the steering wheel is centered.
    • For with LKAS model: Do the steering angle sensor neutral position memorization.
