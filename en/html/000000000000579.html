Mirror Holder Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  1. Adjust the mirror fully to the inward position.
  1. Insert a trim tool between the mirror holder (A) and the mirror housing (B), and then quickly twist the tool to detach the bottom clips (C). Pad the edge of the mirror housing with a shop towel (D). Take care not to scratch the mirror housing.
  1. Carefully pull out the bottom edge of the mirror holder (A), then release the side clips (B). Separate the mirror holder from the actuator (C) by releasing the hooks (D). Disconnect the mirror defogger connectors (E) from the heater pad terminals, and disconnect the automatic dimming mirror connector (F).
  1. Reconnect the mirror defogger connector and automatic dimming mirror connector.
  1. Reattach the hooks of the mirror holder to the actuator, then position the mirror holder on the actuator. Carefully push on the clip portions of the mirror holder until the mirror holder locks into place.
  1. Check the operation of the actuator.
