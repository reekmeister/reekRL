Rear License Trim Replacement - Without Rear Fog Light
Without Rear Fog Lights
NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  1. Remove the trunk lid trim.
  1. Remove the screws from the rear license trim (A).
  1. From inside the trunk lid, remove the nuts securing the rear license trim (A).
  1. Gently close the trunk lid, and pull the rear license trim (A) out to detach the clip, then remove the trim. Take care not to scratch the trunk lid.
  1. Install the trim in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clip, and if necessary, replace it with a new one.
    • Push the clip into place securely.
