Engine Removal
Special Tools Required

Engine tilt hanger set
 07KAK-SJ40101

Engine hanger stay
 07KAK-SJ40200
NOTE:
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • To avoid damage, unplug the wiring connectors carefully while holding the connector portion.
  • Mark all wiring and hoses to avoid misconnection. Also, be sure that they do not contact other wiring, hoses, or interfere with other parts.
  1. Disconnect the support struts from both sides of the pivot ball (bolted to the hood). Secure the hood in a vertical position. Remove the right side pivot ball and install it into the lower threaded hole, then reattach the support strut.
    NOTE: Do not attempt to close the hood with the support strut in the vertical position, as it will damage the support strut and hood.
  1. Relieve the fuel pressure.
  1. Drain the power steering system fluid, then plug the fluid reservoir and return hose.
  1. Do the battery removal procedure.
  1. Remove the engine cover.
  1. Remove the right upper fender trim, the battery trim, the left upper fender trim, the upper grille cover, and the cowl cover.
  1. Remove the air cleaner.
  1. Remove the air intake duct cover.
  1. Remove the harness clamp (A) from the battery base (B).
  1. Remove the two upper bolts (C), and loosen the two lower bolts (D), then remove the battery base.
  1. Remove the harness clamps (A), the starter cable (B), and the ground cable (C).
  1. Remove the battery cable (A) from the under-hood fuse/relay box.
  1. Remove the harness clamp (B), then disconnect the harness connector (C).
  1. Remove the two bolts (D) securing the under-hood fuse/relay box, then remove the under-hood fuse/relay box.
  1. LHD model: Remove the harness clamp (A), and disconnect the engine wire harness connectors (B) on the left side of the engine compartment.
  1. LHD model: Remove the connectors (A).
  1. Remove the transfer breather hose (B), then remove the front tower bar (C).
  1. RHD model: Remove the Passenger's dashboard under cover.
  1. RHD model: Disconnect the powertrain control module (PCM) connectors (A) and main wire harness connectors (B), then remove the harness clamps (C).
  1. RHD model: Remove the harness clamp (A) and grommet (B), then pull the engine wire harness through the bulkhead.
  1. Remove the quick-connect fitting cover (A), then disconnect the fuel feed hose (B).
  1. Disconnect the brake booster vacuum hose (A) and the evaporative emission (EVAP) canister hose (B).
  1. Remove the shift cable.
  1. Remove the drive belt.
  1. Remove the heat shield.
  1. Remove the radiator cap.
  1. Raise the vehicle on the lift to full height.
  1. Remove the front wheels.
  1. Remove the splash shield.
  1. Loosen the drain plug in the radiator, and drain the engine coolant.
  1. Drain the automatic transmission fluid (ATF).
  1. Drain the engine oil.
  1. Disconnect the stabilizer links.
  1. Remove the damper fork.
  1. Separate the tie-rod end ball joints from the knuckles.
  1. Separate the knuckles from the lower arms.
  1. Remove the driveshafts. Coat all precision-finished surfaces with clean engine oil. Tie plastic bags over the driveshaft ends.
  1. Remove the exhaust pipe assembly.
  1. Remove the propeller shaft.
  1. Remove the transfer assembly.
  1. Remove the ATF cooler hoses from the transmission.
  1. Remove the power steering (P/S) line from the clamps (A), then remove the P/S line bracket mounting bolt (B).
  1. Remove the bolts securing the transmission lower mount.
  1. Lower the vehicle on the lift.
  1. Disconnect the upper radiator hose (A) and the lower radiator hose (B).
  1. Disconnect the heater hoses.
  1. Remove the radiator.
  1. Remove the four bolts securing the A/C compressor.
  1. Remove the two bolts securing the upper bracket.
  1. Remove the P/S pump (A) without disconnecting the P/S hoses from the pump, then remove the P/S hose clamp mounting bolt (B).
  1. Attach the special tools to the engine as shown.
  1. LHD model: Remove the P/S hose from the clamp (A), then remove the shift cable bracket mounting bolt (B) and P/S line bracket mounting bolt (C).
  1. RHD model: Remove the shift cable bracket mounting bolt (A) and P/S line bracket mounting bolt (B).
  1. Remove the vacuum hose (A) and front mount stop (B), then remove the front mount bolt (C).
  1. Remove the vacuum hose (A) and vacuum hose bracket mounting bolt (B).
  1. Remove the rear mount stop (C), then remove the rear mount bolt (D).
  1. Remove the rear mount (E).
  1. Remove the steering gearbox mounting bolts (A), then remove the steering gearbox stiffener (B) and damper (C).
  1. Make sure the hoist brackets are positioned properly. Raise the lift to full height.
  1. Support the front subframe with a 4 x 4 x 40 in. piece of wood and a jack.
  1. Remove the subframe.
  1. Check that the engine/transmission is completely free of vacuum hoses, fuel hoses, coolant hoses, and electrical wiring.
  1. Slowly lower the engine/transmission assembly about 150 mm (6 in.). Check once again that all hoses and electrical wiring are disconnected and free from the engine/transmission, then lower it all the way.
  1. Disconnect the chain hoist from the engine/ transmission assembly.
  1. Remove the engine/transmission assembly from under the vehicle.
