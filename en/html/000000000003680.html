Navigation System Symptom Troubleshooting - Trip computer-no distance
Trip computer-no distance
Diagnostic Test: Car Status
NOTE:
  • Check the vehicle battery condition first.
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  • Always check for and resolve all CAN DTCs before troubleshooting the navigation system.
  • Verify that the correct navigation unit is installed for this model. Go into the Diagnostic mode and use Version.
  • Make sure that the correct version DVD is installed.
  • Check any official Honda service website for more service information about the navigation system.
  1. Go into the diagnostic mode and use Car Status diagnostic to check for a vehicle speed pulse (VSP).
Is there a VSP when the vehicle is moving?
YES -
Check the CAN bus wires for an open or short to ground between navigation unit connector B (32P) No. 8 and No. 24 terminals and PCM connector A (31P) No. 15 and No. 26 terminals. If OK, replace the navigation unit.■
NO -
Check the VSP wire for an open or short to ground between navigation unit connector A (8P) terminal No. 6 and PCM connector A (31P) No. 11 terminal.■
